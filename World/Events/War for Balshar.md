# War for Balshar

The War for Balshar is the Fourth Age conflict caused by [[Razor|Razor's]] attempt to establish control of the [[Balshar Mountains]].

## Current Alignments

- **Resistance:** [[Erie]] and [[Cloud Seers]]
- **Razor's coalition:** [[Black Fang Orcs]] and nearly all mountain kobolds
- **Uncommitted:** Several other orc tribes; the Frostvein have opened talks but remain neutral

The named neutral tribes are the [[Frostvein Orcs]], [[Graven Hand Orcs]], and [[Hollow Drum Orcs]]. They are not actively at war with one another, but all three retain longstanding resentment toward the [[Erie]].

## Strategic Sites

The Erie guard ancient anti-dragon artifacts and a mine containing ore capable of strengthening dragon breath. One Erie outpost has already been destroyed by combined orc and kobold forces equipped with magical air-launch devices.

## Current Status

The Cloud Seer rider [[Zing Zow]] is checking the mine and calling for reinforcements.

A [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] column of five diplomats under [[Gorak Broken-Tusk]] and fifteen warriors aligned with [[Vekka Ash-Eye]] is traveling toward [[Icehold]] to recruit the Frostvein for Razor. The mission remains protected by a peace banner; an attack on the Frostvein has not been established in play.

Lil Chang and the rescued Cloud Seer scout [[Da Long]] reached Icehold on [[Quickfeather]]. They warned [[An Yue]] and her council, offered hunting knowledge as a concrete benefit, and received hospitality. Frostvein has not joined the resistance. Its leaders require winter support, control of their passes, limited obligations, and written postwar guarantees before committing.

The rest of the party recruited the escaped kobold [[Scritch]], passed the Black Fang camp at the [[Stone Stairs]], and fought a Black Fang plateau patrol. All but one scout were defeated. The survivor can warn the main delegation before the party completes its journey to Icehold.

The prepared threat of a manufactured avalanche at Three-Bells Basin remains unrevealed GM material and did not occur during Session 08 or Session 09.
