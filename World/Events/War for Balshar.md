# War for Balshar

The War for Balshar is the Fourth Age conflict caused by [[Razor|Razor's]] attempt to establish control of the [[Balshar Mountains]].

## Current Alignments

- **Resistance:** [[Erie]] and [[Cloud Seers]]
- **Razor's coalition:** [[Black Fang Orcs]] and nearly all mountain kobolds
- **Uncommitted:** Several other orc tribes waiting to see which side gains the advantage

The named neutral tribes are the [[Frostvein Orcs]], [[Graven Hand Orcs]], and [[Hollow Drum Orcs]]. They are not actively at war with one another, but all three retain longstanding resentment toward the [[Erie]].

## Strategic Sites

The Erie guard ancient anti-dragon artifacts and a mine containing ore capable of strengthening dragon breath. One Erie outpost has already been destroyed by combined orc and kobold forces equipped with magical air-launch devices.

## Current Status

The Cloud Seer rider [[Zing Zow]] is checking the mine and calling for reinforcements.

A [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] diplomatic entourage, protected by approximately fifteen to twenty orcs, is traveling toward the Frostvein settlement to recruit the neutral tribes for Razor. The mission is diplomatic; an attack on the Frostvein has not been established.

Lil Chang and the rescued Cloud Seer scout [[Da Long]] are racing ahead on Da Long's griffon, [[Quickfeather]], while the rest of the party follows by secret mountain paths.
