# War for Balshar

The War for Balshar is the Fourth Age conflict caused by [[Razor|Razor's]] attempt to establish control of the [[Balshar Mountains]].

## Current Alignments

- **Resistance:** [[Erie]] and [[Cloud Seers]]
- **Razor's coalition:** [[Black Fang Orcs]] and nearly all mountain kobolds
- **Uncommitted:** Several other orc tribes waiting to see which side gains the advantage

## Strategic Sites

The Erie guard ancient anti-dragon artifacts and a mine containing ore capable of strengthening dragon breath. One Erie outpost has already been destroyed by combined orc and kobold forces equipped with magical air-launch devices.

## Current Status

The Cloud Seer rider [[Zing Zow]] is checking the mine and calling for reinforcements. A mixed adventuring party led by connections to both the Erie and mountain orcs has chosen to recruit the uncommitted tribes rather than confront Razor directly.
