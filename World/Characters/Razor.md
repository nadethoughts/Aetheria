# Razor

![[img/Warren's Want/Razor.png]]

Razor is a tremendously powerful dragon who established a roost in the [[Balshar Mountains]] during the Fourth Age.

He arrived from the hill country to the southwest and began gathering a regional army. Nearly all mountain kobolds now treat him as a god, while the [[Black Fang Orcs]] serve him through a practical alliance.

## Appearance

Razor is an immense iron-black dragon with cold blue mineral iridescence across his scales, a crown of long blade-like horns, heavy cheek spines, and piercing amber eyes. His weathered scars and mountainous stature give him the bearing of an ancient conqueror rather than a common predator.

## Known Goals

- Establish permanent control over the Balshar Mountains
- Seize artifacts guarded by the [[Erie]]
- Gain access to ore capable of strengthening a dragon's breath weapon
- Eliminate or subjugate the Erie–[[Cloud Seers|Cloud Seer]] resistance

## Threat Level

Razor is a campaign-scale threat far beyond the present Nimble party's ability to defeat directly. Several companies of mixed Birdfolk and orc warriors have gone toward his roost and failed to return.

The exact kind of dragon, limits of his power, timetable, and ultimate plan remain unknown.
