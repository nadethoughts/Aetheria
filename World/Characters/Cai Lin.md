# Cai Lin

Cai Lin tends Frostvein's hearths and injured climbers. She believes Razor must be resisted before war reaches the settlement, but she expects both the Cloud Seers and Lil Chang to put Frostvein lives ahead of winning an argument.

## Character Build

- **Current Build**: Level 3 Orc Shepherd (Luminary of Mercy)
- **Background**: Tradesman / Artisan — Healer and Herbalist
- **Combat Snapshot**: HP 34, Armor 10, Speed 6, Initiative -1, Mana 9; advantage on WIL saves and disadvantage on DEX saves
- **Build Focus**: Restoring dying allies, amplifying healing through Mercy, and supporting the party with healer and herbalist expertise
- **Spell Tracking**: Lifebinding Spirit, Heal, Shadow Trap, Rebuke, True Strike, Entice, Withering Touch, Bond of Peace, and Gravecraft are tracked separately as spells or cantrips.

### Selected Non-Spell Abilities

- Merciful Healing
- Searing Light
- Life Is Beautiful
- Tradesman / Artisan — Healer and Herbalist
- Relentless

## Campaign Role

Cai Lin is prepared as a temporary PC and Frostvein negotiating voice. She resents generations of Erie condescension but fears neutrality will survive only as long as Razor permits it.
