# Zing Zow

Zing Zow is a [[Cloud Seers|Cloud Seer]] orc, griffin rider, and apparent patrol leader. He wears woven blue clothing and swirling pale and dark blue war paint and carries a battleaxe.

He is an old childhood friend of Lil Chang from Lil Chang's years in the [[Balshar Mountains]]. Their precise shared history has not yet been defined.

Zing Zow explained [[Razor|Razor's]] coalition and the regional war to the Nimble party. He and his riders volunteered to secure the nearby Erie mine and request reinforcements while the party approached the unaligned orc tribes.
