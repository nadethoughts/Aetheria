# Bao Jun

Bao Jun is the chief of the [[Graven Hand Orcs]], the religious keepers of the mountain orc burial grounds.

[[Zing Zow]] described Bao Jun as unusual and suggested that this quality reflected the Graven Hand more broadly. Bao Jun has not chosen a side in the [[War for Balshar]].
