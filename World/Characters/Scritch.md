# Scritch

Scritch is the kobold the Nimble party captured near [[Clackston]] while he tried to drag away a stolen mule. Rowan questioned and released him after the party learned the route to the Warren.

With nowhere else to go, Scritch returned to the [[Balshar Mountains]] and joined the kobolds serving [[Razor]], despite reservations about the dragon. He later traveled with a [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] diplomatic column. An injured leg slowed the group, and mistreatment convinced him to escape during a rest. He became lost in the pine forest and sheltered beneath a Black Fang blanket until the party found him again.

Rowan gave Scritch a healing draught. Scritch then joined the party, supplied intelligence about the column, and fought beside them during a seven-kobold ambush.

## Character Build

- **Current Build**: Level 3 Kobold Cheat (Tools of the Scoundrel)
- **Background**: Wily Underdog
- **Combat Snapshot**: HP 21, Armor 6, Speed 6, Initiative +2; advantage on DEX saves and disadvantage on WIL saves
- **Build Focus**: Reliable melee critical hits against distracted enemies, Sneak Attack, evasive tricks, and fast social leverage
- **Spell Tracking**: No spell selections are part of this build.

## Known Intelligence

- The Black Fang column contains five diplomats and fifteen warriors.
- [[Gorak Broken-Tusk]] leads the diplomats.
- Quartermaster [[Vekka Ash-Eye]] holds influence over the warriors.
- The column travels beneath a peace banner toward the [[Frostvein Orcs]].
- Scritch believes the mission has a hidden objective but does not know what it is.

## Capabilities

Scritch was played as a temporary **Cheat**. He is stealthy, uses a sling, rapier, and dagger, and can exploit a distracted enemy for a reliable critical strike and sneak attack.
