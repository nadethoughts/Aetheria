# Mei Rong

Mei Rong preserves Frostvein's oral histories. She joined [[An Yue|An Yue's]] council when Lil Chang and [[Da Long]] arrived at [[Icehold]] in Session 09.

She considers Razor dangerous, but she will not support a resistance that simply restores Erie rule or dismisses the real injuries Frostvein has suffered. During the hearing, she recalled earlier emergency alliances that left Frostvein beneath more powerful neighbors once the crisis ended.

## Character Build

- **Current Build**: Level 3 Orc Songweaver (Herald of Courage)
- **Background**: History Buff
- **Combat Snapshot**: HP 26, Armor 5, Speed 6, Initiative +1, Mana 6, Inspiration 4; advantage on WIL saves and disadvantage on STR saves
- **Build Focus**: Inspiration, party-wide temporary HP, recovery during rests, negotiation, and historical knowledge
- **Spell Tracking**: Vicious Mockery, Razor Wind, Ice Lance, Snowblind, Breath of Life, Blustery Gale, Frost Shield, Feather Fall, and Ice Disk are tracked separately as spells or cantrips.

### Selected Non-Spell Abilities

- Songweaver's Inspiration
- Quick Wit
- Inspiring Presence
- Jack of All Trades
- Song of Rest
- History Buff
- Relentless

## Campaign Role

Mei Rong was portrayed as a temporary PC during the Session 09 negotiation. She called for written, signed assurances that defeating Razor will not place Frostvein beneath the Erie's talon again.
