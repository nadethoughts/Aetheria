# Vekka Ash-Eye

Vekka Ash-Eye is the quartermaster associated with the fifteen warriors escorting [[Gorak Broken-Tusk|Gorak Broken-Tusk's]] five-person diplomatic mission to the [[Frostvein Orcs]]. The escaped kobold [[Scritch]] described Vekka as clever and influential over the column's military side.

The delegation travels beneath a peace banner. Scritch believes it has a hidden objective but could not identify it.

In Session 09, the land party defeated a Black Fang plateau patrol but failed to stop one escaping scout. If the survivor reaches the column, Vekka may learn that the party is ahead and that Scritch is traveling with it.

> [!warning] GM-only preparation
> Session 08 prep makes Vekka the architect of the delegation's manufactured rescue. She knows Rock Scale kobolds are weakening the snow shelf above Rimehook, carries the timing slate, coordinates two outriders, and has secretly enlarged the planned avalanche beyond Tikka's limited design. None of this had been discovered by the end of Session 09.
