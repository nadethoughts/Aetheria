# General Brand

General Brand directs the [[Erie]] war effort against [[Razor]] from the principal Erie city, described as their greatest fortress.

Brand frequently leaves the capital for weeks and follows a repeated circuit through the eastern [[Balshar Mountains]]. The purpose of those journeys has not been revealed. Possible connections to artifact sites, wards, and military coordination remain unconfirmed.
