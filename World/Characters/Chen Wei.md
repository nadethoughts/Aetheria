# Chen Wei

Chen Wei is the chief of the [[Hollow Drum Orcs]], the subterranean tribe that communicates across the mountains using drums.

[[Zing Zow]] described Chen Wei as a person of action and expressed surprise that he had not yet chosen a side in the [[War for Balshar]].
