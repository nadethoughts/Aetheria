# An Yue

An Yue is the chieftess of the [[Frostvein Orcs]]. [[Zing Zow]] described her as cool and level-headed. She governs from the great hall at [[Icehold]].

She has not chosen a side in the [[War for Balshar]]. In Session 09, Lil Chang and [[Da Long]] warned her that Razor and the Black Fang could dominate the divided mountain tribes. An Yue heard them with senior Frostvein voices [[Feng Rui]], [[Jian Bo]], and [[Mei Rong]].

An Yue did not reject the danger, but she refused to promise Frostvein blood without clear benefits, limits, and postwar guarantees. She accepted Da Long's hunting-ground information, extended hospitality to the visitors, and will confer privately with her council.

The Black Fang travel beneath a peace banner. An Yue intends to hear their public case from [[Gorak Broken-Tusk]] and asked Lil Chang and Da Long to remain for it. Her decision will turn on winter survival, Frostvein control of its passes, enforceable terms, and whether either coalition can be trusted after Razor is defeated.
