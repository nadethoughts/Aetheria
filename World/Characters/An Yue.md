# An Yue

An Yue is the chieftess of the [[Frostvein Orcs]]. [[Zing Zow]] described her as cool and level-headed.

She has not chosen a side in the [[War for Balshar]]. Lil Chang and [[Da Long]] flew ahead to seek her support, while the land party passed a [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] delegation unseen at the [[Stone Stairs]]. Neither group's audience with An Yue has yet been shown in play.

The Black Fang travel beneath a peace banner. Their public case will be presented by [[Gorak Broken-Tusk]], while fifteen accompanying warriors are aligned with [[Vekka Ash-Eye]].
