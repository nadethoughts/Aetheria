# An Yue

An Yue is the chieftess of the [[Frostvein Orcs]]. [[Zing Zow]] described her as cool and level-headed.

She has not chosen a side in the [[War for Balshar]]. Both a [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] diplomatic entourage and Lil Chang's party are traveling toward her settlement to seek Frostvein support.
