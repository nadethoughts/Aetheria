# Feng Rui

Feng Rui commands Frostvein's northern pass wardens. He begins from a position of neutrality and closed borders: he resents outsiders crossing Frostvein territory without permission and will only support an alliance that leaves the settlement in control of its own passes.

## Character Build

- **Current Build**: Level 3 Orc Commander (Champion of the Bulwark)
- **Background**: Devoted Protector — [[Cai Lin]]
- **Combat Snapshot**: HP 34, Armor 14, Speed 6, Initiative -1; advantage on STR saves and disadvantage on DEX saves
- **Build Focus**: Armored battlefield support, moving allies into position, emergency recovery, and protecting Cai Lin
- **Spell Tracking**: No spell selections are part of this build. Coordinated Strike can order an ally to use a weapon attack or cantrip, but it is a class ability rather than a spell.

### Selected Non-Spell Abilities

- Coordinated Strike!
- Reposition!
- Shield Expert
- Hold the Line!
- Field Medic
- Armor Master
- Devoted Protector — Cai Lin
- Relentless

## Campaign Role

Feng Rui is prepared as a temporary PC and Frostvein negotiating voice. He fears either coalition treating Frostvein as a road and can be won over if An Yue's authority over the passes is respected.
