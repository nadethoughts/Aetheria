# Jian Bo

Jian Bo keeps Frostvein fed, equipped, and moving. He is willing to hear the Black Fang offer before choosing a side and judges alliances by what food, metal, labor, routes, and mutual defense they will actually provide before winter.

## Character Build

- **Current Build**: Level 3 Orc Berserker (Path of the Mountainheart)
- **Background**: Fearless
- **Combat Snapshot**: HP 42, Armor 1, Speed 6, Initiative +3; advantage on STR saves and disadvantage on INT saves
- **Build Focus**: Generating Fury, converting it into offense or damage reduction, and anchoring dangerous mountain rescues through raw strength
- **Spell Tracking**: No spell selections are part of this build.

### Selected Non-Spell Abilities

- Rage
- Intensifying Fury
- Bloodlust
- That All You Got?!
- Mountainous Tenacity
- One with the Ancients
- Stone's Resilience
- Relentless
- Fearless

## Campaign Role

Jian Bo is prepared as a temporary PC and Frostvein negotiating voice. He fears a long war that empties the settlement's stores and can support the coalition if its promises include practical supply and defense commitments.
