# Talon of Unmaking

The Talon of Unmaking is a lesser [[Erie]] artifact formerly carried by Umbrail's magical mentor. Umbrail unknowingly took it among his mentor's possessions when he left the [[Balshar Mountains]].

At the ruined Erie outpost, Umbrail found the incantations needed to cleanse and reactivate the artifact. It takes the form of a talisman and does not occupy an equipment slot.

## Mechanical Effect

When Umbrail's spell damages an armored creature, he may spend 2 mana to reduce the target's armor by one step until the start of its next turn.

Against a dragon, the reduction lasts until the end of the dragon's next turn.
