# Talon of Unmaking

> [!warning] GM-facing master entry
> The party knows the history and power described in **What the Party Knows**. Later awakenings, their conditions, and the Talon's complete purpose should be discovered through play.

## Artifact Summary

The Talon of Unmaking is an unlockable Erie artifact made during the ancient war against the dragon flights. It does not kill through overwhelming force. It finds the hidden assumptions that make a creature appear invulnerable—joined scales, armored plates, magical wards, effortless flight—and briefly proves those assumptions false.

It is a **lesser Erie artifact** only in the sense that it was not intended to win a war alone. Talons were carried by severance mages who created openings for an entire flight of warriors. A greater relic could kill a dragon. A Talon made the dragon mortal enough for others to try.

That cooperative purpose makes it a natural counterpoint to [[Campaigns/Nimble/Items/Shadow Axe|Kharum Vhar]]. The axe tells Rubius that strength gives him the right to decide alone. The Talon teaches Umbrail that knowledge matters most when it creates an opportunity others can share.

## What the Party Knows

The Talon formerly belonged to Umbrail's magical mentor. Umbrail took it among the mentor's possessions when he left the [[Balshar Mountains]], without recognizing it as an Erie relic.

At the ruined Erie outpost, Umbrail searched his mentor's surviving books and found the incantations necessary to cleanse and reactivate it. What had appeared to be an inert talisman unfolded into fine metal segments shaped like a single hooked claw.

The Talon:

- Is a carried talisman and occupies no equipment slot.
- Reacts to magical analysis and damaging spells.
- Can peel protection from an armored creature.
- Holds its opening longer against dragons.
- Is important, but is not one of the three great Erie artifacts.
- May cause conflict when the Erie learn that Umbrail carried it away.

Umbrail has already unlocked its first state.

## Appearance

When dormant, the Talon resembles a thumb-sized knot of dull silver bands surrounding a cloudy blue stone. Once cleansed, the bands unfold into one articulated claw. Fine inscriptions appear only while Umbrail is casting: diagrams of wings, scale patterns, air currents, and narrow lines showing where one protected surface meets another.

Each awakening unfolds another metal digit:

1. **One claw:** Find the seam.
2. **Two claws:** Hold the opening.
3. **Three claws:** Bring the mighty down.
4. **The open hand:** Make the impossible mortal.

At its final state, the talisman resembles a small open raptor's foot grasping empty air. When activated, a translucent version of that talon closes around the target's defenses rather than its body.

## True History

### The Severance Flights

During the ancient Erie-dragon war, the Erie learned that their greatest problem was not harming dragons. It was creating a moment in which harm could matter. Dragon scales turned weapons, their wings controlled distance, their breath scattered formations, and their ancient magic answered threats before those threats became dangerous.

The Erie responded by forming small units called **Severance Flights**. Each centered on a mage carrying a Talon of Unmaking. The mage did not deliver the killing blow. They identified one certainty on which the dragon relied and removed it for a heartbeat. The rest of the flight had to recognize that heartbeat and act together.

This doctrine produced an Erie maxim:

> The Talon does not make victory. It makes victory possible.

Many Talons were lost with their bearers. A few became family or teaching-line relics entrusted to mages whose judgment mattered more than their raw power.

### Umbrail's Mentor

Umbrail's mentor was the latest recognized bearer of this Talon. The relic had not fully opened for the mentor either; it was waiting for a student capable of completing a severance rite the old Erie had forgotten.

The strongest version of the truth is not that the mentor secretly gave Umbrail permission to take it. Umbrail really did leave with something that was not his. The mentor discovered the loss, understood who had taken it, and chose not to hunt Umbrail down. Instead, the mentor left the cleansing incantation among materials Umbrail would recognize if he ever returned.

That was neither forgiveness nor a trap. It was a final lesson: **returning is not the same as taking responsibility, but it creates the opportunity to begin.**

This truth can be established through the mentor's field journal, an Erie witness, a magical memory held by the Talon, or testimony from [[General Brand]].

## Unlock Structure

The Talon does not awaken at predetermined character levels. It responds when Umbrail demonstrates one of the principles for which the Severance Flights were created.

| State | Story Principle | Suggested Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Talon | Knowledge reveals weakness | Already unlocked through research and cleansing |
| Second Talon | Responsibility must precede authority | Confess the taking and willingly risk surrendering the relic |
| Third Talon | An opening belongs to everyone | Use Erie power to protect or empower a non-Erie people without demanding submission |
| Open Hand | No one brings down a dragon alone | Recover the final severance rite and enter battle with a genuine coalition |

These conditions are intentionally narrative. If the campaign develops differently, preserve the principle and change the specific scene.

## State I — The First Talon

**Current state. Already unlocked.**

### Find the Seam

When Umbrail's spell damages an armored creature, he may spend **2 mana** to reduce the target's Armor by one step until the start of its next turn.

Against a dragon, the reduction lasts until the end of the dragon's next turn.

This effect does not stack with itself. A new use refreshes the duration.

### Signs of Awakening

- One metal claw unfolds around the central stone.
- Umbrail briefly sees armor as overlapping translucent planes.
- The Talon tightens when a creature is protected by something that can be analyzed or dismantled.

### Intended Play

This is the artifact's established Session 07 ability. It asks whether 2 mana now is worth improving the entire party's damage window. It should remain useful even after later states unlock.

## Unlock II — The Second Talon

### Story Condition: The Weight of Taking

The second claw unfolds when Umbrail tells an Erie authority or community exactly how he came to possess the Talon and willingly offers them the power to reclaim it.

The unlock does not require the Erie to forgive him. It requires Umbrail to stop protecting himself with ambiguity. The best scene allows a rightful claimant to take the Talon, examine it, and return it because the relic has chosen an active severance mage—or because the Erie need Umbrail more than they need punishment.

If Umbrail refuses to surrender it but accepts a serious public consequence, that can also satisfy the principle.

### Hold the Opening

Whenever Umbrail uses **Find the Seam**, choose one ally who can see or hear him. That ally gains advantage on the next attack it makes against the target before the unmaking ends.

If that attack damages the target, extend the Armor reduction until the end of the target's following turn. This extension can occur only once per activation.

### Returning Current

The first time each encounter that the chosen ally damages the unmade target, Umbrail may move **2 spaces** for free. If he can fly, this movement may be flight.

### Why It Matters

The Talon begins behaving like a Severance Flight relic rather than a private implement. Umbrail creates the opening, another character commits to it, and their success lets him reposition for the next problem.

## Unlock III — The Third Talon

### Story Condition: The Sky Is Shared

The third claw unfolds when Umbrail uses the Talon, Erie knowledge, or his standing among the Erie to preserve the freedom of a non-Erie mountain people without requiring them to accept Erie rule.

Possible unlock scenes include:

- Preventing the manufactured disaster at Three-Bells Basin and publicly crediting Frostvein witnesses.
- Saving a neutral orc settlement from Razor while respecting its decision to remain politically independent.
- Giving a kobold, orc, or Cloud Seer the opening needed to defeat a threat the Erie could not defeat alone.
- Refusing General Brand if Brand demands obedience where the coalition requires partnership.

### Clip the Wing

**1/encounter, Reaction:** When a creature affected by **Find the Seam** flies, teleports, or moves more than 2 spaces, Umbrail may spend **2 mana** to seize the opening.

The creature makes a **STR save against DC 10 + Umbrail's INT**.

- **Failure:** Its movement ends. If airborne, it descends safely to the nearest space that can support it and becomes **Prone**.
- **Success:** Its movement is halved, and it cannot fly or teleport again until the end of its turn.

Dragons make this save normally even if their size or traits would usually make forced movement irrelevant. Legendary resistance can protect them if such a mechanic exists, but doing so visibly expends that defense.

### Unmake the Ward

**1/Safe Rest:** When Umbrail damages a creature protected by a magical barrier, resistance, immunity, or defensive stance, he may spend **3 mana** and name the protection he is trying to understand.

The GM reveals whether that protection can be unmade and one concrete requirement for doing so. If the requirement is already satisfied, suppress that protection until the end of the creature's next turn.

Examples of requirements include:

- Strike the same location with two different damage types.
- Ground the creature.
- Break its concentration.
- Destroy or separate a focus.
- Have three different characters damage it in one round.

This is not an automatic answer to every immunity. It turns a hidden defense into a playable objective.

## Unlock IV — The Open Hand

### Story Condition: The Last Severance Rite

The final digit unfolds only after Umbrail recovers the missing Severance Rite and enters the confrontation with Razor as part of a real coalition.

The rite can be recovered from one or more of the following:

- The vault beneath the ancient battlefield.
- The resting place of the Erie general who defeated the old dragon flights.
- The mentor's final memory, preserved inside the Talon.
- A surviving member or descendant of a Severance Flight.
- General Brand's unexplained circuit through the eastern mountains.

Merely collecting the words is insufficient. Before the Talon opens, Umbrail must assign parts of the rite to allies from at least **three different peoples or factions**. He must trust them to act without his control.

### Make the Impossible Mortal

**1/Safe Rest, 2 Actions, 5 mana:** Choose a dragon or Legendary creature within **12 spaces** that Umbrail can see. The Open Hand closes around one certainty on which the target relies. Choose **two** severances below:

- **Sever Scale:** Its Armor becomes **0** and cannot be increased.
- **Sever Wing:** It cannot fly or teleport, and its Speed is halved.
- **Sever Breath:** It cannot use breath weapons, cones, lines, or other large innate area attacks.
- **Sever Crown:** It cannot use Reactions or command other creatures to move or attack.
- **Sever Ward:** Suppress one magical resistance, immunity, transformation, or defensive trait identified through **Unmake the Ward**.

The severances last until the end of the target's next turn. Against a dragon, they instead last until the end of its following turn.

Afterward, the Talon folds completely shut. Umbrail cannot use any Talon abilities until he completes a Safe Rest.

### The Coalition's Opening

When **Make the Impossible Mortal** is used, choose up to three allies who participated in the final Severance Rite. Each may immediately move up to half Speed for free. This movement must bring them closer to the target, a threatened ally, or an objective involved in the battle.

The artifact creates one extraordinary coordinated window. It does not supply the victory that must fill it.

## Optional Final Evolution — The Mended Talon

If the campaign continues after Razor, the Talon can change rather than simply becoming a stronger anti-dragon tool.

When Umbrail returns it voluntarily after the war, the Erie may formally entrust it to him as the first relic of a new, mixed Severance Flight. Its name becomes the **Talon of Open Skies**.

Once per Safe Rest, when an ally fails a save against an effect that would restrain, dominate, frighten, or forcibly move them, Umbrail can use a Reaction and spend 2 mana to let that ally reroll the save. On success, both may move 2 spaces for free.

The artifact's final lesson becomes freedom rather than vulnerability: what once opened dragons for destruction now opens cages, compulsions, and inherited political bonds.

## Artifact Voice

The Talon is not a conversational intelligence like Kharum Vhar. It communicates through diagrams, memories of wind, and momentary certainty about how things fit together.

As it awakens, Umbrail experiences fragments of former Severance Flights:

- A dozen wingbeats aligning without a spoken order.
- A dragon scale parting along a line too fine to see.
- A mage choosing not to take the killing shot because another warrior is better placed.
- The terror of falling beside companions who trusted the opening would hold.
- His mentor closing a ledger after discovering the Talon was gone.

The Talon never tells Umbrail that he is worthy. It shows him what other people could do if he gives them a chance.

## Failure, Delay, and Alternate Paths

- **If the Erie confiscate it:** The Talon remains dormant for anyone who takes it through authority alone. It reawakens if Umbrail accepts accountability and is entrusted with a specific task, or if a new bearer genuinely fulfills the relevant principle.
- **If Umbrail conceals the theft:** The Second Talon cannot open, but later scenes can still reveal the truth and create another opportunity.
- **If the party skips an unlock site:** Move the needed rite or memory to another site already important to the campaign. Do not require a single missed clue.
- **If Umbrail dies or leaves:** Another character can inherit the Talon, but its unlock principles remain. Its mechanics should be adjusted to trigger from that character's primary abilities.
- **If Razor gains it:** The Talon cannot unmake itself, but Razor can study its inscriptions to anticipate Erie tactics. Recovering it becomes a coalition objective rather than an automatic campaign loss.

## GM Guidance

- Telegraph an unlock one scene before it occurs: a second segment loosens, a new diagram appears, or the central stone shows a memory.
- State the unlock principle through imagery and consequences, not a checklist delivered by an NPC.
- Let political choices matter. The Talon should open because Umbrail changes his relationship to responsibility and community.
- Preserve the current 2-mana ability exactly until the Second Talon opens.
- Use **Unmake the Ward** to turn opaque boss immunity into a solvable encounter mechanic.
- Let final severances work on Razor. A campaign artifact loses its meaning if the main villain is simply immune to it.
- Keep the opening brief. The artifact should make coordinated action thrilling, not replace the party's strategy.

## Reveal Plan

1. **Frostvein:** A historian or ritualist recognizes the talisman as something carried by an Erie formation, not a lone hero.
2. **Erie capital:** Umbrail's possession becomes public. Confession or judgment unlocks the Second Talon.
3. **Coalition victory:** A non-Erie ally acts through an opening Umbrail creates, awakening the Third Talon.
4. **Ancient battlefield or General Brand's circuit:** The party learns the complete Severance Flight doctrine and recovers the final rite.
5. **Razor confrontation:** The Open Hand gives the coalition the brief mortal window it needs.
6. **After the war:** Umbrail decides whether the artifact returns to the Erie, remains his, or becomes the first relic entrusted to a shared mountain order.
