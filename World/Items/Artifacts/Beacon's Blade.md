# Beacon's Blade

Beacon's Blade is the relic of the bard and diplomat of the ancient [[The Veilcant Guard|Veilcant Guard]]. It resonates with the [[Llano]], the Song of Creation.

The blade was sealed within the [[Sunken Temple of Cadens]] with the vow that truth should wait until the world was ready. In the Fourth Age, Alwor Thistlegift remembered that sacrifice, reversed the vow, and reclaimed the weapon from a crumbling stone column.
