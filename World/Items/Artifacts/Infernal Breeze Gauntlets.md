# Infernal Breeze Gauntlets

The Infernal Breeze Gauntlets are wind-marked relics once used by the general of the ancient [[The Veilcant Guard|Veilcant Guard]]. Their bearer remembered using them to break armies before sealing them away in the name of peace.

Karsten Kendrick reclaimed the gauntlets from a statue in the [[Sunken Temple of Cadens]] after promising to use them for unity rather than conquest.
