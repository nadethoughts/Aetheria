# Heart of the Flame

The Heart of the Flame is an orichalcum circlet and relic of the warrior-king among the ancient [[The Veilcant Guard|Veilcant Guard]].

It was sealed in molten brass beneath the [[Sunken Temple of Cadens]] when its bearer renounced kingship for duty. Cahir Emersyn reclaimed it in the Fourth Age after declaring that it was time for the kings to rise again.
