# Mirror of Ashes

The Mirror of Ashes is an obsidian mirror framed in orichalcum and associated with memory, hidden truth, and the ancient [[The Veilcant Guard|Veilcant Guard]].

Its original bearer entrusted it with the truth of who the Guard had been and what the world needed to forget. Simear reclaimed it from a mirrored water basin in the [[Sunken Temple of Cadens]].
