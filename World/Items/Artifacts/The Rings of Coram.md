## The Rings of Coram
**Location:** Ironhold frontier, encountered on the route from [[Sentinel’s Rise]] toward [[Ironhold]]  
**Type:** Ancient Dwarven Ritual Site

---
### 🧑‍🎓 Historical Background
The Rings of Coram are a series of ancient stone circles, carved and consecrated by dwarven priests during the Second Age. Originally built as a place of communion with the elemental planes, the site was used for rituals of protection, binding, and the forging of alliances with elemental spirits. Over centuries, the Rings fell into disuse and legend, their true purpose lost to all but the most learned loremasters.

---
### 🏰 Role in The Cracked Granite
- **Elemental Convergence:** During the events of The Cracked Granite, the Rings became a focal point for the giants’ resurgence. A cave giant used the site to transform weakened trolls into forest, frost, cavern, and other elementally empowered forms.
- **Cave Giant Ritual Leader:** The party infiltrated the Rings and witnessed an unnamed cave giant overseeing the transformation process. This giant acted as the ritual leader and enforcer, commanding the warband and directing the elemental conversion of captives.
- **Battle and Discovery:** Rurik Embercloak, Hurgan Ironmark, and Demlor Ironmark confronted the cave giant and four trolls, disrupted the ritual, and recorded the aftermath as proof that giants had returned.
- **Lore and Clues:** The working Rings demonstrated that the giants understood ancient dwarven elemental infrastructure better than modern Ironhold did. The recording became central evidence during the exposure of [[Varric Silvervein]].

---
### 🔥 Magical Properties
- **Elemental Nexus:** The Rings act as a conduit for elemental energies, allowing rituals that summon, bind, or transform creatures using the power of fire, earth, and other primal forces.
- **Protective Wards:** When properly activated, the Rings can shield an area from extraplanar intrusion or elemental corruption. However, in the campaign, these wards had been corrupted or bypassed by the giants.
- **Hidden Lore:** Ancient inscriptions within the Rings can be deciphered to reveal lost dwarven history, elemental secrets, or warnings about the dangers of unchecked magic.

---
### 🧩 Adventure Hooks
- The party may return to the Rings to reclaim or purify the site, restoring its protective magic for Ironhold.
- The Rings could serve as a battleground for future conflicts, or as a source of powerful relics and knowledge.
- Rival factions (Radicals, cults, or even Isolationists) may seek to control or destroy the Rings to further their own agendas.

---
**Significance:**  
The Rings of Coram are now remembered as the place where the elemental threat was revealed and confronted—a symbol of both dwarven heritage and the dangers of forgotten magic.
