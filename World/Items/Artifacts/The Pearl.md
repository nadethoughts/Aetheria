# The Pearl

The Pearl is a weather-controlling artifact connected to [[Miraine]] and hidden beneath the [[Temple of Miraine]] in Tidewatch Ward.

It is nearly the size of a human head. Evidence gathered in the ward suggests that it once protected or regulated the weather around Penmaris and may have contributed to the island's survival during the [[Flaming Retribution]].

The [[Tidecutters]] attempted to steal it before Tidewatch fell. The Pearl is now surrounded by Pearlbound dead, Coralite, and a guardian known as the Knight of Miraine.

At the end of Session 07 of The Anchor's Secret, the Knight had been defeated and the Pearl remained on the drained chamber floor while Tidecutter pirates approached through the Gloamways.
