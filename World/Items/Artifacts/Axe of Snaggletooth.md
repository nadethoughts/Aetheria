# Axe of Snaggletooth

The Axe of Snaggletooth is an intelligent shadow battleaxe recovered from the Second Age barracks south of [[Clackston]]. It unnaturally enlarged the kobold Snaggletooth and filled its blade with light-drinking shadow.

After Snaggletooth's death, the axe became dormant. When Rubius Haggard touched it, it resized for his Half-Giant frame, filled him with peace and safety, and said:

> Welcome, friend. Let's drink the light together.

## Known or Suspected Properties

- More damaging than an ordinary battleaxe
- Intelligent and capable of communicating privately with its bearer
- Emotionally comforting to Rubius
- Grows more dangerous or powerful through continued use
- Resembles a cursed weapon said to corrupt its bearer toward a dark power
- Apparently returned to Rubius after being thrown over a mountain cliff

The only known lead for removing its power is the [[Shadow Well]] in the [[Undershadow]], though the party has not yet reached either place.
