# Axe of Snaggletooth

> [!info] Related GM entry
> The complete lore, corruption track, giant connection, and cleansing outcomes are maintained in [[Campaigns/Nimble/Items/Shadow Axe|Shadow Axe]]. This page records only campaign-revealed information.

The Axe of Snaggletooth is an intelligent shadow battleaxe recovered from the Second Age barracks south of [[Clackston]]. It unnaturally enlarged the kobold Snaggletooth and filled its blade with light-drinking shadow.

After Snaggletooth's death, the axe became dormant. When Rubius Haggard touched it, it resized for his Half-Giant frame, filled him with peace and safety, and said:

> Welcome, friend. Let's drink the light together.

## Known or Suspected Properties

- More damaging than an ordinary battleaxe; its played weapon damage is 3d4
- Intelligent and capable of communicating privately with its bearer
- Emotionally comforting to Rubius
- Grows more dangerous or powerful through continued use
- Progressively corrupts its bonded bearer and can make its impulses feel reasonable
- Can turn Rubius's rage indiscriminate and override his choice of target
- Consumes the souls or essence of creatures slain by it, reducing their bodies to black matter that is drawn into the blade
- Consumed and was empowered by one of Alder's shadow minions
- Returned to Rubius roughly thirty minutes after being thrown over a mountain cliff
- Resists ordinary separation from its bearer

## Session 08 Manifestation

The axe visibly asserted itself twice before the party reached Frostvein territory. It first drove Rubius to move a beehive against the colony's wishes after he had decided to leave it alone. During a later kobold ambush, it made his rage indiscriminate, consumed three slain kobolds, blackened his eyes, and turned him against Alder.

Umbrail's Tempest Command helped Rubius regain control. Repeated Dispel Magic later suppressed another forced rage after the axe consumed a shadow minion, but neither spell removed the curse. Rubius ended the session with **three tracked Shadow Points**.

The only known lead for removing its power is the [[Shadow Well]] in the [[Undershadow]], though the party has not yet reached either place.
