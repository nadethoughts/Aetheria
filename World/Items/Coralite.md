# Coralite

Coralite is a magical coral found within the sealed Tidewatch Ward of [[New Penmaris]]. It appears in monster growths, the bodies of the Coralmarked and Pearlbound, and most dramatically in the complete sphere surrounding the [[Temple of Miraine]].

The material is valuable for crafting and city defense. Lil John entered Tidewatch in part to identify its source and potential uses.

Coralite responds to the ward's magical forces and may serve as armor, containment, or a conduit for the power of [[The Pearl]]. Its long-term effects on living creatures remain dangerous and incompletely understood.
