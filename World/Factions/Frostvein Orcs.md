# Frostvein Orcs

The Frostvein are a neutral orc tribe of the eastern [[Balshar Mountains]]. They live among the high, cold mountain peaks. Their settlement is the closest neutral village to the northern ford across the gorge.

Their chieftess is [[An Yue]], who is known for her cool demeanor and level-headedness.

They have not chosen between [[Erie|the Erie]] and [[Razor|Razor]]. A [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] delegation of five diplomats and fifteen warriors is traveling beneath a peace banner to recruit them for Razor. Lil Chang and the Cloud Seer scout [[Da Long]] flew ahead on Da Long's griffon, [[Quickfeather]], to present the opposing case.

Like the other eastern tribes, the Frostvein remember their people's old conflict with the Erie. Their immediate needs and terms for an alliance remain unknown.

The rest of the Nimble party passed the Black Fang column at the [[Stone Stairs]] and reached the Frostvein plateau during a severe snowstorm. Neither delegation's audience with An Yue has yet been established in play.
