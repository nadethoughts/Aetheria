# Frostvein Orcs

The Frostvein are a neutral orc tribe of the eastern [[Balshar Mountains]]. They live among the high, cold mountain peaks. Their principal settlement, [[Icehold]], is the closest neutral community to the northern ford across the gorge.

Their chieftess is [[An Yue]], who is known for her cool demeanor and level-headedness.

They have not chosen between [[Erie|the Erie]] and [[Razor|Razor]]. A [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] delegation of five diplomats and fifteen warriors is traveling beneath a peace banner to recruit them for Razor. Lil Chang and the Cloud Seer scout [[Da Long]] reached Icehold first on [[Quickfeather]] and presented the opposing case to An Yue's council.

Like the other eastern tribes, the Frostvein remember their people's old conflict with the Erie. Their Session 09 council identified the core terms of any resistance agreement:

- Frostvein retains sovereignty and control over its passes.
- Troops, supplies, and foreign access must be specifically limited rather than open-ended.
- Allies must provide practical winter support.
- Postwar guarantees must be written and signed so victory over Razor does not restore Erie domination.
- Route intelligence, training, territorial defense, and limited volunteers may be more sustainable than a large troop levy.

Da Long gave the Frostvein the location of overlooked hunting grounds as proof of good faith. An Yue offered hospitality but did not choose a side. She intends to hear the Black Fang delegation under its peace banner and asked Lil Chang and Da Long to be present.

The rest of the Nimble party passed the Black Fang column at the [[Stone Stairs]], then defeated a Black Fang plateau patrol. One scout escaped and may reach the delegation before the party reaches Icehold.
