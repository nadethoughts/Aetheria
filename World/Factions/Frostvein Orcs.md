# Frostvein Orcs

The Frostvein are a neutral orc tribe of the eastern [[Balshar Mountains]]. They live among the high, cold mountain peaks. Their settlement is the closest neutral village to the northern ford across the gorge.

Their chieftess is [[An Yue]], who is known for her cool demeanor and level-headedness.

They have not chosen between [[Erie|the Erie]] and [[Razor|Razor]]. A [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] diplomatic entourage protected by approximately fifteen to twenty orcs is traveling to recruit them for Razor. Lil Chang and the Cloud Seer scout [[Da Long]] are racing ahead on Da Long's griffon, [[Quickfeather]], to present the opposing case.

Like the other eastern tribes, the Frostvein remember their people's old conflict with the Erie. Their immediate needs and terms for an alliance remain unknown.
