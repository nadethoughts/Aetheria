# Cloud Seers

The Cloud Seers are an orc tribe of the [[Balshar Mountains]] known for blue woven clothing, swirling blue war paint, trained griffin riders, high-pass guides, and storm-hunters.

They are allied with the [[Erie]] against [[Razor]]. The alliance is based on a shared need to prevent Razor from conquering the range and seizing Erie artifacts and strategic resources.

## Known Members

- [[Zing Zow]] — Griffin rider and childhood friend of Lil Chang

## Unrevealed GM Preparation

- **Kora Skybreak** — May recognize Lil Chang and call him by an old Cloud Seer name or nickname
- **Jun Ashstep** — Optional Cloud Seer scout or storm-hunter
- **Elder Mauk** — May question whether the clan should trust "the road that took Lil Chang away"

## Current Activity

Zing Zow and approximately six riders answered an Erie call for aid but arrived after an outpost had been destroyed. They later volunteered to secure the nearby Erie mine and request reinforcements while the Nimble party sought support from neutral orc tribes.
