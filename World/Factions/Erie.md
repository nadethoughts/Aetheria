# Erie

The Erie are a declining Birdfolk people of the [[Balshar Mountains]]. Their outposts and settlements are built on rock pillars, treetops, and high crests that normally require flight to reach. They once held much greater power in the range but now struggle with a shrinking population and the departure of younger generations.

## Role in the Fourth Age

The Erie guard artifacts, mines, battlefields, and other sites connected to an ancient war against dragons. Their most important known assets include:

- A principal artifact in the Erie capital
- An artifact in a vault beneath the ancient dragon-war battlefield
- An artifact on an eastern peak associated with the Erie general who defeated the dragons
- Lesser artifacts entrusted to important individual bearers
- A mine containing ore that can empower draconic breath weapons

The Erie are allied with the [[Cloud Seers]] against [[Razor]]. [[General Brand]] directs their war effort.

## Nimble Campaign

Umbrail is an Erie mage who left the mountains years ago. During the party's return, he found his former mentor dead at a destroyed outpost and learned that he may unknowingly carry one of the lesser Erie artifacts.
