# Graven Hand Orcs

The Graven Hand are a neutral orc tribe in the eastern [[Balshar Mountains]]. They are a religious people who oversee the burial grounds of the mountain orc tribes.

Their chief is [[Bao Jun]]. [[Zing Zow]] described both Bao Jun and the Graven Hand as unusual.

They have not chosen between [[Erie|the Erie]] and [[Razor|Razor]]. Like the other eastern tribes, they retain longstanding resentment toward the Erie. Their immediate needs and terms for an alliance remain unknown.
