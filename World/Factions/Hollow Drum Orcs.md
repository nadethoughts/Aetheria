# Hollow Drum Orcs

The Hollow Drum are a neutral, subterranean orc tribe in the eastern [[Balshar Mountains]]. They live in caverns and use drums for long-distance communication through the mountains.

Their chief is [[Chen Wei]], whom [[Zing Zow]] described as a person of action. Zing Zow was surprised that Chen Wei had not already chosen a side in the war.

They have not chosen between [[Erie|the Erie]] and [[Razor|Razor]]. Like the other eastern tribes, they retain longstanding resentment toward the Erie. Their immediate needs and terms for an alliance remain unknown.
