# Broadhurst

Broadhurst is a town near the Silverwood and the current Fourth Age stopping place of the reincarnated Veilcant Guard.

The party woke at the Featherbed Inn after a shared dream of their former incarnations defeating the hag Velkra. A local blacksmith named Ashley asked them to rescue her daughter Violet after the Dawnguard refused to help.

The goblins took Violet to the nearby [[Tomb of Delius]].
