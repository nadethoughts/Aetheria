# Icehold

Icehold is the principal settlement and fortress of the [[Frostvein Orcs]] in the eastern [[Balshar Mountains]]. It lies several hours beyond the outer stone watchtowers that protect the Frostvein plateau.

The settlement is substantial but austere. Buildings, tools, and stores are arranged for practical use in a climate where wasted labor or material can become fatal. Frostvein scouts guard the approaches from stone towers, and an ice labyrinth is visible from the air on the route toward the settlement.

At Icehold's center stands a great hall used for tribal government. [[An Yue]] receives visitors from a large chair made of tusks and bone and covered with carefully arranged furs.

In Session 09, [[Feng Rui]] escorted Lil Chang and [[Da Long]] to Icehold. They opened coalition negotiations before An Yue, [[Jian Bo]], and [[Mei Rong]]. The visitors and [[Quickfeather]] received hospitality while the council waits for the rest of the Nimble party and a peace-bannered [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] delegation.

## Current Situation

- Frostvein has not joined either side in the [[War for Balshar]].
- Lil Chang is waiting for his companions at Icehold.
- Da Long plans to seek a formally authorized delegation after one night's rest.
- An Yue intends to hear the Black Fang case before deciding.
- Winter stores, control of the passes, and postwar sovereignty are the council's central concerns.
