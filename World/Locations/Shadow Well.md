# Shadow Well

The Shadow Well is a magical site somewhere within the [[Undershadow]]. A historical account studied by Umbrail claims that immersing a cursed intelligent shadow axe in the well can remove its power.

The Nimble party suspects this may be the only way to cleanse or neutralize the [[Axe of Snaggletooth]]. The need became urgent after the axe consumed three kobolds, overrode Rubius's judgment, and drove him to attack Alder during Session 08.

Alder has told the party that his lost family heirloom also lies in the Undershadow and that he can sense a general route toward it. The group still does not know the Shadow Well's exact route, ritual, price, or consequences.
