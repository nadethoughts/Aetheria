# Far Grand

Far Grand is a large island or newly formed landmass northwest of [[Ironhold]]. It appeared when the [[Flaming Retribution]] reshaped coastlines and merged or raised smaller islands.

Fourth Age scouts discovered giant forces on Far Grand and a larger army attempting to cross the mountains toward Ironhold. After the conspiracy of [[Varric Silvervein]] was exposed, the giants abandoned secrecy and began open war.

Hurgan Ironmark later became an important scout and infiltrator in Far Grand, recovering information and battle plans from behind giant lines.
