# Gloamways

The Gloamways are hidden passages beneath Tidewatch Ward in [[New Penmaris]]. A concealed stair behind the shield-and-wave symbol in the [[Temple of Miraine]] descends into them.

The passages lead to the chamber containing [[The Pearl]]. Mistral's Tidecutter map included instructions for opening the temple route, and Tidecutter pirates followed the reclaimers through the Gloamways after the party reached the artifact first.
