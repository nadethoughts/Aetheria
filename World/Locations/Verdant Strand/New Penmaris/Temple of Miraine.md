# Temple of Miraine

The Temple of Miraine stands at the center of Tidewatch Ward in [[New Penmaris]]. After the ward's fall, a complete sphere of glowing [[Coralite]] formed around the temple and dozens of [[Pearlbound]] gathered outside it.

The temple's grand hall contains a boat-keel altar and Miraine's shield-and-wave symbol. A mechanism behind the symbol opens stairs into the Gloamways.

## Chamber of the Pearl

Deep below the temple is a flooded chamber containing [[The Pearl]]. Four elemental levers control the water level and the regeneration of the Knight of Miraine. During the reclaimers' battle, opening the levers drained the chamber and weakened the guardian enough for Skirk to destroy it.

The Pearlbound parted peacefully for Xander Floodbringer as he approached the temple, and the Knight hesitated when attacking him, suggesting a special connection to Miraine or the artifact.
