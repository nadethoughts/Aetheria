# Tomb of Delius

The Tomb of Delius is an ancient, reputedly haunted burial site in the Silverwood approximately an hour northeast of [[Broadhurst]].

Goblins occupied the entrance and main chamber after kidnapping Violet, daughter of the blacksmith Ashley. Deeper exploration revealed a shadow-sorcery staff, Violet's lunch, a trapped fresco corridor, twelve sarcophagi, a glowing red skull, and a warrior statue inscribed:

> Once this fails, the battle is lost.

At the current campaign stopping point, zombies, skeletons, and ghouls have erupted from the sarcophagi while sinister chanting continues behind the northern wall.
