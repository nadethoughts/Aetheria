# Balshar Mountains

The Balshar Mountains are a vast highland range of sheer passes, snowbound peaks, caves, hidden valleys, and isolated settlements. A normal mountain hex can require several days of difficult travel. The range is home to the declining Birdfolk people known as the [[Erie]], numerous orc tribes, mountain kobolds, griffins, and much larger aerial predators.

## Fourth Age Conflict

The dragon [[Razor]] established a roost in the range after arriving from southwestern hill country. Nearly all local kobolds now worship him, and the [[Black Fang Orcs]] have joined his coalition. The [[Erie]] and [[Cloud Seers]] resist him, while other orc tribes remain uncommitted.

The Erie protect ancient sites and artifacts connected to an earlier dragon war. A nearby mine contains ore capable of strengthening a dragon's breath weapon. One Erie outpost was destroyed by combined orc and kobold forces using magical air-launch devices to overcome its elevated defenses.

## Campaign Revelations

- Black-scaled kobolds traveled from the range to raid [[Clackston]].
- The mountains contain kobold excavation sites and desecrated Erie shrines.
- A huge unidentified flying creature has left enormous claw marks on high ledges.
- [[General Brand]] directs the Erie war effort from their principal city.
- The Nimble party's current objective is to recruit neutral orc tribes against Razor.
