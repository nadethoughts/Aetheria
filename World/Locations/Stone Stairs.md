# Stone Stairs

The Stone Stairs are a long series of road-like switchbacks climbing from pine forest into the high territory of the [[Frostvein Orcs]]. Seen from a distance, the repeated turns resemble steps cut into the mountain.

The route is broad and shallow enough to serve as a main Frostvein thoroughfare. Brush along the switchbacks provides some concealment, while the cliffs between them can be climbed with ropes at greater risk.

During Nimble Session 08, the [[Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] diplomatic column camped partway up the Stone Stairs. Umbrail secured Rubius's rope between ledges, allowing the party and [[Scritch]] to climb above the camp without being detected. The group then continued to a plateau of heavy wind, snow, ice, and limited visibility.

The secrecy did not last. A Black Fang patrol found the party on the plateau in Session 09. One scout escaped the ensuing battle and may carry news back to the delegation.
