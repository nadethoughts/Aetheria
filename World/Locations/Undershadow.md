# Undershadow

The Undershadow is a vast subterranean or shadow-adjacent realm beneath Aetheria. It is associated with necrotic power, abyssal patrons, ancient darkness, and artifacts that do not belong comfortably in the surface world.

During the Nimble campaign, Alder's patron revealed that Alder's lost rot-heirloom lies somewhere in the Undershadow. The nearest known entrance may be in or near the Shimmering Forest.

The realm also contains the [[Shadow Well]], the only currently known lead for stripping the power from the [[Axe of Snaggletooth]]. The party has not yet entered the Undershadow.
