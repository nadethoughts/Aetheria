# Nimble Session 07 Report

## Executive Summary

Session 07 began at the ruined Erie outpost as the party prepared to pursue its new objective: recruit the neutral orc tribes before Razor could bring them into his coalition. Zing Zow and the Cloud Seer riders agreed to secure the nearby Erie mine and send a signal once it was safe.

The party spent the night under Cloud Seer protection and received a safe rest. Rubius healed his wound and recharged his True Strike Wand. Alder communed with Erabose and learned that his patron still expects him to fulfill an earlier demand. Umbrail searched the outpost's books, confirmed that he had unknowingly taken a lesser Erie artifact from his mentor, learned how to cleanse it, and selected its identity: the **Talon of Unmaking**, an anti-armor talisman that is especially effective against dragons.

At dawn, Zing Zow identified the three neutral tribes. The **Frostvein**, led by the level-headed chieftess **An Yue**, live among the cold mountain peaks. The religious **Graven Hand**, led by **Bao Jun**, oversee the orc burial grounds. The subterranean **Hollow Drum**, led by the action-oriented **Chen Wei**, communicate across the mountains using drums. The tribes are not at war with one another, but they share longstanding resentment toward the Erie.

The party chose the northern ford and began traveling toward the Frostvein. Several days into the journey, it tried to bypass a wild warg family protecting injured pups. The detour accidentally brought the party directly into the pack. Umbrail, Rubius, and Whisper successfully intimidated and assessed the animals, allowing everyone to withdraw without combat.

Near the river gorge, a riderless Cloud Seer griffon named **Quickfeather** sought the party's help. It carried Lil Chang to its dying rider, the Cloud Seer scout **Da Long**, who had been shot down by Black Fang arrows. Lil Chang revived Da Long with a healing potion and chest compressions.

The scout delivered the session's decisive revelation: a Black Fang diplomatic entourage, protected by a full platoon of approximately fifteen to twenty orcs, was already heading toward the Frostvein to recruit the neutral tribes for Razor. Quickfeather could still beat the larger group to the village. Lil Chang and the scout flew ahead while the rest of the party followed by secret paths and considered how to delay the Black Fang without falsely portraying the delegation as an attacking army.

## Detailed Chronological Report

### 1. The Party Confirmed Its Strategic Direction

The session opened with a recap of the Cloud Seers, the Black Fang, and Razor's growing coalition. The Cloud Seers and Erie are allied against Razor, while the Black Fang and the mountain kobolds support the dragon. Other orc tribes remain neutral.

Zing Zow and the Cloud Seer riders took responsibility for the Erie mine, whose ore can affect a dragon's breath weapon. This allowed the party to concentrate on diplomacy.

### 2. The Party Received a Safe Rest

The Cloud Seers remained at the ruined outpost overnight and planned to depart at first light. Their protection made the night a safe rest despite the outpost's damaged condition.

The rest restored the party's normal resources. Rubius healed his one wound and buried his True Strike Wand at the battlefield to recharge it.

### 3. Alder Communed with Erabose

Alder contacted Erabose during the night. The table clarified that Erabose is a deeply malevolent fallen god whose symbol is a murder of crows consuming the sun.

Erabose is displeased that Alder has not completed an earlier command, but Alder remains useful enough that the relationship continues. The session did not restate the command or create a new bargain.

### 4. Umbrail Recovered His Artifact

Umbrail searched the outpost's books for information about the artifact he suspected he had taken from his dead mentor. He confirmed that he possessed a meaningful Erie artifact, though not one of the three major artifacts hidden throughout the mountains.

Umbrail found the incantations needed to cleanse and reactivate the item. Five forms were considered, and he chose the **Talon of Unmaking**.

The talisman's established effect is:

- When Umbrail's spell damages an armored creature, he can spend 2 mana to reduce its armor by one step until the start of its next turn.
- Against a dragon, the reduction lasts until the end of the dragon's next turn.
- It is carried as a talisman and does not occupy an equipment slot.

### 5. Zing Zow Identified the Neutral Tribes

At dawn, Zing Zow named three neutral tribes and their leaders:

| Tribe | Leader | Revealed identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frostvein | An Yue | High-peak orcs associated with cold mountain territory; An Yue is cool and level-headed |
| Graven Hand | Bao Jun | Religious keepers of the orc burial grounds; Bao Jun and his people are considered unusual |
| Hollow Drum | Chen Wei | Subterranean orcs who communicate across caverns by drums; Chen Wei is known as a person of action |

The three tribes are not currently fighting each other. Their common complication is resentment toward the Erie, who have no presence in the eastern Balshar Mountains because the tribes pushed them out long ago.

Zing Zow emphasized that the party would need all three tribes if it hoped to defeat the Black Fang, Rock Scale kobolds, and Razor.

### 6. The Party Chose the Ford

The party considered traveling by griffin, but the animals were uneasy around Rubius and Alder. Zing Zow could spare a griffin or two for other party members, but the group initially chose to remain together.

The Hollow Drum settlement was closest by air. By land, the Frostvein settlement was closest because it lay nearest the northern ford. The party therefore began traveling toward the ford.

### 7. The Party Avoided a Warg Fight

After several days, the party found large tracks along a game trail. Umbrail scouted ahead and discovered wild wargs traveling in the same direction.

Closer observation showed that the pack was moving slowly to protect injured pups. The party attempted to navigate around the animals but became lost in the woods and emerged directly into the pack.

The adult wargs closed around their young but did not immediately attack. Umbrail used Tempest's Command to appear larger and more dangerous, Rubius raised a boulder, and Whisper assessed the pack's intentions.

Whisper determined that the animals would allow the party to leave if no one advanced aggressively. The party withdrew, and the wargs did not pursue. The encounter ended without attacks or casualties.

### 8. Quickfeather Found the Party

Near the river gorge, the party saw a griffin approaching without a rider. The animal wore Cloud Seer tack and tried to pull Lil Chang back the way it had come.

The griffin communicated that its friend needed help. Lil Chang mounted it and was carried across the river to a rocky outcrop. There he found an unconscious Cloud Seer scout who was no longer breathing.

The scout had been struck by black-tipped, barbed Black Fang arrows. Lil Chang used a healing potion and chest compressions to revive him.

The griffon was identified as **Quickfeather**, Da Long's companion.

### 9. The Scout Revealed the Black Fang Mission

The scout had discovered that the Black Fang were farther east than expected. A Black Fang diplomatic entourage was traveling toward the neutral tribes with a full platoon of approximately fifteen to twenty orcs.

Their immediate destination was the Frostvein settlement. Their purpose was recruitment, not a confirmed attack. They detected the scout and shot him down before he could return with the warning.

The scout believed the neutral tribes could decide the outcome of the war. He also warned that the tribes were proud and could not simply be purchased.

### 10. The Party Split to Win the Race

Quickfeather was faster than the Black Fang entourage. Lil Chang and the scout therefore agreed to fly ahead to the Frostvein settlement.

The scout gave the rest of the party directions through secret paths. The party hoped to move alongside the Black Fang group and create delays, potentially by drawing away smaller groups, while Lil Chang began negotiations.

The group recognized that telling the Frostvein an attacking army was coming would be dishonest. The Black Fang were protected diplomats, and the party still needed a credible way to expose their aggression or counter their offer.

The session ended before the party settled on a final delaying tactic.
