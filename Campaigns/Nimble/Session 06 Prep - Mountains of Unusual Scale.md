# Session 06 Prep - Mountains of Unusual Scale

**Campaign:** Nimble  
**Arc:** Mountains of Unusual Scale  
**Expected level:** Party is level 2 at start; very likely level 3 after the griffin/outpost beat.  
**Current location:** A ruined Erie outpost in the Balshar mountains, after the party investigated the aftermath of a kobold/orc assault and made camp near the crest.

## Current State

The party finished Warren's Want in Clackston, saved the livestock, defeated Snaggletooth, and recovered the cursed shadow axe. The axe is now with **Rubius Haggard** and currently presents itself as comforting, safe, and meant for him. It deals **3d4** as a battleaxe and has already spoken in his mind: "Welcome friend, let's drink the light together."

The party chose to investigate where the black-scaled kobolds came from. Umbrail recognized the kobolds as linked to the Balshar range, which pulled the party toward his homeland and the declining Erie birdfolk.

The party found:

- **Kobold and orc tracks moving together**, despite those groups normally not cooperating.
- **Kobold dig sites/quarries** in the mountains.
- A **desecrated Erie wind shrine**, recarved with draconic/kobold features.
- A **huge shadow overhead** and claw marks from something much larger than a kobold.
- A destroyed **Erie outpost** atop a high rock pillar/nest.
- Dead Erie, orcs, and kobolds. The orcs were the main assault force; the kobolds mostly failed below.
- Strange spent **air-launch devices** that let orcs or kobolds attempt controlled jumps/flight up to Erie perches.
- Umbrail found the body of his old magical mentor among the dead. Dave has not named this person yet.

The session ended as a **griffin** flew toward the camp, defending the fallen outpost as if the party were part of the attack.

## Opening Recap

"Last time, you followed the trail of the kobolds and orcs into the Balshar mountains, where the weather turned against you and the mountains started answering with teeth. After fighting off wargs, you lost the orc trail but found signs that something much larger is moving over the region: kobold quarries, a desecrated Erie shrine, strange claw marks, and a shadow in the sky. Then you reached an Erie outpost built high on a stone pillar, a place that should have been almost impossible for orcs or kobolds to breach. It had been burned, shattered, and overrun. ## Cold Open Read-Aloud

The pyres burn low against the mountain dark, their smoke whipped thin by a knife-cold wind. Below the crest, the dead lie in separate ash beds: Erie honored as best as the moment allows, orcs and kobolds burned before the mountain scavengers can claim them.

Then the wind changes.

The campfire gutters sideways. Loose feathers, ash, and flakes of old snow lift from the stone. Above the ruined perch, a shape cuts across the last bruised light of evening: eagle wings, lion body, talons opening as it folds into a dive.

It is not circling.

It has chosen.

## Haggard's Shadow Points

Use Shadow Points as a visible corruption track for the intelligent cursed axe. The point is temptation first, punishment second: every step should make the axe feel useful, safe, and increasingly possessive.

**Current Shadow Points:** 1

### How Haggard Gains Shadow Points

- +1 when he drops to 0 HP / becomes Dying while carrying or wielding the axe. This already happened once.
- +1 when he accepts the axe's offer to avoid a major consequence.
- +1 when he uses the axe to execute a helpless, surrendered, or non-hostile creature.
- +1 when he chooses the axe's whispered solution over an obvious act of mercy or restraint.
- Limit routine gains to **1 per session**, unless Haggard deliberately embraces the curse in a major scene.

### Mechanical Track

| Shadow Points | Gift | Corruption Tell |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shadow-Soothed.** While wielding the axe, Haggard reduces damage by **+1 extra** whenever he uses Defend. | The axe feels safer than armor. Haggard has disadvantage on checks to willingly hand it away, abandon it, or let someone bind it. |
| 2 | **Hungry Edge.** Once per turn after Haggard hits with the axe, if either he or the target is Bloodied, add **+2 shadow damage**. | When he drops a creature with the axe, DC 10 WIL save or his next action must move toward or attack a hostile creature if one is available. |
| 3 | **The Axe Keeps Him Standing.** When Haggard would drop to 0 HP, he may instead stay at 1 HP and gain another Shadow Point. | For a moment his shadow keeps fighting before his body catches up. Sensitive creatures, Erie rites, and sacred places notice. |
| 4 | **Drink the Light.** Once per field rest, action: extinguish mundane flames within reach 4 and gain temp HP equal to **LVL + Shadow Points**. | Bright light feels thin and irritating. The axe whispers most clearly near fire, dawn, and holy symbols. |
| 5 | **Dread Cleave.** Once per safe rest, after hitting with the axe, force the target to make a DC 12 WIL save or become Frightened of Haggard for 1 round. | Haggard has disadvantage on rolls to retreat, surrender, or spare an enemy while the axe can still "feed." |
| 6 | **Sung Into Night.** The next time Haggard would die, the axe offers survival at a terrible price. | This is a story climax, not an automatic character loss. The party needs the Shadow Well, a major cleansing rite, or a direct confrontation with the axe's will. |

### Axe Voice

Keep the voice calm, protective, and intimate. It should not sound like obvious villain dialogue.

- "I kept you here."
- "Pain is only a door. I can close it."
- "They fear what you carry because they fear what you could become."
- "You survived. They did not. I can help with that."
- "Let me be the quiet place where fear cannot reach you."

## Encounter 1 - The Griffin At The Ruined Crest

Use this as a dramatic guardian fight with vertical terrain. The griffin is an Erie ally and outpost guardian. It believes the party may be looters, desecrators, or the returning attackers. The goal is not to kill it; the goal is to reveal that the Erie had allies, that the outpost mattered, and that the mountains have more factions than Erie versus Shattered Fang.

### Terrain

- **Ruined Erie crest:** high stone platform with broken rails, burned beams, shattered masonry, and drop-offs.
- **Pyres:** smoky squares; entering or starting in smoke gives cover/obscurement, but fire can spread on a crit or GM move.
- **Broken wind devices:** can be studied or used as improvised hazards. On a clever DC 12 Arcana/Lore/Naturecraft, a hero can trigger a burst of wind to push a creature 2-4 spaces or launch themselves a short distance.
- **Cliff edge:** forced movement over the edge is dangerous. Use falling damage if someone goes over, but give a reaction/check before a PC is simply removed from the scene.
- **Collapsed beams:** cover against swoops and javelin-style attacks.

### Griffin Stat Block

Use if you want a level 2 party with six heroes to have a serious but survivable solo-style fight.

**Level 3 Solo Large Griffin**  
**HP:** 90  
**Armor:** Medium  
**Saves:** STR+, DEX+  
**Speed:** Fly 10, ground 6  

**Guardian's Fury:** The griffin has advantage on its first attack against a creature standing near Erie bodies, handling outpost relics, or wielding obvious shadow magic.

**Actions:** After each hero's turn, choose one:

- **Swoop and Talons:** Fly 8 before or after attacking. Attack for **2d6+4** damage. On crit: the target is **Prone**.
- **Beak and Rake:** Adjacent target only. Attack for **2d6+8** damage. If the target is Prone or Grappled, roll with advantage.
- **Wing Buffet:** Creatures within reach 2 make a **DC 11 STR save** or take **1d6** damage and are pushed 3 spaces. If pushed into a wall/edge/pyre, apply the normal consequence. Use once, recharge when bloodied.

**Bloodied at 45 HP:** The griffin shrieks and Wing Buffet recharges. It immediately flies 8 to high ground without provoking. The party can now notice an Erie-made bronze harness charm under its feathers.

**Story Point at 0 HP:** Do **not** kill the griffin. It crashes to the stone, alive but broken, and the bronze harness charm cracks open with stored wind-magic. Trigger **The Guardian Falls** below.

### If They Try Noncombat

The griffin is defending the Erie dead and the old outpost. It is wounded, exhausted, and acting on trained guardian instinct.

- **Offer fresh meat / make space:** DC 13 Naturecraft or Influence to get it to hesitate.
- **Use fire, smoke, or wing-shielding tactics:** DC 12 Naturecraft/Finesse/Arcana to create an opening or impose disadvantage.
- **Use Erie signs or old outpost calls:** Umbrail can attempt DC 12 Lore/Influence to recall a warning cry or warding signal. Success does not tame it, but it pauses long enough for the party to see the harness charm.
- **Bond of Peace or similar calming magic:** Let it matter. On a strong roll or good use, the griffin may become a clue rather than a corpse.

### The Guardian Falls

Trigger this when the griffin reaches 0 HP, is successfully calmed, or the party clearly proves they are honoring the Erie dead.

Read or paraphrase:

The griffin's talons scrape furrows through the scorched stone as it falls. For one sharp heartbeat, the outpost goes silent. Then the bronze charm woven into its harness splits open, and a trapped gust bursts free.

Ash, snow, and old feathers spiral into the air. In the turning wind you see broken images: Erie riders launching from this perch, the griffin flying beside them, a vast winged shadow crossing the mountain sun, orcs rising on crude wind-harnesses, kobolds swarming below with devices they barely understand.

The last image is not memory but warning: a dragon-shaped shadow passing over a high valley while a voice like stone dragged over steel says, "The old flights sleep beneath me now."

Then the wind dies.

The griffin still breathes.

## Core Mystery

The party should learn, over the next few scenes, that **Razor**, a dragon from the Meegalalor Hills, has moved into the western Balshar range. Razor wants to reclaim the mountains as a draconic homeland and is searching for the ancient resting place of the dragon flights. The Erie were secret guardians of that place after an ancient war between Erie and dragons.

The bigger story is not simply Erie versus orcs. Razor is breaking the old mountain balance between Erie, griffin guardians, orc clans, kobolds, and older things under the stone. Some groups are joining him, some are resisting, and some are trying to survive long enough to see who wins.

Razor has:

- Won the **Rock Scale Kobolds** into religious devotion.
- Made a quiet alliance with the **Shattered Fang Orcs**.
- Pressured rival orc clans to submit, move, or be destroyed.
- Given orcs/kobolds a way to breach Erie aerial superiority through wind-launch devices.
- Started attacking outposts to break the guardians before revealing himself openly.

## Factions

### Erie Birdfolk

Once a dynasty over the Balshar range. Now reduced to a village and scattered outposts. Proud, declining, still dangerous in the air. They secretly guard the dragonflight resting place and may blame exiles/wanderers for abandoning the old oaths.

Use with Umbrail:

- They know him, or know his family/teacher.
- Some will be relieved; others resent that he left.
- They are not extinct, but the outpost losses hurt badly.
- They may hide the dragonflight truth even from allies unless Umbrail vouches for the party.

### Shattered Fang Orcs

Cave/tunnel orcs of Balshar Gorge. Long blood feud with the Erie. Traditionally avoid open-air conflict because Erie dominate the skies. Now emboldened by Razor and the launch devices.

Tells:

- Broken fangs worn as tribal tokens.
- Orc bodies from the outpost had these markings.
- War parties are willing to die in unusual numbers because they believe Erie superiority is ending.

### Cloud-Tusk Orcs

Lil Chan's old mountain connection. The Cloud-Tusk are not Shattered Fang: they are high-pass orcs, guides, traders, and storm-hunters who maintain rope bridges, cliff trails, and old safe routes through the western Balshar. They have fought the Erie at times, traded with them at others, and still respect the griffin guardians as part of the mountain's law.

Use them to give Lil Chan an immediate social stake and to show that Razor's rise is threatening orc culture too.

Key NPCs:

- **Kora Skybreak:** Cloud-Tusk pathwarden. Direct, weather-beaten, and impossible to impress by accident. She recognizes Lil Chan and calls him by an old clan name or nickname.
- **Jun Ashstep:** Lil Chan's old sparring partner/rival. Quick to smile, quicker to accuse him of running when debts came due.
- **Elder Mauk Cloud-Tusk:** clan elder at the hidden camp. Cautious and political; wants proof before risking the clan against Razor.

Possible Lil Chan history, pick or ask Ryan at table:

- Lil Chan trained with Cloud-Tusk pathrunners before leaving the mountains.
- He owes Kora a favor after she covered for him during a failed job or broken oath.
- Jun believes Lil Chan abandoned the clan during a winter crisis, whether or not that is fair.
- The clan still uses a younger nickname for him, which gives Ryan a chance to define who he used to be.

What they know:

- The Shattered Fang have accepted Razor's "sky-gifts" and are being used as the first wave against Erie holds.
- The Cloud-Tusk refused Razor's offer, so kobolds have sabotaged their rope bridges and wind shelters.
- Razor wants the ancient dragonflight resting place and is forcing every mountain faction to kneel, flee, or become useful.
- The wind-launch devices come from a kobold worksite beneath a corrupted wind shrine.
- The griffin, **Kareth**, is an Erie-bonded guardian, not a wild beast.

### Rock Scale Kobolds

Cowardly, opportunistic, tunnel-minded kobolds who worship Razor almost like a god. They are digging, scavenging, and testing devices they barely understand.

Tells:

- Molted black scales.
- Dig sites and rough quarries.
- Graffiti that overstates their role and glorifies giant leaders/ascensions.
- They may know Snaggletooth only as "the one who found a drinking shadow" or "the false-big."

### Razor

Dragon from the Meegalalor Hills. Wants the Balshar mountains to become a dragon homeland again. Does not need to appear fully yet. A shadow, a voice through kobolds, claw marks, heat-distorted air, and displaced predators are enough for now.

Titles kobolds might use:

- Razor Who Opens the Sky
- The Mountain That Breathes
- Scale-Father of the New Flight
- The Returning Wing

## Secrets And Clues

Use these flexibly. Do not wait for a specific roll; attach them to investigation, captured enemies, Erie survivors, or environmental discovery.

- The orcs and kobolds did not merely happen to raid the same place. They coordinated.
- Not all mountain orcs are Shattered Fang. The Cloud-Tusk have refused Razor and are paying for it.
- The air-launch devices are recent field tools, not ancient Erie architecture.
- The devices use captured/compressed wind, like a crude inversion of Erie shrine magic.
- The kobolds do not fully understand the devices; they overloaded some by dogpiling them.
- The orcs used the devices more successfully, implying someone trained or directed them.
- The Shattered Fang are not Razor's equals. They are useful because they can be aimed at Erie holds first.
- Cloud-Tusk bridges and shelters have been sabotaged to cut off neutral routes through the western Balshar.
- The outpost was reinforced with more Erie than usual, so they expected trouble.
- The stone damage resembles hurricane-force wind, not normal siege work.
- Razor is looking for an ancient site the Erie call the Resting Place of the Flights.
- Razor's next pressure point is a high valley where Erie patrol routes, Cloud-Tusk paths, and kobold tunnels nearly meet.
- The dragonflight resting place may sit above or near a route into the **Undershadow**.
- The Shadow Well rumor points to the Undershadow as the only place to unmake or drain the axe's power.
- Alder's lost rot/heirloom is also somewhere below, giving him a reason to keep following the deep route.
- Snaggletooth's "ascension" was a corrupted imitation of dragon transformation, shadow relic power, or both.
- The Flaming Retribution destabilized seals and exposed old ruins, which is why all of this is waking now.

## Nimble Table Reminders

- **Actions:** 3 actions per turn, refreshed at end of turn.
- **Initiative first round:** less than 10 = 1 action, 10-19 = 2 actions, 20+ = 3 actions. Whisper may be able to bump low initiative to 10.
- **Attack:** roll damage; a 1 on the primary die misses.
- **Multiple attacks:** each additional attack on the same turn stacks disadvantage.
- **Defend reaction:** spend an action to reduce one attack by armor.
- **Interpose reaction:** move an ally within 2 spaces out of the way and become the target.
- **Help reaction:** grant an ally advantage if the fiction supports it; call for a skill check only if needed.
- **Opportunity attack:** reaction, roll weapon damage at disadvantage when an enemy willingly leaves reach.
- **Assess:** use DC 10 per Session 04 errata/ruling, even though the printed cheat sheet says DC 12. Ask a question, create an opening, or anticipate danger.
- **NPCs:** generally do not have reactions.
- **Dying:** at 0 HP, a hero has 1 action per round. Attacking or casting while dying risks a wound on failed DC 10 STR save. Taking damage while dying causes wounds.
- **Field rest, 10 minutes:** spend hit die to heal rolled value + STR.
- **Field rest, 8 hours:** spend hit die to heal max value + STR.
- **Safe rest:** recover all HP/resources, heal 1 wound, may allow class option changes.
- **Smoldering:** status with no default effect, but it empowers features/spells that key off Smoldering.

## Level 3 Beat

After the griffin or after the first safe narrative pause, award level 3 if it feels earned. This is a good moment because:

- The party survived the Warren.
- They chose a broader direction.
- They found the Erie outpost tragedy.
- The campaign has shifted from "local kobolds" to "regional dragon conspiracy."

Ask each player to confirm subclass/class choices. Haggard was leaning **Mountain Heart** over **Red Mist** because of the giant connection. Others need table confirmation.

## Prep To Do Before Table

- Make/print the griffin stat block or put it in Foundry.
- Prepare a ruined Erie outpost map with cliff edges, smoke, broken beams, and one or two wind-device hazards.
- Have level 3 class/subclass references ready.
- Decide whether Razor appears only as a shadow this session or speaks through a kobold/orc.
- Pick whether the next strong lead points to the Erie village, the Cloud-Tusk camp, a kobold excavation, or an orc launch-device camp.
- Ask Dave to name Umbrail's mentor if possible.
- Ask Ryan for one detail about Lil Chan's old Cloud-Tusk life, or let Kora's first line force the answer in play.
- Decide whether Lil Chan left the Cloud-Tusk because of a debt, a failure, a betrayal, or simple ambition.
