# Nimble Session 09 Report

## Executive Summary

Session 09 split between a Black Fang patrol fight on the Frostvein plateau and Lil Chang's first audience at Icehold.

The land party was approached by Black Fang fighters and horse-sized wargs shortly after passing the main delegation at the Stone Stairs. Combat began before either side attempted to speak. Umbrail used Arc Lightning and the Talon of Unmaking to break the patrol's armor; Alder's shadows killed a warg; Scritch exploited openings with his sling; Rowan survived being grappled in a warg's jaws and killed it with his spear; and Rubius returned to the Axe of Snaggletooth despite his attempt to control his rage.

Rubius accepted two explicit axe bargains, raising his transcript-supported total from three to **five Shadow Points**. The axe consumed the patrol's veteran and visibly intensified. Rubius also suffered a Wound, though the table did not announce the additional Shadow Point that the current GM-facing axe rules would assign for it. One Black Fang scout escaped, meaning the delegation can learn that the previously unseen party is ahead and has killed members of its faction.

Meanwhile, Lil Chang and Da Long reached a Frostvein watchtower, landed to avoid appearing aggressive, and were escorted by Warden Feng Rui to **Icehold**. Chieftess An Yue heard their warning alongside quartermaster Jian Bo and lore keeper Mei Rong. The council did not accept an immediate alliance. Frostvein needs its people and supplies to survive winter, refuses to surrender control of its passes, and wants written assurances that resistance to Razor will not restore Erie rule.

Da Long offered the location of useful hidden hunting grounds as proof of good faith. The discussion produced possible terms involving formal independence, limited contributions, material support, shared route intelligence, and military training. An Yue granted hospitality, asked the visitors to remain for the Black Fang hearing, and said she would confer with her advisors. Da Long intends to contact higher authorities after spending the night; Lil Chang will wait for the land party.

## Detailed Chronological Report

### 1. A Black Fang Patrol Found the Land Party

Howls announced Black Fang riders and war wargs approaching across the plateau. Their tribal markings were visible. No warning, capture demand, peace pennant, or diplomatic exchange occurred in the played scene before initiative.

This encounter is consistent with the prepared **Black Fang Outrider Patrol**, including its veteran, hunters, war wargs, and shield-bearer. The prep's nonlethal capture objective and attempted parley did not enter play and therefore should not be treated as character knowledge.

### 2. The Party Defeated the Patrol at a Cost

Important contributions included:

- Umbrail chained Arc Lightning between armored targets and repeatedly activated the Talon of Unmaking.
- The overloaded shield-bearer's shield exploded and killed him.
- Alder summoned shadows and destroyed one warg with minion attacks and a Shadow Blast.
- Scritch used distraction and Cheat abilities to land decisive sling attacks.
- Rubius interposed against the veteran's strongest attack and reduced it with his Fury and Mountainheart resilience.
- A warg grappled Rowan in its jaws; Rowan killed it by driving his spear upward through its mouth.
- The party brought down one retreating scout, but the last survivor escaped.

Because the escaping scout can identify the party, the Black Fang column's lack of awareness at the end of Session 08 is no longer a safe assumption.

### 3. Rubius Chose the Axe's Power Twice

Rubius attempted to enter a controlled rage and initially avoided touching the axe. In anger, he later drew it almost unconsciously.

After a critical hit, the axe offered increased damage for one Shadow Point. Rubius accepted, killed the veteran with an empowered attack, and watched the body dissolve into the blade. He accepted the bargain again after another critical hit, escalating a later attack to 3d8.

The session contains two explicit bargain costs, bringing the recorded total from three to five. Rubius also became Wounded. The current GM-facing artifact rules state that gaining a Wound while bonded always grants a Shadow Point, but no such award was announced. This report preserves **five** as played canon and flags **six** as a GM rules-reconciliation option rather than silently rewriting the table result.

### 4. Lil Chang and Da Long Reached Frostvein Territory

Quickfeather carried the two orcs over the plateau, an ice labyrinth, and a series of defended watch posts. They landed near a stone tower instead of overflying it.

Feng Rui identified Da Long as a Cloud Seer and Lil Chang as an outlander. After hearing their request for an audience, he led them several hours to Icehold and gathered Jian Bo and Mei Rong for the council.

### 5. An Yue Heard the Coalition's Warning

Lil Chang described Razor and the Black Fang as a combined threat capable of defeating divided tribes one at a time. Da Long admitted that he had been spying on the Black Fang and showed the injury from being shot down.

The Frostvein did not deny the danger. Their question was whether the proposed resistance would preserve Frostvein freedom or merely place the tribe back beneath Erie authority.

### 6. Frostvein Defined Its Needs

The council established several constraints:

- Winter is arriving quickly, and Frostvein's survival is materially precarious.
- Warriors are also hunters, laborers, and pass keepers; sending them away weakens the settlement.
- Foreign armies may not use Frostvein territory without local control and permission.
- Any contribution of soldiers or supplies must be specific and limited.
- Postwar independence requires written, signed assurances.
- The Black Fang will receive the peace their banner promises until their conduct proves otherwise.

Da Long contributed a concrete benefit by revealing overlooked hunting grounds. The information would not solve the winter crisis alone, but it would improve Frostvein's stores.

### 7. The First Negotiation Ended Without a Verdict

Lil Chang explained that the Erie possess anti-dragon artifacts and argued that the war need not become an endless drain on Frostvein. Neither he nor Da Long claimed authority to sign for the Erie or Cloud Seers.

Possible cooperation narrowed toward:

- Route and intelligence exchange
- Specialized military or animal-handling training
- Limited volunteers rather than conscription
- Material support for winter
- Formal recognition of sovereignty and territorial control
- A future meeting with authorized leaders

An Yue offered hospitality and asked the visitors to remain for the Black Fang presentation. She will confer privately with her council before deciding. Da Long plans to request a formal delegation, and Lil Chang will wait for his companions.
