# Nimble Session 02 Report

## Executive Summary

Session 02 took the party through the first major section of the kobold warren and served as the campaign's practical combat tutorial. Umbrail translated the warning at the entrance, identifying the site as a dangerous Second Age ruin associated with the "Shadow of the Sun." The party then entered an old barracks complex occupied by black-scaled kobolds.

The first battle demonstrated Nimble's action economy and the party's emerging roles. Rubius charged into the enemy line, Whisper turned a failed attack into a critical hit, Alder summoned a shadow minion, Rowan protected allies through Interpose, and Umbrail used a Charged Overload to destroy a clustered group of kobolds. The victory produced the first fragment of an orichalcum sun crest and a dangerous healing item called a Caustic Brew.

Graffiti and environmental evidence showed that the kobolds intended to conquer Clackston and served a physically enormous "big boss." At a junction, the party chose to investigate an eastern magical resonance before following the sounds of stolen animals to the north. A successful group challenge carried them through pressure plates, steam, a collapsing ceiling, and fallen masonry into a treasury. There they recovered gold and another crest fragment.

The party then entered the animal pens and fought a larger kobold force led by a support specialist called a Kobold Clangor. Rowan was knocked down during the fight, but Alder used the Caustic Brew to restore him at the cost of poisoning him. Rubius ultimately killed the Clangor with a brutal axe strike. The session ended with the animal-room defenders defeated, the stolen livestock found, and the deeper warren—including the mysterious boss and the complete sun crest—still unresolved.

## Session at a Glance

- **Starting situation:** The five-member party stood at the newly exposed doorway to the kobold warren.
- **Primary location:** A buried Second Age barracks beneath the crater south of Clackston.
- **Major encounters:** Entrance-hall kobolds, a four-part environmental challenge, and the animal-pen battle.
- **Major discoveries:** Kobold conquest graffiti, two orichalcum crest fragments, and evidence of Shadow Spawn history.
- **Major decision:** The party explored the magically resonant eastern passage before rescuing the animals to the north.
- **Major setback:** Rowan was knocked down and then poisoned by the healing brew used to save him.
- **Major victory:** The party defeated the Kobold Clangor and located the stolen livestock.
- **Unresolved objective:** The animals still needed to be freed and the "big boss" remained deeper in the ruin.

## Detailed Chronological Report

### 1. The Warning Was Translated

The session resumed at the crater entrance. Umbrail used his knowledge of the Old Tongue to interpret the inscription warning the Keepers of the Dawn about the "Shadow of the Sun" and a thing that "drinks the light."

The party connected the structure to the **Second Age** and the ancient conflicts involving Shadow Spawn. The exact history remained incomplete, but the site appeared to have been an old barracks or defensive installation rather than a structure built by the kobolds. The recent impact had exposed something buried and forgotten.

### 2. The Characters Explained Why They Adventured

Before entering, the table developed each character's reason for leaving home:

- Rowan had been a farmer and local helper. After training someone to continue his work, he left to serve a wider community.
- Umbrail came from a dwindling Birdfolk people and wanted to see the world while he still could.
- Rubius wanted to prove he was unlike the hostile giants people feared and to search old ruins for a lost giant enclave.
- Whisper wanted excitement and relief from boredom, while keeping deeper family complications mostly private.
- Alder was a homesteader whose own livestock had been taken by the kobolds.

These answers turned a paid posse into a group whose members had reasons to continue adventuring even after Clackston's immediate problem was solved.

### 3. The Entrance-Hall Battle Taught the Combat System

The first chamber held a large group of kobold minions. The fight was used to establish the core Nimble rules: three-action turns, first-round actions based on initiative, no traditional Armor Class, automatic failure on a natural 1, stacking advantage, and reactions such as Defend, Interpose, Opportunity, and Help.

The party's roles became clear quickly:

- Rubius used his size and speed to charge the kobold line.
- Whisper used the Cheat's ability to transform a natural 1 into a critical result.
- Alder created a shadow minion and attacked with Shadow Blast.
- Rowan Interposed to protect Whisper and held the front line.
- Umbrail initially missed with Zap, became **Charged**, and then used **Overload** to strike a clustered group, eliminating several kobolds at once.

The party won without a prolonged attritional struggle, demonstrating how quickly coordinated actions and area attacks could remove minions.

### 4. The First Crest Fragment and Caustic Brew

After the fight, the party searched the chamber and recovered a golden-orange metal fragment shaped like part of a sun. Umbrail identified the metal as **orichalcum** and took responsibility for the fragment.

The party also found a **Caustic Brew**. The brew heals **3d6 hit points**, but its drinker becomes **Poisoned for three rounds**. Alder held the item initially. Its combination of strong healing and a serious temporary penalty made it an emergency resource rather than a routine potion.

### 5. The Ruin's History and the Kobolds' Intentions

The architecture and surviving symbols suggested that the complex had once been a Second Age barracks. The warning's "Shadow of the Sun" was associated with the old Shadow Spawn conflicts and the War of the Fractured Sun. The precise connection to La Pelu and the site's builders remained uncertain.

The party also found newer kobold graffiti. It described plans to conquer Clackston and glorified a very large "big boss." This confirmed that the livestock thefts were part of an organized campaign rather than isolated scavenging.

### 6. The Party Chose the Eastern Passage

At a T-junction, the party heard animals bleating to the north and detected a strange resonating tone to the east. Rescuing the livestock was the obvious mission objective, but the magical resonance suggested a mechanism or secret that could matter deeper in the ruin.

The group chose to investigate east first. This was a calculated detour: the animals were alive, while the unknown resonance might lead to a key, treasure, or danger that would be better handled before confronting the main kobold force.

### 7. The Environmental Challenge

The eastern route presented a sequence of hazards that required different characters to contribute:

- Umbrail and Rowan cooperated to understand resonating floor plates.
- Alder used Nature Craft, with an exceptional result, to manage bursts of steam.
- Whisper handled the danger of a falling ceiling.
- Rubius dealt with a fallen stone pillar through strength.

The party succeeded across the full sequence. Two kobolds had died in the hazardous passage, and one carried another piece connected to the sun crest.

### 8. The Old Treasury

Beyond the hazards, the party found an ancient treasury or storage room. Most weapons, tapestries, and other contents had become brittle or decayed. Rubius cautiously tested a chest rather than trusting it blindly.

The useful rewards were:

- **60 gold pieces**, divided as **12 gold per character**
- A second substantial **orichalcum crest fragment**

The growing crest was clearly more than ordinary treasure. Its shape and the mechanisms in the ruin suggested that it would eventually function as a key.

### 9. The Animal-Pen Battle

The party returned to the northern route and found the stolen animals held behind a mixed kobold force. The defenders included numerous weak kobolds, sturdier lieutenants, and a **Kobold Clangor** whose noise and commands enhanced nearby allies.

The fight was more dangerous than the entrance battle:

- Umbrail used Heart's Fire to grant Whisper an additional action and later relied on Snow Blind and Flame Dart.
- Rubius killed several enemies and drove toward the Clangor.
- Whisper cut through kobolds with the Cheat's explosive offense.
- Alder used his shadow minion and attacks to pressure multiple targets.
- Rowan repeatedly protected the group but was eventually knocked down.

Alder used the Caustic Brew on Rowan. It restored enough health to return him to the fight, but the brew's drawback left Rowan Poisoned for three rounds.

Rubius finished the Clangor with a devastating axe strike described as splitting the creature from crown to groin. Once the support leader fell, the remaining defenders could not reverse the battle.

The session ended immediately after the victory. The livestock had been located, but the party still had to release and escort the animals, search the fallen kobolds, complete the crest, and confront the boss.

## Character-by-Character Impact

### Rubius Haggard

Rubius emerged as the party's main physical force. He handled obstacles, drove into enemy formations, and killed the Clangor with the session's most violent finishing blow. The ruin continued to appeal to his interest in lost history, though no giant connection was confirmed.

### Whisper

Whisper demonstrated why the Cheat is dangerous: even a natural 1 could become a critical success. Umbrail's Heart's Fire further amplified his action economy, establishing Whisper as one of the party's best targets for additional actions and advantage.

### Umbrail

Umbrail translated the entrance, identified or carried the crest fragment, and delivered the most decisive attack of the entrance battle with a Charged Overload. His combination of lore, support magic, and area damage made him central to both investigation and combat.

### Rowan Hearthboro

Rowan repeatedly protected allies through Interpose and front-line positioning. Being knocked down in the animal-room fight showed the cost of that role. Accepting the Caustic Brew kept him alive but left him with a mechanical consequence that could carry into the next scene.

### Alder

Alder's shadow minion and Shadow Blast established his battlefield identity. More importantly, he used the party's dangerous healing resource to save Rowan, demonstrating practical judgment and loyalty despite the unsettling nature of his magic.

## Overall Assessment

Session 02 converted the campaign's premise into a functioning adventuring party. Every character contributed through a distinct mechanic, and the increasingly dangerous encounters showed how support, protection, positioning, and resource use matter as much as raw damage. The party also proved willing to take a thoughtful detour when the eastern resonance suggested a larger mystery.

The most important narrative development was the confirmation that the warren was built inside a Second Age complex and that the kobolds were preparing an attack on Clackston. The crest fragments provided a physical through-line toward the deeper chambers, while Rowan's near defeat demonstrated that the warren could threaten even a coordinated group. Session 03 was positioned to resolve the livestock rescue, introduce whatever the crest opened, and finally reveal the kobolds' oversized leader.
