# Session 11 — GM Run Sheet

## Session Objectives

- Begin the Frostvein negotiation.
- Make Vekka's staged landslide easy to uncover.
- Introduce Old Bristle as an apparent selfish thief, with room to reinterpret his actions.
- End Session 11 without a Frostvein alliance decision.

**Why:** Session 12 needs time to resolve the local threat, Bristle's change, and the final political choice—in that order.

## Run Order

| Beat | Target time | Required result | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1. Bristle appears | 5–10 min | Party sees him taking food and supplies from the dead | Introduce him before the crisis as a suspect and apparent villain |
| 2. Negotiation begins | 15–25 min | Erie/party and Black Fang offers are heard | Establish the competing promises the crisis will test |
| 3. Landslide alarm | 5 min | Rimehook residents and supplies are at risk | Force an urgent response |
| 4. Black Fang splits | 5 min | Vekka moves with half the guards; Gorak is surprised | Show the hidden plan through behavior |
| 5. Rescue/investigation | 30–45 min | People rescued; conspiracy identified | Let actions and evidence shape Session 12's decision |
| 6. Debrief | 10–15 min | Evidence recorded; An Yue postpones her choice | Preserve the decision for the end of Session 12 |

## GM-Only Truth

- Quartermaster **Vekka Ash-Eye** arranged a landslide with a contingent of Razor-aligned kobolds.
- Vekka wants the incident to make the Black Fang look like Frostvein's necessary protectors, pushing the tribe toward an alliance with Razor.
- **Gorak Broken-Tusk** does not know the plan. He expects possible trouble but is surprised when the landslide happens and Vekka's response is ready.
- Kobolds directly assisted Vekka. There is no separate engineer or hidden technical explanation.
- **Old Bristle** is a Frostvein recluse and former pass-keeper. He has been stockpiling salvaged food and winter supplies for himself. He is grumpy, distrustful, and selfish in practice, but not malicious. He leaves personal valuables and helps no one during the landslide because he assumes others will handle rescue and believes food should not be wasted.
- Bristle's hidden stockpile contains enough food to let Frostvein survive winter despite the landslide losses. He must choose to share it; the party can help him see that Frostvein may accept him if he extends kindness.

**Why:** Keep motives simple and legible. There is no secret mastermind beyond Vekka and the kobolds, and Bristle's flaw is hoarding rather than cruelty.

## 1. Introduce Old Bristle

**Place:** Before the hearing, near the hall or on the route from the gate.

- The party sees Bristle taking food, blankets, and usable gear from the bodies of people killed in a recent snow incident or from their abandoned supply sled.
- He does not take jewelry, weapons, or personal keepsakes. He gives no aid and leaves with the provisions.
- Frostvein locals call him a thief and a miser. Bristle refuses to explain himself and avoids the party.
- If confronted, he says the food would otherwise spoil or be wasted. He does not threaten anyone or attack.
- Do not reveal the size or location of his stockpile yet.

**Why:** Give the party a clear reason to distrust him while planting limits to his selfishness. His later change must be earned through play.

## 2. Negotiation Begins

An Yue asks each side for an offer. Give each player one chance to make a point, ask a question, or name a term.

| Speaker | Offer / concern |
| --- | --- |
| Gorak | Black Fang protection, non-aggression, and a place in Razor's order; he believes this is a genuine offer |
| Vekka | Logistics, escorts, winter supplies, and pass defense; presents dependence as practical security |
| Party | Erie coalition, anti-dragon knowledge, Da Long's hunting grounds, and sovereignty protections; cannot sign for Erie/Cloud Seer leaders |
| An Yue | Frostvein needs winter survival, pass control, limited obligations, and signed postwar independence |

Interrupt before An Yue decides.

**Why:** Make the impending crisis directly test what the delegates promised.

## 3. Landslide Alarm and Delegation Split

- Rimehook's bells report a landslide at Three-Bells Basin.
- Frostvein residents and a supply sled are trapped.
- A runner reports kobolds at the slope.
- Vekka immediately takes 7–8 of the 15 Black Fang warriors and heads to the site.
- Gorak and the remaining warriors are surprised. Gorak tries to organize help and asks Vekka why she already knows the route.
- An Yue asks the party to help but does not authorize Black Fang troops to enter Frostvein territory.
- The party may follow Vekka, take another route, question Gorak, or split up.

**Why:** The contrast between Vekka's preparation and Gorak's surprise is the first direct clue.

## 4. Rescue and Easy-to-Find Evidence

### Rescue objectives

- Rescue two trapped Frostvein residents.
- Recover the supply sled or its remaining food.
- Stop kobolds from triggering another slide or escaping with their equipment.

### Evidence — reveal at least two without a check

| Evidence | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| Kobold tools, markers, and a prepared trigger point are visible at the slope | The landslide was deliberate |
| A captured kobold admits Vekka hired or directed them | Vekka arranged it |
| Vekka's group arrives at the exact rescue point before Frostvein searchers identify it | The Black Fang response was planned |
| A signal/token links the kobolds to Vekka's force | The kobolds and Black Fang were working together |
| Gorak's questions and actions show he did not know the plan | Gorak was not part of the conspiracy |

- Give direct answers when players inspect the scene, question the kobold, or challenge the obvious timeline.
- Rolls can affect rescue time, evidence preservation, and injuries; do not use rolls to hide the core truth.
- Vekka may deny intent or claim the plan was to create a safe rescue. The physical evidence and captured kobold make the deception apparent.
- Bristle is also present taking food and usable supplies from the dead or wreckage while others rescue survivors. If confronted, he leaves rather than fight.

**Why:** Players should be able to understand the conspiracy in Session 11, leaving Session 12 for consequences and the final choice.

## 5. End Session 11

- Bring survivors, evidence, and the captured kobold (if any) before An Yue.
- Gorak recognizes that Vekka acted without his knowledge.
- Vekka's future is unresolved: she may be detained, escape, or remain under watch based on the party's actions.
- An Yue delays Frostvein's choice until the end of Session 12, after she sees whether the party can protect Frostvein and what support is truly available.
- Locals repeat their complaints about Bristle's looting; no one yet knows how much food he has stored.

**Why:** Keep the political decision open until Bristle's generosity and the minion threat have both played out.

## Session 12 — Payoff and Final Decision

### 1. Find Bristle's stockpile

- Party investigates Bristle or follows his tracks from the landslide.
- They discover a large, well-organized stockpile of food, blankets, and winter supplies.
- Bristle admits he kept it for himself. He thought sharing would leave him with nothing and does not believe Frostvein would welcome him.
- The reserve is enough for Frostvein to survive winter despite landslide losses, if he shares it.

**Why:** Bristle's selfishness has real consequences, but his store can remove hunger as the deciding pressure in Frostvein's alliance choice.

### 2. Give the party a chance to reach him

- The party can confront him, recognize his fear/isolation, invite him to help, and show that Frostvein can accept his contribution.
- Have a Frostvein survivor or local accept his help if the party makes the bridge.
- Bristle changes through a choice: he opens the stockpile and offers it to Frostvein.
- If the party treats him only as a criminal, he initially refuses. Let them make another appeal or demonstrate trust; do not force the change without player involvement.

**Why:** The emotional payoff depends on the party helping Bristle choose generosity and helping Frostvein accept him.

### 3. Resolve the Razor-minion threat

- Razor-aligned kobolds raid the actual winter stores or Bristle's cache, aiming to seize food and weaken Frostvein.
- Vekka's surviving allies may guide them if Vekka escaped; otherwise the kobolds act on Razor's orders.
- Bristle knows old snow tunnels and can help the party reach or outmaneuver the raiders if he has chosen to trust them.
- Run a contained fight or chase. The objective is to protect the food and drive off, capture, or defeat the raiders.
- Do not bring Razor into the encounter. Resolve this local operation; Razor remains the larger campaign threat.

**Why:** The party gets a concrete victory against Razor's forces, and Bristle's local knowledge makes his change useful to others.

### 4. End with Frostvein's decision

After the raid and Bristle's offer, An Yue asks the party and both delegations for final positions. Make the decision the last major scene of Session 12.

| What Frostvein weighs | Erie coalition | Razor / Black Fang |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Political trust | Stronger if Vekka's plot was exposed and Gorak repudiates it | Stronger if the staged rescue was mistaken for sincere protection |
| Winter survival | Bristle's food reserve makes independent choice possible; require aid commitments | Black Fang offer may still be attractive if Frostvein values immediate protection |
| Sovereignty | Written independence, pass control, and limited contributions must be specified | Require clear limits on Razor's authority and Black Fang troops |
| Party's conduct | Rescue, evidence, and helping Bristle build trust matter | Failure to expose or protect Frostvein strengthens the Black Fang case |

- Do not predetermine the choice. The food reserve gives Frostvein options; it does not automatically decide its allegiance.
- Record the chosen alliance, specific help Frostvein provides, and Vekka's status.
- Close with Bristle included in the community if he shared his food and the party helped restore trust.

**Why:** The alliance is decided after both the political deception and the winter-survival problem have been tested.

## Before-Session Checklist

- Decide how many people died in the earlier supply incident; keep Bristle's actions focused on food and usable gear, not personal keepsakes.
- Choose where Bristle's stockpile is and how the party can find it in Session 12.
- Set travel time to Rimehook and identify two trapped residents.
- Put visible kobold evidence at the trigger point and prepare one captured kobold's direct explanation.
- Decide how many Black Fang warriors Vekka takes (7–8).
- Pick a manageable kobold raid force for Session 12.
- Decide what evidence Gorak needs to turn against Vekka; don't make the plot depend on his choice.
- Leave An Yue's final decision until the final major scene of Session 12.

## Continuity

- Session 10 ends in Icehold before negotiations begin.
- Frostvein requires winter support, pass control, limited obligations, and signed guarantees of independence.
- The party has not yet learned Vekka's plan.
- Session 12 resolves the Frostvein recruitment adventure and the local Razor-minion threat. The larger war and Razor remain open.
