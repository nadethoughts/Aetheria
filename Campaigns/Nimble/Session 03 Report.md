# Nimble Session 03 Report

## Executive Summary

Session 03 completed the party's assault on the kobold warren and introduced its sixth member, **Lil Chang**. In the aftermath of the animal-pen battle, the party searched the kobold quarters, collected more pieces of the orichalcum sun crest, and found Lil Chang chained in a barred room. The Orc Zephyr had been traveling to Clackston to answer the same posse call when kobolds ambushed him. They intended to feed him into an unspecified test being prepared by their leader. The party freed him, and his desire for revenge gave him an immediate reason to join the expedition.

The party learned the name of the kobold leader through graffiti announcing **"Snaggletooth's Ascension."** Deeper in the ruin, they fought regimented kobold defenders near a circular stone door. After recovering and assembling the remaining crest pieces, they used the completed sun emblem as a key.

Beyond the door stood an old barracks or throne chamber dominated by a statue and damaged by kobold excavation. Four workers were widening a side passage while **Snaggletooth**, an enormous thirteen- to fourteen-foot kobold, waited with a shadow-infused axe. Snaggletooth declared that his ascension was at hand and attacked.

The boss fight was the party's most dangerous battle so far. The table discovered that the characters should already have advanced, so the group paused and completed the move to level 2 during the encounter. Snaggletooth passed through Bloodied, Frenzied, and Last Stand phases while Rowan protected the line, Alder multiplied and controlled shadows, Whisper exploited openings, Umbrail and Rubius contributed damage and support, and Lil Chang used his speed to stay engaged. Alder's Shadow Trap restrained the boss long enough for Lil Chang to land the killing blow with his staff, dedicating it to his mentor, **Wing Ding Ho**.

Snaggletooth's death reversed the transformation that had made him gigantic. He shrank into an ordinary kobold, while the shadow axe remained behind and fell dormant. The session ended with the warren's leader dead, the animals found, and the party facing a new question: return to Clackston immediately or investigate where these non-native kobolds came from.

## Detailed Chronological Report

### 1. The Animal-Room Aftermath

The session resumed after the death of the Kobold Clangor. The stolen sheep, goats, mule, and other animals were alive but still held by a magical field or containment system. Umbrail and Rubius stayed near the pens to study the mechanism while the others searched nearby rooms.

The party processed loot from the stronger kobolds. Pieces of metal carried by the Clangor completed more of the orichalcum crest, and the defeated lieutenants possessed additional Caustic Brews. The exact item handoff is difficult to attribute in the transcript, but the group had **two unused Caustic Brews** available after the search.

The party also reviewed breather and rest rules before continuing. This marked a transition from the long animal-room battle into the final section of the warren.

### 2. Lil Chang Was Found in Chains

The search party discovered a barred chamber containing an Orc prisoner. **Lil Chang** had been chained and left with limited food and water.

Lil Chang explained that he had been traveling toward Clackston in response to the same call for a posse that brought the original characters together. Kobolds ambushed him before he reached town. His captors said he was being kept for their "big boss" to test something, suggesting that Snaggletooth's plans involved more than livestock theft.

The party located a key among the fallen kobold leaders, freed Lil Chang, and gave him food and water. He joined immediately, driven by gratitude, self-preservation, and a strong desire to punish the kobolds who captured him.

Lil Chang's history also introduced **Wing Ding Ho**, his mentor. The exact circumstances surrounding the mentor were not fully explained, but Lil Chang invoked the name as a personal dedication when he later killed Snaggletooth.

### 3. "Snaggletooth's Ascension"

The party searched the private rooms used by the kobold leadership. Graffiti repeatedly glorified the boss and included the phrase **"Snaggletooth's Ascension."** This gave the unseen leader a name and indicated that his followers believed he was undergoing or preparing for a transformation.

The references made the "big boss" sound less like an unusually large kobold and more like the beneficiary of a ritual, artifact, or shadow power. The phrase also connected the boss to the warning at the entrance: something in the complex appeared capable of changing a creature by consuming or channeling light.

### 4. The Circular Door and Its Defenders

The next major chamber had a low or unstable ceiling, disciplined kobold guards, and a large circular stone door. The door contained a sun-shaped recess matching the pieces the party had been collecting. A child-sized opening and scraping sounds suggested that the kobolds had been working around or through the ancient barrier rather than operating it as intended.

The defenders included a warden, a trapper, and other regimented kobolds. This group fought more deliberately than the earlier minions. The party defeated them while managing the unstable environment and recovered the final piece or pieces needed to complete the crest.

Once assembled, the orichalcum sun emblem fit the circular recess. The door rotated and withdrew into the surrounding stone, confirming that the crest was an ancient access key rather than ordinary treasure.

### 5. The Chamber of Ascension

Beyond the door was a broad barracks or ceremonial chamber. An old statue held a shield in one hand and had an empty second hand. Four kobold workers were widening a side opening, damaging columns and making the room increasingly unstable.

At the center stood **Snaggletooth**. He was approximately thirteen to fourteen feet tall—far beyond the size of a normal kobold—and carried a shadowy battleaxe. He declared that his ascension was near and treated the party as intruders arriving at the moment of his triumph.

The workers were not committed soldiers. Once violence began, they fled, hid, or grovelled rather than meaningfully support the boss.

### 6. The Boss Fight

Snaggletooth operated under boss rules, acting after each player turn and changing behavior as his health declined. His size, reach, and shadow axe made him capable of threatening multiple characters before the party could safely reposition.

The fight highlighted the group's emerging roles:

- Rowan held the line and used protective reactions to prevent allies from absorbing Snaggletooth's strongest attacks.
- Alder summoned and directed additional shadow minions, attacked from range, and later used Shadow Trap to limit the boss's movement.
- Whisper repeatedly exploited attack opportunities and delivered heavy damage when the party created advantage.
- Umbrail supplied magic and battlefield support.
- Rubius confronted a foe nearly his own scale and contributed the party's most direct physical pressure.
- Lil Chang used Zephyr movement, staff attacks, and unarmed follow-ups to remain active around the boss.

During the fight, the table realized that the characters should have advanced before facing Snaggletooth. Play paused long enough to apply the level-2 benefits: increased hit points, an additional skill point, and new class abilities. The correction made the party more durable without erasing the danger of the encounter.

### 7. Snaggletooth's Final Phases

As Snaggletooth lost health, he became **Bloodied**, then **Frenzied**, and finally entered a **Last Stand**. Each stage made the battle feel more desperate and reinforced that the boss's power came from an unstable transformation rather than normal kobold strength.

Alder's Shadow Trap created the decisive opening. With Snaggletooth controlled and badly wounded, Lil Chang closed and struck with his staff. He dedicated the blow—"This one's for Wing Ding"—and killed the creature that had ordered his imprisonment.

### 8. The Transformation Ended

Death stripped away Snaggletooth's unnatural size. His body shrank into that of an ordinary kobold, strongly implying that the axe or associated shadow power had caused the transformation.

The shadow axe remained at full weapon size and became dormant. It was no longer actively attacking or empowering Snaggletooth, but it was clearly magical and potentially dangerous. No one claimed it before the session ended.

With the boss dead, the party had fulfilled the core mission: the animals were found, the local kobold leadership was destroyed, and Clackston was no longer under immediate threat from the warren. However, the presence of non-native black-scaled kobolds and the source of the shadow axe pointed toward a much larger problem.

- The party freed Lil Chang and allowed him to join the group.
- Lil Chang chose to assist in the assault rather than immediately return to Clackston.
- The party continued deeper after learning about Snaggletooth's ascension.
- The sun crest was assembled and used as the circular door's key.
- The party fought Snaggletooth rather than bargaining with him.
- The table corrected the missed advancement and moved the characters to level 2.

- The shadow axe was left unclaimed at the close of play.

### Important Decision Points Considered

- **Free and trust Lil Chang:** His story aligned with the party's mission, but he was still a stranger discovered inside enemy territory.
- **Rest or press onward:** The group chose to continue to the boss after brief recovery and loot processing.
- **Use the crest:** Opening the door committed the party to confronting whatever the kobolds had been protecting.
- **Kill, capture, or scatter the workers:** Their lack of commitment meant the party did not need to treat them like the primary combatants.
- **Claim the axe:** The potential reward and obvious danger were both left for Session 04.

## Character-by-Character Impact

### Lil Chang

Lil Chang received a complete introduction arc in a single session: prisoner, rescued stranger, active combatant, and slayer of the boss who ordered his captivity. His speed and close-range attacks added a new tactical dimension, while Wing Ding Ho gave him a personal history that could support future plot development.

### Rubius Haggard

Snaggletooth gave Rubius an opponent close to his own extraordinary scale. More importantly, the abandoned axe created a temptation specifically suited to him: a weapon capable of changing size and granting a feeling of power. That choice became his central issue in Session 04.

### Whisper

Whisper continued to excel when allies created openings. The longer boss fight gave him opportunities to exploit advantage and repeated attacks rather than simply eliminate minions. His personal story remained in the background.

### Umbrail

Umbrail helped process the magical enclosure and the crest, tying his lore role to the warren's ancient technology. The discovery that these kobolds were not native to Clackston's region also pointed the campaign toward the mountains associated with his people.

### Rowan Hearthboro

Rowan's protective role remained essential against a boss who acted repeatedly between player turns. His willingness to stand between Snaggletooth and more vulnerable allies helped the party survive long enough to exploit the boss's final phase.

### Alder

Alder's Shadow Trap created the decisive control effect in the boss fight. His expanding minion capability also raised a thematic question: he was using shadow against an enemy empowered by shadow, but the two forms did not appear identical or automatically allied.

## Overall Assessment

Session 03 closed the introductory warren adventure while opening the campaign's larger arc. Lil Chang's rescue strengthened the party both mechanically and socially, and Snaggletooth provided a satisfying explanation for the kobolds' confidence without explaining the deeper source of their power. The boss's transformation and dormant axe turned a local victory into evidence of a wider supernatural danger.

The session's strongest moment was the link between personal and tactical payoff: Lil Chang, whom Snaggletooth intended to use as a victim, became the character who ended the ascension. At the same time, the axe ensured that the consequences of the boss fight would continue. The next session needed to decide whether the party could leave dangerous power buried—or whether one of them would carry it out.
