# Nimble Session 08 Report

## Executive Summary

Session 08 followed the land party along Da Long's hidden forest route while Lil Chang and Da Long remained ahead on Quickfeather. Black Fang cloth led the group to a clearing occupied by the same kobold it had captured and released near Clackston in Session 01. The kobold introduced himself as **Scritch**. Injured, abandoned, and disillusioned with Razor's coalition, he accepted healing and joined the party.

Scritch confirmed that the Black Fang column contains twenty orcs: five diplomats under Envoy **Gorak** and fifteen warriors aligned with Quartermaster **Vekka**. The mission publicly remains a peace-bannered attempt to negotiate with the Frostvein, but Scritch believes it has an undisclosed objective.

Rubius found a beehive that could have served as a distraction. Although the bees did not want their home moved, the Axe of Snaggletooth influenced him to take it, earning him a Shadow Point. Seven kobolds then ambushed the party. During the fight, Rubius's rage became indiscriminate. The axe consumed three killed kobolds and drove Rubius to attack Alder after the enemies near him were gone.

Umbrail suppressed the rage and identified the axe's behavior as progressive corruption and soul consumption. Alder admitted that his patron wants him to kill Rubius and take the axe, then unsuccessfully tried to feed the axe to a shadow minion. The axe consumed the minion, became empowered, and nearly forced Rubius into another rage before Umbrail suppressed it with repeated Dispel Magic. Rubius ended the session with three recorded Shadow Points.

At the Stone Stairs, the party saw the Black Fang column camped on a switchback. It climbed the cliff away from the road and passed the delegation unseen. The session ended on a snowbound Frostvein plateau in a heavy storm, with the Black Fang below and Lil Chang still ahead.

## Detailed Chronological Report

### 1. The Party Followed Da Long's Route

Da Long's directions led the land party into an old pine forest where the trail disappeared and resumed beneath a dense canopy. Black cloth caught in brambles suggested someone associated with the Black Fang had entered the woods.

The party initially prepared to ambush or distract members of the delegation. Rubius found a beehive while Umbrail and the others examined a clearing near a small stream.

### 2. The Party Recruited Scritch

The clearing held the kobold the party had captured beside Clackston's stolen mule in Session 01. He gave his name as **Scritch**.

Scritch had returned to the mountains after his release and joined Razor's kobolds because he had nowhere else to go. An injured leg made him a liability to the Black Fang column. After mistreatment, he slipped away during a rest, became lost, and sheltered beneath a discarded Black Fang blanket.

Rowan gave him a healing draught. Scritch joined the party for protection and revenge.

### 3. Scritch Described the Black Fang Column

Scritch established a clearer command structure:

| Group | Strength | Leader | Known role |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Diplomats | 5 | Gorak | Conduct negotiations under a peace banner |
| Warriors and raiders | 15 | Vekka | Protect the column and answer to the quartermaster |

Scritch had seen no open conflict between the two groups and no obvious spellcasting. He nevertheless believed the column had a hidden purpose beyond the stated Frostvein treaty.

### 4. The Axe Overrode Rubius's Judgment

Rubius's Wild One affinity allowed him to communicate with the bees, which made clear that they did not want their hive moved. He decided to leave them alone, but the axe changed his mind. His eyes flashed black as he took the hive. The GM awarded one Shadow Point.

This was the first played example of the axe visibly replacing one of Rubius's moral decisions with its own impulse.

### 5. Seven Kobolds Ambushed the Group

The party failed to notice seven kobolds approaching through the forest. The attackers included slingers, an alchemist with a bee-filled explosive, and a heavily armored Clanger.

Important outcomes included:

- Umbrail granted Rubius an extra action with Heart's Fire.
- Rubius safely returned the displaced hive to a tree before fighting.
- Alder's shadow minions dealt necrotic damage and absorbed attacks.
- Scritch killed attackers with a sling and rapier, proving his immediate usefulness.
- Rowan used Blessed Aim against the armored Clanger and Interpose to protect Scritch.
- Umbrail blinded enemies with Snowblind and repositioned with Spark Step.
- The party defeated all seven attackers.

The recording did not establish that the party recovered written orders or learned the attackers' purpose. The Session 08 combat prep identifies them as a retrieval team seeking Scritch and route notes, but that motive remains GM truth rather than character knowledge.

### 6. The Axe Consumed Three Kobolds and Turned on Alder

Rubius entered a rage that he allowed to select targets indiscriminately. Three kobolds killed by the axe dissolved into black matter and were absorbed into the weapon. After killing the Clanger, Rubius attacked Alder instead of the remaining enemy.

Rubius's eyes were pitch black during the attack. He dealt twelve damage to Alder. The GM awarded a second Shadow Point, bringing Rubius's tracked total to three.

### 7. The Party Confronted the Curse

Umbrail used Tempest Command to suppress the rage and recognized the axe's behavior from prior research. He explained that the weapon consumes souls, progressively corrupts its bearer, and can make harmful choices feel reasonable while they occur.

The party now knows in character that:

- The axe's corruption is active rather than hypothetical.
- It can override Rubius's judgment.
- It consumes victims killed by it.
- It returns to Rubius after being discarded.
- The Shadow Well is the only known lead for removing its power or separating it from Rubius.
- Dispel Magic can suppress an aspect temporarily but does not cleanse the artifact.

### 8. Alder Revealed His Patron's Demand

Alder told the group that his patron wants Rubius killed and the axe taken. He still did not provide the patron's name, describing the entity only as a bird-like figure. Alder also disclosed that his missing family heirloom lies in the Undershadow and that he possesses a mental sense of the distant route.

Alder attempted to have a shadow minion consume the axe as an offering to his patron. The axe consumed the minion instead and became empowered. Umbrail's repeated Dispel Magic prevented the resulting surge from forcing Rubius back into a rage.

### 9. The Party Passed the Black Fang at the Stone Stairs

At the edge of the forest, the party reached the Stone Stairs, a long switchback road into Frostvein's high territory. Smoke revealed the Black Fang column camped partway up the road.

The party considered engineering a rockslide but chose the safer strategic advantage: continue upward while the column rested. Umbrail secured Rubius's rope between ledges, Rubius helped the others climb, and the party reached the switchback above the Black Fang without detection.

### 10. The Party Entered the Frostvein Storm

The group continued to the plateau. Heavy wind, snow, and limited visibility replaced the pine forest almost immediately. Arctic wildlife moved through the storm, including polar bears in the high mountain terrain.

The recording ended there. The party had passed the Black Fang but had not reached Lil Chang, Da Long, An Yue, Rimehook, or Three-Bells Basin.
