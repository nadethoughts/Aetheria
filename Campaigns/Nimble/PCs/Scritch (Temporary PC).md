## Appearance
Scritch is a small black-scaled kobold with swept horns, bright amber eyes, and patched mountain clothing that has survived more owners and escapes than he is likely to admit. He carries rope, a rapier, a dagger, and the improvised tools of someone who expects to run before anyone checks his story too closely.

His horns and gear show fresh damage from hard travel. The overall impression is alert, cold, and betrayed rather than defeated.

## Back Story
Scritch first encountered the party near Clackston while trying to drag away a stolen mule. Rubius tackled and sat on him, after which Rowan questioned him about the livestock thefts. Scritch revealed the route to the Warren, and Rowan released him with a warning.

With nowhere else to go, Scritch returned to the Balshar Mountains and joined the kobolds serving Razor despite reservations about the dragon. He later traveled with the Black Fang diplomatic column. When an injured leg slowed him, the orcs treated him as dead weight and left him behind. He escaped during a rest, became lost in the pine forest, and took shelter beneath a discarded Black Fang blanket.

The party found him again in Session 08. Rowan healed him, and Scritch joined them for safety and revenge. He supplied intelligence about the Black Fang column and then fought beside the party when seven kobolds ambushed them.

## Personality
Scritch is opportunistic, charming when cornered, and painfully aware of how larger factions treat kobolds when they stop being useful. He wants protection, but he also wants kobolds to gain safe territory and respect rather than merely exchange one mountain ruler for another.

He meets danger with nervous humor and fast calculations. His prepared reaction to seeing the party again was: "The giant who sat on me. This is either very good luck or very bad luck."

## Notable Campaign Moments
- Was tackled and captured near Clackston while stealing a mule for the Warren.
- Gave the party directions to the kobold Warren and was released by Rowan.
- Returned to the mountains and joined Razor's kobolds because he had nowhere else to go.
- Traveled with the Black Fang delegation until an injury made him a liability.
- Escaped the column and was found beneath a discarded blanket in Session 08.
- Identified the column as five diplomats under Gorak and fifteen warriors influenced by Vekka.
- Warned that the delegation had a hidden purpose, though he did not know what it was.
- Killed an ambushing kobold with a sling stone, then used flanking and Cheat abilities to help finish the attackers.
- Helped the party pass the Black Fang camp unseen at the Stone Stairs.

## Current Hooks
- Scritch may know overlooked details about the Black Fang camp, equipment, route, or internal hierarchy.
- Razor's kobolds may brand him a deserter or try to recover him before he reveals more.
- He wants kobolds represented in any new mountain settlement or coalition.
- Rowan's earlier mercy gives Scritch a reason to trust the party, but not necessarily every faction they support.
- His ability to exploit distraction makes him valuable when the group needs infiltration rather than a direct fight.
