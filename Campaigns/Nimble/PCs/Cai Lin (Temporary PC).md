## Appearance
Cai Lin is a dark-haired orc woman with long braids, amber-brown eyes, small lower tusks, and blue ceremonial markings across her face. She dresses for Frostvein weather in layered blue cloth, leather, and fur rather than formal healer's robes.

Her working kit is always close at hand: dried herbs, small bottles, bandages, bells, and the ordinary tools needed to keep an injured climber alive. A golden birdlike spirit often manifests near her shoulder when her magic is active.

## Back Story
Cai Lin tends Frostvein's hearths and injured climbers. Her authority comes from doing the work that keeps people alive after avalanches, patrol accidents, bitter winters, and political decisions made by people who do not have to treat the wounded afterward.

She believes Razor must be resisted before the dragon's war reaches the settlement. That does not make her automatically loyal to the Erie, the Cloud Seers, or the visiting adventurers. Generations of Erie condescension taught Frostvein to be suspicious of alliances that ask neutral people to accept danger while someone else claims command.

Cai Lin therefore judges every faction by conduct rather than rhetoric. Her central question is simple: who will still protect Frostvein when helping becomes costly?

## Personality
Cai Lin is compassionate without being soft-spoken or politically naive. She saves lives first and argues about allegiance afterward. Injured enemies, abandoned scouts, and civilians caught between factions all matter to her, but she has little patience for leaders who use mercy as an excuse to avoid hard commitments.

When roleplayed as a temporary PC, she should keep the group attentive to consequences. She notices who is cold, wounded, hungry, or being treated as expendable.

## Prepared Campaign Role
- Represents the civilian and healing concerns of Frostvein during coalition negotiations.
- Supports resistance to Razor if the proposed alliance protects Frostvein people in deeds rather than promises.
- Expects both the Cloud Seers and Lil Chang to place Frostvein lives ahead of winning an argument.
- Is the person Feng Rui is sworn to protect, giving their temporary-PC builds a direct relationship.
- Can make the cost of war concrete through wounded climbers, depleted remedies, and hard choices about limited healing.

## Current Hooks
- Frostvein may need immediate medical aid before its leaders will entertain a military agreement.
- Cai Lin can test the party by asking them to protect someone politically inconvenient.
- Her Lifebinding Spirit may recognize a disturbance connected to the Undershadow or the shadow axe.
- A shortage of herbs, bandages, or safe gathering routes could force the coalition to prove its usefulness.

## Open Questions
- Who taught Cai Lin to call the golden spirit that accompanies her healing?
- Did she lose anyone because an Erie or orc leader valued strategy over rescue?
- How close are Cai Lin and Feng Rui beyond his oath as her devoted protector?
- What specific action would convince her that the coalition deserves Frostvein's trust?
