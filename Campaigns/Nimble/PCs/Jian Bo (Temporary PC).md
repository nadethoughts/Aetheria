## Appearance
Jian Bo is a heavily built orc with a trimmed black beard, short hair, and a measuring stare that makes every visitor feel briefly inventoried. He wears thick blue-and-fur mountain clothing and carries a stone-headed greatmaul.

A coil of heavy rope rests over one shoulder. Behind him are the barrels, sacks, hooks, and ledgers of a settlement that survives because someone knows exactly what remains in storage.

## Back Story
Jian Bo keeps Frostvein fed, equipped, and moving. He understands the mountain as a chain of material problems: grain must arrive before snow closes the route, damaged tools need metal, patrols need rope, and wounded workers consume supplies whether the council has finished debating or not.

He is willing to hear the Black Fang offer before choosing a side. That willingness is not secret sympathy for Razor. Jian Bo simply refuses to exchange a real storehouse for an inspiring promise. Any alliance must explain what food, metal, labor, routes, and mutual defense it will provide before winter.

His physical strength makes him useful when logistics become rescue. He can break a blocked route, haul someone from an avalanche, or stand where a collapsing line needs one person who will not move.

## Personality
Jian Bo is blunt, skeptical, and materially minded. His prepared challenge is, "Honor does not fill a storehouse. What arrives before the first deep snow?"

He respects courage but distrusts waste. Rage, for him, is not theatrical fury; it is the force used when something heavy must move and failure will cost lives.

## Prepared Campaign Role
- Represents Frostvein's stores, labor, equipment, and winter-readiness concerns.
- Is willing to compare the coalition and Black Fang offers on practical terms.
- Fears a prolonged war that consumes supplies faster than allies can replace them.
- Can support resistance if it includes enforceable commitments for routes, supplies, labor, and mutual defense.
- Provides a durable temporary PC for rescue work and frontline combat.

## Current Hooks
- Frostvein may already lack a critical winter supply that one faction can provide.
- A blocked storehouse, avalanche route, or damaged lift could require immediate action.
- Jian Bo may demand named quantities and delivery dates before accepting an alliance.
- Evidence that the Black Fang intend to seize rather than trade for supplies would move him decisively against them.
