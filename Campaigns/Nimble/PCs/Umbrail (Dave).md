# Umbrail

Dave

## Quick Details
- **Campaign**: Nimble
- **Class**: Mage
- **Ancestry**: Birdfolk, specifically tied to the Erie of the Balshar mountains
- **Current Level**: 2, likely approaching level 3 after the ruined Erie outpost
- **Background Thread**: Took the "bad choice" background; the curse, enemy, or owed consequence is still open
- **Current Arc**: The party has reached Umbrail's homeland and found one of his old Erie outposts destroyed

## Appearance
Umbrail is a large, elder proto-raven birdfolk with graying feathers, a bright white beak, and shimmering robes that seem to have sigils woven through them. He carries himself like someone old enough to know how badly things can go, and sharp-eyed enough to see them going that way before everyone else.

At the party's first meeting, his eyes were constantly moving, measuring exits, strangers, danger, and probably the quality of the pay.

## Back Story
Umbrail is one of a dwindling people from the high mountains. He left home to see the world before the end, or before his own end, or before the Erie became too few for the world to remember.

The choice was reasonable. He believes that. He may even still believe it was the correct choice. But it did not free him from regret. Umbrail misses home, misses his people, and worries that leaving may have sentenced some of them to die without him when they needed him most.

That regret has become the emotional center of the Mountains of Unusual Scale arc. As the party followed black-scaled kobolds and allied orcs into the Balshar range, Umbrail recognized the terrain, the threats, and the signs that his people were in danger. At the ruined Erie outpost, he found the body of his old magical mentor among the dead.

## Personality
Umbrail is wary, clever, and dryly practical. He is not eager to open old wounds, especially if those wounds have feathers, family names, or mountain oaths attached to them.

He is also a true arcane specialist. He deciphered the Old Tongue inscription outside the Warren, understood magical resonance in the trapped corridor, and is the party's natural reader of ancient, dangerous, or half-broken magic.

## Notable Campaign Moments
- Identified the Old Tongue inscription warning against the thing that drinks the light.
- Snatched one of the orichalcum crest fragments with his beak after the entrance fight.
- Used Overload after a failed lightning spell left him Charged, wiping out a cluster of kobolds.
- Read the resonance plates in the trapped corridor and guided the party through their rhythm.
- Helped Haggard work on freeing the magically trapped livestock.
- Became increasingly tense as the party climbed closer to his Erie homeland.
- Found his old magical mentor dead at the ruined Erie outpost.

## Current Hooks
- Umbrail must decide whether he returns to the Erie as kin, exile, witness, or outsider with strange friends.
- His mentor has not been named yet; possible prep names include Master Corveth Greyplume, Soryn Windscribe, or Vaelis Ashquill.
- The Erie may know him, his family, or his teacher, but not all of them will welcome him.
- The dragon Razor is threatening the old mountain balance between Erie, orcs, kobolds, griffin guardians, and older powers below.
- His "bad choice" background still needs its consequence: curse, enemy, stolen item, owed debt, or magic that wants repayment.

## Open Questions
- What was the first lesson Umbrail's master taught him that he actually believed?
- Did Umbrail leave because he was afraid, curious, unwanted, or needed elsewhere?
- What did he take when he left home, and who still remembers?
- What exactly did the "bad choice" cost him?
