# Rowan Hearthboro

## Player
Ramsey

## Quick Details
- **Campaign**: Nimble
- **Class**: Oathsworn
- **Ancestry**: Bun Bun
- **Current Level**: 2, likely approaching level 3 after the ruined Erie outpost
- **Utility Cantrip**: Bond of Peace
- **Role**: Community protector, moral frame, and shield-bearing rescuer

## Appearance
Rowan is a Bun Bun, a rabbit person with dappled white and brown fur. He wears battered mail that has become overgrown with plants, a straw hat, and a carrot-shaped pipe.

He looks like a farmer who became a knight without entirely admitting that he stopped being a farmer.

## Back Story
Rowan was a farmer and local protector before he became an adventurer. He was the sort of person who answered when the community needed someone, and over time he realized that call did not end at the edge of his own fields.

He left only after training a successor, making sure the home he protected would not be left undefended. His regret is the opposite of many of his companions': Rowan wishes he had left earlier. The world was hurting, especially after the [[Flaming Retribution]], and he wonders whether he could have done more good if he had stepped beyond his relatively sheltered life sooner.

Now that he has seen more people, more places, and more ways of living, he appreciates the wider world and regrets not meeting it before disaster forced the question.

## Personality
Rowan is steady, protective, and community-minded. He does not need to be the loudest person in the room to become the person others orient around when a hard decision needs a conscience.

In combat, Rowan is built to put himself between danger and someone more vulnerable. Out of combat, he is just as useful as the one who asks what duty requires when the easy answer is to walk away.

## Notable Campaign Moments
- Sized up the odd posse from under his straw hat during the tavern meeting.
- Identified the black scales as kobold in origin with a strong Nature Craft check.
- Interrogated the captured kobold and extracted directions to the Warren.
- Used manacles to secure the captured kobold before releasing it with a warning.
- Used Interpose to protect Whisper from Umbrail's Overload.
- Went down in the animal pen fight and was revived with Alder's Caustic Brew.

## Current Hooks
- Rowan is a natural voice for honoring the Erie dead, warning survivors, and protecting bystanders.
- His Bond of Peace may matter in the griffin scene if the party chooses calming over killing.
- Erie elders, Cloud Seer orcs, and wounded scouts all give Rowan people to protect and choices to frame.
- His regret about leaving late can play against Umbrail's and Whisper's regret about leaving at all.

## Open Questions
- What oath did Rowan swear, and to whom?
- Who did Rowan train as his successor before leaving home?
- What community does he still feel responsible for?
- What would make Rowan decide protection requires violence instead of mercy?
