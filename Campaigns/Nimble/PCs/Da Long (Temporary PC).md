## Appearance
Da Long is a lean, weather-hardened orc with long black hair, braided feathers, and sweeping blue Cloud Seer markings across his face and neck. He wears layered blue riding cloth and feathered mountain gear designed to move with the wind rather than fight it.

Quickfeather's pale head and watchful golden eye are rarely far behind him. Even at rest, Da Long looks like a rider listening for a change in the air.

## Back Story
Da Long is a Cloud Seer scout bonded to Quickfeather. While observing Black Fang movement through the eastern Balshar Mountains, he discovered a diplomatic column traveling toward the neutral Frostvein settlement to recruit support for Razor.

The Black Fang noticed him before he could escape and shot him from the sky. Quickfeather found Lil Chang and brought the orc to the rocky outcrop where Da Long lay without breath. Lil Chang used a healing potion and chest compressions to revive him.

Da Long immediately repaid that rescue with intelligence and a route forward. He warned the party about the Black Fang column, described its size and destination, and directed the land party toward a hidden game trail. He then carried Lil Chang ahead on Quickfeather to warn Frostvein before the delegation arrived.

## Personality
Da Long is direct, proud, and unwilling to hide an uncomfortable truth behind politeness. His prepared negotiating line captures him well: "I was spying. I will not insult you by pretending otherwise. Razor's people shot first."

He wants his warning to matter, wants Quickfeather protected, and feels a personal debt to Lil Chang. At the same time, he does not want the Cloud Seers reduced to scouts and mounts for someone else's coalition.

## Notable Campaign Moments
- Was shot down while scouting a Black Fang diplomatic column in Session 07.
- Was carried to the party's vicinity by Quickfeather while unconscious and without breath.
- Was revived by Lil Chang with a healing potion and chest compressions.
- Warned that the Black Fang were traveling toward Frostvein with approximately fifteen to twenty orcs.
- Gave the land party directions to a secret mountain trail that allowed them to follow and bypass the column.
- Took Lil Chang ahead on Quickfeather to warn An Yue and the Frostvein settlement.
- Remained offscreen but ahead of the party throughout Session 08; that session did not show his arrival.
- Reached [[World/Locations/Icehold|Icehold]] with Lil Chang and honestly admitted that he had been spying on the Black Fang.
- Gave An Yue's council the location of overlooked hunting grounds as proof of good faith.
- Agreed that he could not bind Cloud Seer or Erie leadership and planned to seek a formally empowered delegation.

## Current Hooks
- Da Long must explain his spying without letting the Black Fang turn it into proof of Cloud Seer aggression.
- His debt to Lil Chang may conflict with orders from Cloud Seer leaders.
- Quickfeather can enable an aerial rescue, pursuit, or race against the Black Fang delegation.
- Da Long wants Frostvein in the resistance but will defend Cloud Seer honor and freedom of action.
- Frostvein expects him to be present when the Black Fang delegation makes its case.
