## Appearance
Rubius is enormous even before anyone remembers he is a half-giant: about thirteen feet tall, with deep red hair, emerald eyes, and a friendly, jolly presence that makes the size difference feel less threatening until combat starts.

He tends to read as warm before he reads as dangerous. In the tavern at [[Clackston]], he made his first impression by enthusiastically drinking from a barrel of ale while the rest of the new posse tried to decide what they had gotten themselves into.

## Back Story
Rubius started adventuring because he is trying to prove that he is not like the giants. He is searching ruins for clues about a giant enclave that he believes must be found and stopped before it becomes a greater threat.

His greatest regret is tied to that mission. Rubius once discovered a ruin that might have held answers about the giants, but in the fight against the site's guardian or boss, he lost control of his own power and brought the chamber down. Whatever secrets could have helped him, his people, or the wider world were buried under the collapse.

That failure still defines him. He wants strength to mean protection rather than destruction, but his body keeps reminding him that one bad swing can erase the thing he meant to save.

## Personality
Rubius is cheerful, direct, and more tender than strangers expect. He has a clear affection for animals and took the stolen livestock in Warren's Want personally once the party found them trapped behind a magical field.

He is also the party's natural battering ram. When subtlety starts taking too long, Rubius is the one most likely to charge, lift, break, or tackle the problem. That instinct has saved the party more than once, but it is also exactly the part of himself he fears losing control over.

- Accepted two explicit bargains for escalating damage; the axe consumed the patrol veteran and became visibly more wicked.
- Ended Session 09 with five transcript-supported Shadow Points and one Wound. The written axe rules would add a sixth point for the Wound, but that award was not announced.

## Current Hooks
- The shadow axe presents itself as comforting, safe, and meant for him.
- The axe currently deals 3d4 as a battleaxe and has whispered, "Welcome friend, let's drink the light together."
- The axe consumes the essence of creatures killed by it, can make its impulses feel reasonable, and can turn Rubius's rage against allies.
- The axe now offers explicit bargains in exchange for Shadow Points; Rubius has accepted two.
- Tempest Command and repeated Dispel Magic can temporarily suppress an active surge, but they do not cleanse the weapon.
- Rubius still seeks old ruins and clues connected to giants.
- The Shadow Well and Undershadow threads may become the only way to unmake or drain the axe's power.
