# Nimble Character Spell Card Print List

## Source PDFs

Use:

- [[System Info/Nimble/SpellCards.pdf|Nimble SpellCards.pdf]] for combat spells.
- [[output/pdf/Nimble Utility Spell Cards.pdf|Nimble Utility Spell Cards.pdf]] for utility spells.

Page numbers below are each PDF's one-based page numbers as displayed by a PDF reader, not spell tiers or printed card numbers.

The PDF contains spell cards on pages 1-56. Pages 57-77 are potion and item cards, and pages 78-96 are action-tracking cards.

> [!note] Card-title difference
> PDF page 50 is titled **Shadowy Trap**. The current rules and temporary-character build notes call the same spell **Shadow Trap**.
