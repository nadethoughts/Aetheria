## Appearance
Alder is a shroomling with a pale white body, a large brown mushroom cap, tiny black bead-like eyes, and an introverted manner that makes civilized spaces feel like they are happening too loudly around him.

He presents as rural and practical: shovel, food concerns, farm instincts, and the air of someone who would rather be near soil than surrounded by tavern noise.

## Back Story
Alder started adventuring because the problem in [[Clackston]] was his problem too. He is a farmer or homesteader whose own sheep and goats were being stolen, and he took the livestock thefts personally.

His greatest regret is losing a family heirloom given to him when he set off to homestead. The heirloom was an artifact tied to food production: it could vastly speed rot and breakdown, making food processing faster and easier. Without it, Alder has had to do things the old-fashioned way, and the loss still gnaws at him.

By the current prep, that lost heirloom has become more than a missing tool. Alder's patron suggests it is somewhere below, tied to the Undershadow, and the same deep shadow current may connect to the cursed axe now carried by Haggard.

## Personality
Alder is nervous, inward, and quietly strange, but not helpless. He thinks like a farmer and problem-solver first, then like a shadow-touched spellcaster second.

He is also more useful under pressure than he looks. In the Warren, he read steam vents, made torches, handled dangerous potions, and sent shadow magic into close quarters while everyone else was busy being stabbed by kobolds.

- Helped track signs of the livestock thefts around [[Clackston]].
- Summoned a tentacled shadow minion and used Shadow Blast in the entrance fight.
- Predicted the timing of the steam bursts in the trapped corridor with a natural 20.
- Administered Caustic Brew to Rowan after Rowan went down in the animal pen fight.
- May currently be holding the Flamecaller's Talisman, though the party still needs to confirm.
- Communed with [[World/Religion/Deities/Erabose|Erabose]] at the ruined Erie outpost; the patron remains displeased about an earlier unfulfilled command but still considers Alder useful.
- Was attacked by Rubius when the Axe of Snaggletooth turned the berserker's rage indiscriminate.
- Told the party that his patron wants Rubius killed and the axe taken, and that his lost heirloom lies in the Undershadow.
- Tried to sacrifice the axe through a shadow minion; the axe consumed the minion and became stronger instead.

## Current Hooks
- Alder's patron wants Haggard killed and the axe taken; the whole party now knows this demand.
- Alder says his lost heirloom lies somewhere in the Undershadow and that he has a mental sense of the distant route.
- The axe may feel to Alder's patron like grit in a wound: not the same power, but close enough to offend.
- The Shadow Well rumor points toward deep places beneath Balshar, possibly near dragonflight burial tunnels or an Undershadow breach.
- Alder has a personal reason to follow the dark route even if the dragon arc pulls the party upward.

## Open Questions

- What is the name and exact form of the lost rot-heirloom?
- Did Alder lose the heirloom through carelessness, theft, betrayal, or something worse?
- How much of Alder's magic comes from his people, his patron, or the heirloom's absence?
