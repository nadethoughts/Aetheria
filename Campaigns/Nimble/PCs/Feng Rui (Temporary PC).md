## Appearance
Feng Rui is a broad, weathered orc with close-cropped black hair, a scar crossing his face, and the guarded expression of someone who has spent years deciding who may enter a dangerous pass. His heavy armor is scratched, rust-stained, and practical rather than ceremonial.

He carries a round shield and dresses for active duty in snow and stone. Nothing about him suggests that rank has moved him far from patrol work.

## Back Story
Feng Rui commands Frostvein's northern pass wardens. His first duty is not to a distant orc coalition or to the old authority of the Erie; it is to the settlement and the people who depend on Frostvein controlling its own approaches.

He begins from neutrality and closed borders. Outsiders have a habit of describing mountain roads as strategic assets while forgetting that those roads pass through someone's home. Feng Rui will resist any plan that turns Frostvein into a battlefield, supply corridor, or buffer without its consent.

His oath as Cai Lin's devoted protector gives that political position a personal center. He knows who treats the wounded when proud leaders misjudge the cost of a campaign.

## Personality
Feng Rui is disciplined, suspicious, and economical with words. He asks direct questions and expects concrete answers: "Name what you want from our pass - and what you will give when winter closes behind you."

He is not opposed to cooperation. He is opposed to cooperation that quietly becomes occupation. In combat he protects the formation, moves allies out of danger, and treats survival as the measure of a commander's competence.

## Prepared Campaign Role
- Represents Frostvein's wardens and its authority over the northern passes.
- Begins negotiations from a position of neutrality, restricted access, and closed borders.
- Can support an alliance if An Yue's control of the passes is explicitly respected.
- Gives the party a military ally whose priority is defense rather than conquest.
- Protects Cai Lin personally and will judge others by whether they endanger or support her work.
