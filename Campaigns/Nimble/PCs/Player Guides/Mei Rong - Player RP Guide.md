# Mei Rong - Player Roleplay Guide

> [!info] Player-facing handout
> This is everything you need to portray Mei Rong during the Frostvein negotiation. You do not need to memorize it or read the full campaign notes.

## Your Character in One Sentence

You preserve Frostvein's history, and you will help resist Razor only if the new alliance recognizes Frostvein's independence instead of rebuilding the unjust old mountain order.

## Background

You are one of Frostvein's memory keepers. You preserve treaties, betrayals, migrations, disasters, and the names that disappear when powerful people tell a cleaner version of history.

You believe Razor is dangerous. You also know that fear can persuade people to restore an old hierarchy without asking whether that hierarchy was just. The Erie and other powers have treated Frostvein as lesser, useful, or strategically convenient before.

You are not asking the settlement to ignore the present threat. You are insisting that survival should create a fairer future rather than excuse the past.

## Disposition and Voice

- Patient, incisive, and comfortable with silence.
- Listen for the part of history a speaker leaves out.
- Name old harm without turning every conversation into punishment.
- Support courage and unity when they are built on honest recognition.
- Ask what story the proposed agreement will tell after the war.

**Voice line:** "We can resist Razor without pretending the old order was just."

## What You Want from the Negotiation

1. Formal recognition of Frostvein independence and territorial rights.
2. A genuine hearing for Frostvein's grievances.
3. Resistance to Razor without restoring Erie rule over the mountain tribes.
4. An agreement whose terms can be remembered publicly and enforced later.

You can support a conditional alliance: local defense, guides, intelligence, volunteers, and representatives sent to negotiate broader terms. Full commitment should follow explicit recognition of Frostvein's status.

## Your Red Lines

- No language implying that Frostvein returns to Erie authority.
- No demand that past grievances be postponed forever in the name of unity.
- No secret agreement whose terms can later be denied.
- No treating Razor's threat as proof that every old Frostvein complaint was mistaken.
- No bargain that erases kobolds or smaller factions from the mountain's future.

## What You Know

- The Erie and Cloud Seers oppose Razor, but their alliance does not settle Frostvein's relationship with them.
- The Black Fang serve Razor and are approaching as diplomats under a peace banner.
- Frostvein's history gives it reasons to distrust both domination and isolation.
- The visiting party cannot honestly promise everything distant Erie leaders will accept.
- A limited alliance and formal delegation may be more truthful than an immediate permanent commitment.

## Player Aid: Listen for the Missing Clause

Whenever someone proposes an agreement, ask:

1. **Whose authority does this assume?**
2. **Whose harm does this leave unnamed?**
3. **What happens after Razor is defeated?**

Your job is not merely to object. Add the missing clause that could make the agreement worthy of support.

## Player Aid: Ways to Shape the Room

- Restate an opponent's strongest fair point before answering it.
- Ask that a promise be spoken publicly and recorded in the settlement's memory.
- Offer a story about a past alliance that succeeded—or failed—for the same reason.
- Invite Umbrail or another Erie voice to acknowledge a true grievance.
- Frame compromise as the beginning of a better tradition, not a surrender.

## If You Need an Opening Statement

"Razor's danger is real. So is the history that brought us to this table. If you want Frostvein beside you after the war, write an agreement that still respects us when fear is no longer doing the persuading."

## If the Scene Turns Dangerous

Use Inspiration and your presence to keep the group functioning. Your Songweaver abilities reward supporting several allies, creating temporary HP, and turning a rest or pause into renewed resolve.
