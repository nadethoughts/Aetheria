# Scritch - Player Roleplay Guide

> [!info] Player-facing handout
> This is everything you need to portray Scritch during the Frostvein negotiation. The character-sheet and ability-card assets spell his name **Skritch**, but the campaign recording uses **Scritch**.

## Your Character in One Sentence

You are a clever kobold survivor abandoned by the Black Fang, and you want protection, revenge, and a future where kobolds are not disposable servants for whichever powerful faction wins.

## Background

The party first caught you near Clackston while you were stealing a mule for the kobold Warren. Rubius tackled and sat on you. Rowan questioned you, learned the route to the Warren, and let you go.

With nowhere else to go, you returned to the Balshar Mountains and joined kobolds serving Razor. You later traveled with the Black Fang diplomatic column. When an injured leg slowed you down, the orcs treated you as dead weight. You escaped during a rest and became lost in the forest.

The party found you again. Rowan healed you, and you joined them for safety and a chance to repay the Black Fang for leaving you behind. You fought beside the party during a kobold ambush and helped it pass the Black Fang camp unseen.

## Disposition and Voice

- Alert, opportunistic, funny when frightened, and always watching for the safest exit.
- You want protection but hate being treated as property or baggage.
- Offer useful details to prove your value, but do not pretend to know more than you do.
- Distrust grand promises made on behalf of kobolds who are not in the room.
- You can be brave when someone else has first shown you loyalty.

**Voice line:** "The giant who sat on me. This is either very good luck or very bad luck."

## What You Want from the Negotiation

1. Keep the Black Fang from reclaiming, silencing, or abandoning you again.
2. Make your intelligence valuable enough that Frostvein protects you.
3. Win recognition that kobolds need safe territory and a voice of their own.
4. Expose any hidden Black Fang purpose—but only when evidence supports it.

You can support cooperation against Razor. You do not want kobolds to trade one ruler for another or become acceptable casualties in someone else's resistance.

## Your Red Lines

- Do not return to Black Fang custody without an extraordinary guarantee.
- Do not let negotiators dismiss all kobolds as traps, monsters, or servants.
- Do not invent proof. If caught lying, you lose the one thing keeping you safe: usefulness.
- Do not volunteer for danger unless you gain protection, leverage, or a real chance to help someone who protected you.

## What You Know

- The Black Fang column has twenty orcs: five diplomats and fifteen warriors.
- Gorak Broken-Tusk leads the diplomats.
- Quartermaster Vekka Ash-Eye has influence over the warriors.
- They travel under a peace banner toward Frostvein.
- Their stated goal is a treaty, but you sensed that something else was happening.
- You did not witness spellcasting or open conflict inside the column.
- You do **not** know the secret objective and should say so clearly.

## Player Aid: Fact, Suspicion, Bargain

Divide what you say into three categories:

- **Fact:** Something you personally saw or heard.
- **Suspicion:** Your best guess, labeled honestly as a guess.
- **Bargain:** What you want in exchange for taking a risk or revealing more.

This lets Scritch be slippery without becoming unreliable to the group.

## Player Aid: Useful Ways to Participate

- Correct anyone who gets the column's numbers or leadership wrong.
- Point out behaviors that fit or contradict what you saw while traveling with them.
- Ask what protection Frostvein offers witnesses and deserters.
- Push for kobolds to receive safe surrender terms or representation.
- Watch Vekka and the guards while everyone else watches the diplomats.
- Break tension with nervous humor, then deliver one uncomfortable fact.

## If You Need an Opening Statement

"They say treaty. Maybe they mean treaty. They also brought fifteen warriors, left me in the cold, and were hiding something from the likes of me. I know what I saw. I know what I only suspect. Protection first, and I will tell you which is which."

## If the Scene Turns Dangerous

Do not fight fairly. Hide, flank, and strike enemies distracted by tougher allies. Your Cheat abilities reward turning an opening into a critical hit and escaping before someone larger decides you are the easiest target.
