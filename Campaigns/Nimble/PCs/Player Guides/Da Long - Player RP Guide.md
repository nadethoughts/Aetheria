# Da Long - Player Roleplay Guide

> [!info] Player-facing handout
> This is everything you need to portray Da Long during the Frostvein negotiation. You do not need to memorize it or read the full campaign notes.

## Your Character in One Sentence

You are a proud Cloud Seer griffon scout who was shot down while uncovering the Black Fang mission, and you need Frostvein to hear the warning without treating your spying as proof that the Cloud Seers want control.

## Background

You are bonded to Quickfeather, a large griffon and your closest companion. While scouting Black Fang movement, you found a delegation of roughly twenty orcs traveling toward Frostvein. They detected you and shot you from the sky.

Quickfeather found Lil Chang, who revived you with a healing potion and chest compressions. You warned the party, directed its landbound members toward a hidden route, and carried Lil Chang ahead toward Frostvein.

You owe Lil Chang your life. You also need your warning to matter. The Cloud Seers oppose Razor, but you do not want them reduced to mounts, scouts, or subordinates in someone else's coalition.

## Disposition and Voice

- Direct, disciplined, and proud.
- Admit uncomfortable facts before an opponent can weaponize them.
- Protect Quickfeather and defend Cloud Seer honor.
- You are more comfortable reporting what you saw than making promises for political leaders.
- Respect courage and honest disagreement; dislike evasive half-truths.

**Voice line:** "I was spying. I will not insult you by pretending otherwise. Razor's people shot first."

## What You Want from the Negotiation

1. Convince Frostvein that the Black Fang mission deserves close scrutiny.
2. Bring Frostvein into resistance against Razor without claiming authority over it.
3. Preserve the Cloud Seers' honor and freedom of action.
4. Repay Lil Chang by making his dangerous flight ahead worthwhile.

You can support a conditional alliance that keeps each tribe responsible for its own territory while sharing guides, warnings, intelligence, and volunteers.

## Your Red Lines

- Do not fabricate evidence or claim to know the delegation's secret purpose.
- Do not apologize for warning Frostvein, though you can acknowledge that spying creates legitimate suspicion.
- Do not promise Cloud Seer troops, territory, or submission without authority.
- Do not allow Quickfeather to be treated as equipment or expendable transport.

## What You Know

- The Black Fang column contains roughly fifteen to twenty orcs and is heading toward Frostvein.
- They noticed your surveillance and shot you down.
- They travel as a diplomatic group; a peace banner still matters.
- The land party has information from Scritch about five diplomats and fifteen warriors.
- You know secret routes through the mountains and can speak credibly about movement, timing, and terrain.
- You do **not** know the delegation's hidden objective.

## Player Aid: Report, Interpret, Recommend

When speaking, separate three things:

- **Report:** What you personally saw.
- **Interpret:** What you think it may mean.
- **Recommend:** What Frostvein should do next.

This makes Da Long sound honest and prevents suspicion from becoming an unsupported accusation.

## Player Aid: Questions to Ask

- Why does a diplomatic column need that many warriors?
- What does the Black Fang offer Frostvein in exchange for support?
- Will both sides accept Frostvein scouts observing their forces and supplies?
- What guarantee prevents an allied army from remaining after Razor is defeated?
- Can the parties agree to investigate before anyone violates the peace banner?

## If You Need an Opening Statement

"I saw the Black Fang column moving toward you. They saw me watching and put me in the snow. That proves hostility toward me, not every purpose they carry. Hear their offer if you must—but watch their hands while they make it."

## If the Scene Turns Dangerous

Mark the most important enemy and coordinate with Quickfeather. You are strongest when you identify a quarry, stay mobile, and let your companion protect or reposition you.
