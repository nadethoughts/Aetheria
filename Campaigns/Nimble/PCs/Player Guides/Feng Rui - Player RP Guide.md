# Feng Rui - Player Roleplay Guide

> [!info] Player-facing handout
> This is everything you need to portray Feng Rui during the Frostvein negotiation. You do not need to memorize it or read the full campaign notes.

## Your Character in One Sentence

You command Frostvein's northern pass wardens, and you will resist Razor only through an agreement that leaves Frostvein—not foreign armies—in control of its own borders.

## Background

You have spent years deciding who may enter dangerous Frostvein territory and who must turn back. Outsiders call the passes strategic routes. You call them home, responsibility, and the difference between safety and an army marching through the settlement.

You begin from neutrality and closed borders. That does not mean you trust Razor or the Black Fang. It means every faction must ask permission before bringing its conflict onto Frostvein land.

You are Cai Lin's devoted protector. She treats the people harmed by military mistakes, and her safety and judgment matter deeply to you.

## Disposition and Voice

- Controlled, guarded, and difficult to impress with speeches.
- Ask for authority, boundaries, numbers, and enforcement.
- Respect competence and people who take responsibility for consequences.
- Prefer a limited agreement that can be enforced over a sweeping promise that cannot.
- You are not trying to stop all cooperation; you are trying to keep cooperation from becoming occupation.

**Voice line:** "Name what you want from our pass—and what you will give when winter closes behind you."

## What You Want from the Negotiation

1. Preserve Frostvein control over every pass, patrol, and foreign force inside its territory.
2. Keep your wardens and Cai Lin alive.
3. Learn whether either delegation respects limits when those limits are inconvenient.
4. Establish a defensive agreement without surrendering political independence.

You can accept Frostvein defending its territory, sharing intelligence, providing guides, or sending limited volunteers. Foreign troops enter only with permission and leave when that permission ends.

## Your Red Lines

- No permanent foreign garrison.
- No army moving through Frostvein without local authorization and escorts.
- No agreement that treats the passes as coalition property.
- No exposing Cai Lin, civilians, or wardens to a danger that outsiders refuse to share.
- No attack beneath a peace banner without clear evidence of immediate danger.

## What You Know

- Razor threatens the mountain balance, but Frostvein has not committed to either coalition.
- The Black Fang serve Razor and are approaching under a peace banner.
- The party and Cloud Seers want Frostvein to join the resistance.
- Frostvein guides and passes are valuable enough that every side has a reason to flatter or pressure you.
- An Yue has final political authority; your job is to make sure any agreement can actually be defended.

## Player Aid: The Border Test

Evaluate every proposal with three checks:

1. **Control:** Who decides who enters and when they leave?
2. **Cost:** Which Frostvein people and resources are exposed?
3. **Enforcement:** What happens if an ally breaks the agreement?

If a proposal cannot answer all three, demand revisions rather than rejecting negotiation outright.

## Player Aid: Useful Moves in the Conversation

- Convert a broad promise into a limited, testable commitment.
- Ask both sides to accept the same restriction.
- Offer guides or intelligence before offering troops.
- Ask Cai Lin what civilian protection requires.
- Ask Jian Bo whether the proposed support is materially possible.
- Say, "I could agree to that if..." and name one enforceable condition.

## If You Need an Opening Statement

"Frostvein is not a road between your army and your enemy. If you want our passes, begin by recognizing that they remain ours before, during, and after this war."

## If the Scene Turns Dangerous

Hold the line and move allies into safer positions. Your Commander abilities are strongest when you protect the formation, issue clear orders, and keep Cai Lin from being isolated.
