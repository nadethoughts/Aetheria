## Appearance
Mei Rong is an older orc woman with silver threaded through her long braided hair, blue markings across her face and throat, and the composed bearing of someone accustomed to speaking while a whole council listens. Beads and carved metal pieces record affiliations, memories, or stories in a form outsiders may not know how to read.

She carries a broad hand drum marked with a wind spiral. Pale currents curl around her when her songs give old words new force.

## Back Story
Mei Rong preserves Frostvein's oral histories. She remembers treaties from the promises made, the promises omitted, and the people who paid when those distinctions mattered.

She considers Razor dangerous and believes the dragon must be resisted. She will not support a resistance that uses the emergency to restore Erie dominance or dismiss the injuries Frostvein suffered under the old mountain order. To Mei Rong, forgetting that history would not create unity; it would guarantee the same conflict returns under a different banner.

Her preferred alliance is one that recognizes Frostvein independence and grants its grievances a formal hearing. Resistance to Razor must build something more just than what came before.

## Personality
Mei Rong is patient, incisive, and difficult to rush. She listens for the story a speaker is trying to make everyone accept, then supplies the missing names and consequences.

Her guiding line is, "We can resist Razor without pretending the old order was just." When played as a temporary PC, she should preserve morale while asking who controls the version of events that survives.

## Prepared Campaign Role
- Represents Frostvein's memory, identity, and historical case for independence.
- Can explain the settlement's grievances against the Erie and other mountain powers.
- Supports resistance to Razor if it does not recreate the old hierarchy.
- Can use history and song to turn a hostile negotiation toward mutual recognition.
- Provides magical support, recovery, and broad knowledge during an expedition.

## Current Hooks
- Mei Rong may know an older verse about Razor, the dragonflight burial tunnels, or the Shadow Well.
- A disputed treaty or boundary marker could prove Frostvein's claim to the passes.
- The Erie may remember the same historical event very differently.
- She can demand that the coalition publicly record Frostvein's independence as the price of support.
