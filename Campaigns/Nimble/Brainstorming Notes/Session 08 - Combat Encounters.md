# Encounter 1 - Recover Scritch

## Purpose

An easy, fast skirmish that introduces the kobold splinter cell and gives Scritch a reason to trust the party. The retrieval team wants Scritch and its written orders. It does not stand and die for the objective.

## Base Roster - Easy

| Fictional role | Nimble creature | Qty. | Level each | Total |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Rock Scale retriever | [[Kobold Sneak]] | 5 | 1/2 | 2.5 |
| Alchemist | [[Kobold Trapper]] | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Rigger | [[Kobold Clanger]] | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| **Total** |  | **7** |  | **4.5** |

This is below the nominal easy threshold, but the Clanger's attack bonus and the Trapper's automatic damage make it meaningful. Its short duration is intentional.

## Battlefield - The Broken Creek Camp

Use a roughly 12-by-16-space clearing.

- **Scritch's shelter:** Half cover; contains the written orders.
- **Creek:** Difficult terrain. A creature shoved into it is Prone but gains cover from the bank.
- **Three pines:** Full cover from ranged attacks across the trunk.
- **Snare line:** Clearly visible after anyone searches or a kobold uses it. Crossing carelessly causes a DC 10 DEX save or Prone.
- **Alchemist's satchel:** An adjacent creature can spend 1 action to seize, throw, or destroy it.
- **Escape trail:** Kobolds reaching the northern edge leave the encounter with whatever they carry.

## Stat Blocks

### Rock Scale Retriever - Kobold Sneak

**Level 1/2 | Small | HP 15 | Armor none | Speed 6**

- **Revenge:** When an ally dies, move up to 6 spaces before using Nooooo!
- **Stab or Sling:** 1d4+2; Sling Range 8.
- **Kobold group rule - Nooooo!:** When an ally within 2 dies, attack once for free.

### Alchemist - Kobold Trapper

**Level 1 | Small | HP 26 | Armor none | Speed 6**

- **Throw Scorpion (2x):** Range 8, 1d4+2.
- **BEEES! (1/encounter):** When an enemy moves adjacent to the Alchemist or an ally, deal 5d4 damage that cannot miss; half to every adjacent creature.
- **Hidden Net (1/encounter):** When an enemy moves adjacent to the Alchemist or an ally, Restrained; escape DC 10.

Reskin **BEEES!** as a bursting acid-and-stinging-powder flask. Keep the damage unchanged.

### Rigger - Kobold Clanger

**Level 1 | Small | HP 16 | Heavy Armor | Speed 6**

- **CLANG!:** Allies who can hear the Rigger roll one additional die whenever they attack.

Reskin the armor as a reinforced climbing harness covered in metal tools. The rigger does not need an attack; it moves, protects the orders, and keeps CLANG! active.

## Enemy Plan

### Opening position

- Two Retrievers circle behind Scritch's shelter.
- Two remain in the trees with slings.
- One starts near the Alchemist.
- The Rigger begins near the escape trail.

### Round priorities

1. **Rigger:** Reach the shelter, seize the orders, then flee.
2. **Alchemist:** Net the hero closest to Scritch; use BEEES! only where it will not catch more than one kobold.
3. **Retrievers:** Grapple, shove, screen, and carry Scritch toward the escape trail. They are not trying to execute him.

### Morale

The team scatters when any two of these occur:

- The Alchemist drops to 0 HP.
- The Rigger drops to 0 HP.
- Three Retrievers drop to 0 HP.
- The orders are secured by the party and the escape route is blocked.
- A hero offers safe withdrawal while visibly controlling the field.

## Win States Other Than Defeat Everyone

- Keep Scritch from being carried off for 3 rounds.
- Secure the orders and allow the kobolds to flee.
- Capture the Alchemist or Rigger for questioning.
- Convince them that Scritch is not the only witness.
- Trade Scritch's silence for testimony about Quartermaster Vekka.

## Scaling

- **Softer:** Use 3 Retrievers. The Rigger flees as soon as the orders are lost.
- **Stronger easy:** Add 2 Retrievers. Total level 5.5.
- **Near-medium pursuit:** Add 2 [[Worg|Worgs]] waiting beyond the escape edge. Total level 6.5. The Worgs carry fugitives away rather than fighting to the death.

## Evidence

The orders say:

> Recover the witness. Burn the route notes. Report to Quartermaster Vekka—not the envoy.

This implicates Vekka in secrecy, not in an attack on Frostvein.

# Encounter 2 - Black Fang Outrider Patrol

## Purpose

The primary conventional fight. The patrol attempts capture under controlled force. Its members genuinely believe they are protecting a peaceful diplomatic mission.

## Base Roster - Easy/Medium Border

| Fictional role | Nimble creature | Qty. | Level each | Total |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Black Fang veteran | [[Bandit Captain]] | 1 | 4 | 4 |
| Black Fang scout | [[Bandit Hunter]] | 3 | 1 | 3 |
| War warg | [[Worg]] | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| **Total** |  | **6** |  | **9** |

This is below the nominal medium target of 11, but it has strong action economy: the Captain attacks three times, the Hunters hit hard at range, and each Worg attacks twice. Capture objectives also make the terrain more dangerous than the raw budget suggests.

## Fresh-Party Reinforcement - Medium

Add one **Black Fang shield-bearer** using [[Bandit Bruiser]] statistics.

**New total:** 11 monster levels.

Do this only if the heroes are fresh and have not already fought the kobold team.

## Battlefield - The Stone Stairs

Use a switchback trail with three elevations.

- **Lower stair:** Where the heroes enter.
- **Middle landing:** Eight spaces across with an ancient Erie marker providing cover.
- **Upper shelf:** Hunters begin here, 4 spaces above the landing.
- **Drop:** Each tier is 20 feet (4 spaces) lower. Falling one tier deals 2d6 under Nimble's normal 1d6-per-10-feet rule and leaves the creature Prone. A fall off the map is not instant death; it leaves the creature hanging from roots or a ledge and needing rescue.
- **Rope bridge:** Can be cut, loosened, pulled sideways, or used to swing between tiers.
- **Peace pennant:** The veteran carries a small white pennant identifying the patrol as part of the protected delegation.

## Stat Blocks

### Black Fang Veteran - Bandit Captain

**Level 4 | Medium | HP 36 | Medium Armor | Speed 6**

- **Slice (3x):** 1d8+1.
- May instead **Shoot:** Range 8, 1d8+1.

The veteran uses the flat of an axe, shield hooks, and pommel blows. Reaching 0 HP still means defeated; the nonlethal description changes intent, not damage rules.

### Black Fang Scout - Bandit Hunter

**Level 1 | Medium | HP 22 | Armor none | Speed 6**

- **Battlebow:** Range 12, 2d8+2.

Use barbed capture arrows trailing light cord. On a hit, the damage is unchanged. A nearby ally may use the cord fictionally to pull, anchor, or follow the target; do not add free Restrained unless an ability or successful action creates it.

### War Warg - Worg

**Level 1 | Large | HP 28 | Armor none | Speed 10**

- **Savage:** Always crits when attacking a Grappled creature.
- **Rip Apart (2x):** 1d6+2; on hit, Grappled, escape DC 10.

### Shield-Bearer - Bandit Bruiser

**Level 2 | Medium | HP 24 | Medium Armor | Speed 6**

- **Bash:** 2d8+4.

## Patrol Plan

### Warning before initiative

The veteran calls:

> “Weapons down. You are interfering with protected envoys. You will be bound, questioned, and released when our passage is secure.”

If the party talks, the patrol talks. Initiative begins only when someone commits to force, escape, or a dangerous deception.

### Round priorities

1. **Wargs:** Grapple isolated heroes and drag them toward the lower stair.
2. **Hunters:** Target ropes, escape lanes, and heroes threatening the veteran. They do not focus-fire a Dying hero.
3. **Veteran:** Uses three attacks to pressure several heroes rather than executing one.
4. **Shield-Bearer:** Blocks the narrowest stair and uses Bash to push enemies away from the Hunters.

## Capture Track

A hero is captured only when all three are true:

1. They are Grappled, Restrained, unconscious, or have surrendered.
2. A patrol member spends an action binding them.
3. The patrol gets them off the lower edge or holds the battlefield.

This gives the party time to intervene and makes capture a visible objective rather than a surprise consequence of reaching 0 HP.

## Morale and Negotiation

The patrol stops fighting if:

- The party produces credible evidence that someone inside the delegation is conducting an unauthorized operation.
- The veteran is Bloodied and a hero offers a face-saving truce.
- A larger predator threatens both groups.
- The peace pennant is endangered and both sides agree to retrieve it.

The veteran does **not** know the hidden objective. Under honest questioning, the patrol can reveal:

- Vekka ordered unusually wide scouting loops.
- The column carries rescue gear.
- Two outriders report to Vekka rather than Envoy Gorak.
- The guards were explicitly ordered to take interferers alive.

## Scaling

- **After the kobold fight:** Omit the Shield-Bearer. If the party is badly depleted, use 2 Hunters.
- **Fresh and confident party:** Add the Shield-Bearer for a true medium encounter.
- **Hard pursuit:** Add a second Shield-Bearer and one additional Hunter. Total level 14. Use only after clearly telegraphing the patrol's strength and offering escape routes.

# Encounter 3 - Grimbeak on the Wind Pass

## Purpose

An optional non-faction set piece and temporary-alliance catalyst. Use only if the session needs a major battle or the players deliberately enter the territorial nesting ground.

Use the official level-3 solo legendary owlbear [[Grimbeak, the Unyielding]]. This is already designed as a full encounter for level-3 heroes and does not require additional monsters.

## Why Grimbeak Attacks

Choose or infer one:

- A Black Fang pack animal carries an egg stolen by an outrider.
- A rockslide has blocked access to Grimbeak's cub.
- The delegation's smoke and bells have driven prey into the nest.
- Kobold trail markers accidentally lead both groups through her territory.

Grimbeak is territorial, not evil. Returning the egg, freeing the cub, leaving the ridge, or driving away the pack animals can end the fight.

## Battlefield - The Wind Pass

- **Central ridge:** 4 spaces wide; normal footing.
- **Snow banks:** Difficult terrain and half cover while crouched.
- **Three prayer poles:** Each can be cut or pushed. A falling pole creates a temporary barrier 4 spaces long.
- **Nest shelf:** Contains the egg or trapped cub.
- **Wind gust:** At the end of each round, creatures standing at an exposed edge make a DC 10 STR or DEX save or move 2 spaces. This movement cannot throw a creature directly into the abyss; it leaves them hanging at the edge unless another effect moves them again.
- **Black Fang supply sled:** Contains rope, blankets, and meat. It can distract Grimbeak, anchor a rescue line, or be sent sliding downhill.

## Official Stat Block

### Grimbeak, the Unyielding

**Level 3 solo legendary | Large Owlbear | HP 100 | Medium Armor | STR+**

- **Brutal:** Treat the highest die rolled as the Primary Die. On a crit, knock the target Prone.

After **each hero's turn**, choose one action:

- **Savage Screech (1 use):** All enemies within Reach 12 take 2d6 damage ignoring Armor. DC 11 WIL save or Frightened for 1 round.
- **Rend & Tear:** 2d6+10.
- **Beak:** Move 8, then attack for 2d6.

**Bloodied - 50 HP:** Savage Screech recharges.

**Last Stand:** At 0 HP, Grimbeak is Dying. She dies after 30 additional damage. Until then, attacks use d10s instead of d6s.

## Running the Temporary Alliance

Do not add the full Black Fang patrol as allied damage; that would trivialize the solo fight and slow play.

Instead, at initiative 10 each round, the patrol accomplishes **one support effect** selected by the party or GM:

- Pull one hanging creature to safety.
- Move the supply sled up to 6 spaces.
- Give one hero advantage on a save against the wind.
- Distract Grimbeak, causing her next Beak movement to target a Black Fang guard instead of a hero; narrate the guard escaping or being knocked aside.
- Secure or free the egg/cub if the party has opened a route.

This lets cooperation matter without adding a second army to initiative.

## Ending the Fight

Grimbeak withdraws or becomes non-hostile when:

- Her egg or cub is safe and no creature blocks the nest.
- The intruders retreat beyond the prayer poles.
- She is Bloodied and offered food while given an open escape.
- The party uses animal knowledge or magic to establish the real threat.

If the heroes drive her into Last Stand, make the stakes explicit before further attacks. Killing her is allowed, but it should be a conscious decision rather than the automatic end condition.

## What the Fight Reveals

During the crisis, the Black Fang patrol shows its actual character:

- The veteran protects guards and civilians rather than the diplomatic cargo.
- One outrider tries to recover Vekka's satchel instead of helping.
- The rescue equipment is perfectly organized for mountain casualties.
- A cooperative truce creates a credible relationship the party can use later.

# Recommended Session Use

## If the group follows Scritch immediately

Run **Recover Scritch**. Later, present the patrol as a social/stealth obstacle. Use Grimbeak only if the party seeks another confrontation or the pacing needs a climax.

## If the group shadows the delegation

Use the **Outrider Patrol** when the party is discovered. The party can talk, flee, surrender, misdirect, or fight. The kobold camp remains an explorable location after the patrol.

## If the group creates a major delay in the Wind Pass

Introduce **Grimbeak** as a consequence of noise or entering the nesting shelf. The Black Fang patrol may become reluctant allies.

## Resource safeguard

After one medium or hard fight, do not force another full combat before Frostvein. Convert later opposition into:

- A chase
- A negotiation
- A rescue problem
- A stealth crossing
- Enemies who flee after one objective is lost
