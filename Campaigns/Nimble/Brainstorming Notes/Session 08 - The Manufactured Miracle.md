# Session 08 - The Manufactured Miracle

> [!info] Post-session continuity
> The party did not reach Three-Bells Basin, Rimehook, the avalanche rig, or any of the evidence nodes during Session 08. This situation remains unrevealed GM preparation for Session 09. Begin from the party's stormbound position on the Frostvein plateau, with the Black Fang column camped below the Stone Stairs and Lil Chang still ahead with Da Long.

> [!summary] Session premise
> The Black Fang delegation travels toward Frostvein beneath a peace banner. Rock Scale kobolds are preparing a controlled avalanche near Frostvein's outer settlements so the delegation can arrive as rescuers. The party is close enough to notice pieces of the operation, interfere with it, exploit it, warn someone, or let it unfold.

## GM North Star

This is an exploration situation, not a mystery with one correct solution.

- Put visible, useful objects in the environment.
- Let clues explain what an object *can do* before explaining who put it there.
- Never require a particular clue, roll, route, or confrontation.
- The delegation's peace banner is real. Violence against it has political consequences.
- The avalanche danger is real even though it is being engineered.
- Kobolds are people carrying out a risky plan, not disposable trap components.
- Let the players decide what counts as proof and what they do with it.

## The Situation Before the Party Touches It

The route passes through **Three-Bells Basin**, a steep bowl above a Frostvein waystation called **Rimehook**. The waystation shelters climbers, stores rescue equipment, and controls the final safe trail into Frostvein territory.

Rock Scale kobolds have spent two nights weakening a cornice above Rimehook. Their goal is to bury buildings and block the main approach without destroying the entire settlement. A second team has prepared a narrow rescue route. The Black Fang delegation carries conspicuously useful relief supplies and plans to arrive moments after the collapse.

### Default truth

- **Envoy Gorak Broken-Tusk**, the public leader, knows only that a crisis is “likely” and has been told to be ready to help. He believes Razor's foresight favors the strong.
- **Quartermaster Vekka Ash-Eye** knows the disaster is staged. She carries the timing slate and controls two Black Fang outriders who coordinate with the kobolds.
- Most Black Fang guards know nothing. They believe the extra ropes, shovels, blankets, and burn salves are diplomatic gifts.
- **Tikka-of-the-Cairns**, the kobold survey leader, designed a limited slide. She does not know that Vekka quietly moved one charge toward a larger shelf. Tikka wants a spectacle; Vekka is willing to accept mass casualties.
- The avalanche will happen without the party, but its timing and severity remain fluid until it does.

Change any of these truths if play produces a more interesting interpretation. Preserve the physical clues already established.

## What Everyone Wants

| Actor | Immediate want | What they avoid | What changes them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gorak | Reach Frostvein peacefully and look indispensable | A battle beneath the peace banner | Proof Vekka risked the delegation or made him a dupe |
| Vekka | Turn catastrophe into political leverage | A searchable trail back to her | A clean escape with the timing slate |
| Tikka | Prove kobold engineering can serve Razor | Killing Frostvein children or being abandoned by Black Fang | Rescue, honest negotiation, or proof Vekka enlarged the plan |
| Black Fang guards | Protect the envoy and complete the march | Dishonor, ambush, separation from the column | A visible threat to their own people |
| Frostvein outriders | Protect Rimehook and their trail rights | Outsiders manufacturing a reason to enter Frostvein land | Practical rescue and preserved evidence |

## The Basin as a Toy Box

Do not present these in numbered order. Show two or three from the party's position and let them choose where to go.

### 1. The Peace Column

**Visible:** A white banner on an ash pole, fifteen to twenty disciplined orcs, two diplomats, pack animals, and far more rescue gear than a diplomatic party normally carries.

**Things to manipulate:**

- Cut, steal, replace, or publicly honor the peace banner.
- Spook, redirect, free, or inspect the pack animals.
- Join the column openly, shadow it, impersonate a guide, or create a trail obstruction.
- Separate an outrider by sending a false signal or presenting a genuine emergency.

**What inspection reveals:** The shovels are new; the blankets are bundled for rapid distribution; several rope coils are already cut to Frostvein rescue lengths. One supply crate bears damp blue chalk identical to chalk near the prepared rescue route.

**Complication:** Attacking first makes the party the aggressor. Theft or sabotage may slow the column without bloodshed, but discovered interference gives Gorak a truthful grievance.

### 2. The Reversed Cairns

**Visible:** Frostvein route stones are stacked correctly, but their windward faces have been freshly scraped. One points toward a slower shelf trail; another points away from Rimehook.

**Things to manipulate:**

- Restore, rotate, duplicate, or deliberately misread the markers.
- Leave a warning for Frostvein scouts.
- Use the false route to split the delegation or reach the upper basin first.

**What inspection reveals:** Tiny three-claw prints occur only on sheltered sides. A broken Fang token has been pressed into mud beside one cairn, too obvious to be an accidental loss.

**Interpretations:** Kobolds framed Black Fang; Black Fang marked the collaboration; someone wants investigators to reach a particular conclusion.

### 3. The Humming Snow Anchors

**Visible:** Three iron stakes disappear into the snow. Their cords hum differently when plucked. Colored beads sit on each cord: white, blue, and black.

**Things to manipulate:**

- Pull an anchor to release tension.
- Move it to redirect part of the slide.
- Tune two cords to cancel or amplify their vibration.
- Tie the system into the party's ropes, a tree, the delegation's wagon, or the recovered air-launch device.

**Clear feedback:**

- **White cord:** loosens the small shelf above the road.
- **Blue cord:** opens the pre-cut rescue chute.
- **Black cord:** runs upward toward the large cornice and is under dangerous tension.

No roll is required to learn that changing a cord changes the snow. A relevant check predicts scale or performs a precise change under pressure.

### 4. The Buried Resonator

**Visible:** A pottery drum buried beneath crusted snow. It uses the same compressed-air principles as the kobold launch devices recovered at the Erie outpost.

**Things to manipulate:**

- Vent it harmlessly.
- Re-aim its shockwave.
- Remove its air bladder.
- Trigger it early.
- Carry it elsewhere as evidence or a tool.

**What inspection reveals:** It is not an explosive. It produces a low pulse intended to fracture a prepared snow layer. Its repair wire is Black Fang iron, but its design is kobold.

**Risk:** Loud force, thunder magic, or a failed rough extraction advances the avalanche. Careful work does not.

### 5. The Kobold Survey Blind

**Visible:** A snow-colored hide with sighting sticks aimed at Rimehook, the road, and the upper cornice. It contains dried mushrooms, charcoal sketches, a child's carved dragon, and a schedule made from knotted cord.

**Things to manipulate:**

- Observe from it and learn the whole basin geometry.
- Alter the sighting sticks or schedule.
- Leave a message, trade, set an ambush, or follow supply tracks.
- Take the sketch map—but doing so deprives the kobolds of their safety plan.

**What it reveals:** The original sketch shows a limited road-blocking slide and a marked safe pocket at Rimehook. A later charcoal line extends the release into the large shelf. The new line is drawn by a broader hand.

### 6. The Prepared Miracle Route

**Visible:** A hidden traverse has been brushed clean, fitted with rope loops, and stocked with two sleds. It reaches Rimehook faster than the main road after an avalanche.

**Things to manipulate:**

- Use it to reach and evacuate Rimehook.
- Reveal it to Gorak, the guards, or Frostvein scouts.
- Disable, trap, reroute, or claim it.
- Replace its blue chalk markings with the party's own route code.

**What it implies:** Someone expects both the disaster and its exact shape. This is strong evidence of preparation, but not by itself proof that the Black Fang ordered it.

### 7. Rimehook Waystation

**Visible:** Six Frostvein adults, two children, three injured climbers, goats, a signal horn, rescue stores, and buildings beneath the slope.

**Things to manipulate:**

- Evacuate people, animals, records, or supplies.
- Sound the horn, which warns Frostvein but also tells the kobolds the site is alert.
- Reinforce roofs, open the downhill gate, create snow breaks, or move everyone into the marked safe pocket.
- Tell the truth, tell part of it, or keep the inhabitants calm while investigating.

**The human question:** If warned without proof, the keeper asks: “Do we abandon winter stores because strangers found footprints?”

### 8. Tikka-of-the-Cairns

Tikka is found checking a tension line, trapped beneath a shifted slab, or watching the party from the survey blind—whichever creates contact naturally.

**She knows:** The limited plan, the timing signal, the safe pocket, and that the Black Fang promised no deaths.

**She does not know:** Vekka moved the black line to the upper cornice.

**She can offer:** The trigger sequence, a hidden route, testimony, or help disarming the slide.

**She wants:** Her two missing riggers found and her people not handed to the Black Fang.

Useful lines:

> “A disaster is weather. A rescue is leadership. We are making both arrive on time.”

> “That black cord was not there when I measured the shelf.”

## Portable Player Toys

These are deliberately reusable objects rather than single-answer keys.

### Three tension tokens

Place three visible tokens on the table: **Road Shelf**, **Rescue Chute**, **Upper Cornice**.

- Player actions can add, remove, or move tension.
- At 3 tension, that feature changes or releases.
- Tell players what physical change follows before they commit whenever their characters could reasonably predict it.

### The route map

Draw five connected zones:

```text
Upper Cornice ---- Survey Blind
      |                 |
Road Shelf -------- Main Road ---- Peace Column
      |                 |
   Rimehook ---- Miracle Route
```

Let the party add or erase connections by finding paths, anchoring ropes, collapsing ledges, moving markers, or using flight.

### Signal vocabulary

The kobolds and Vekka use simple visible signals:

| Signal | Meaning | How players can misuse it |
|---|---|---|
| One red kite | Column has entered basin | Launch early, delay, or redirect watchers |
| Two mirror flashes | Rimehook is occupied | Conceal evacuation or fake more witnesses |
| Three horn notes | Release the road shelf | Trigger, cancel with the counter-note, or implicate someone |
| Black smoke | Plan failed; scatter | Flush out riggers or create confusion |

### Evidence cards

Write each on an index card when found. Evidence is physical and can be traded, planted, destroyed, compared, or presented.

- Black Fang iron repair wire
- Blue chalk on relief supplies and rescue route
- Tikka's original limited-slide sketch
- The broad-hand upper-cornice alteration
- Broken Fang token placed too neatly beside a cairn
- Knotted timing cord
- Vekka's slate: “White road. Blue path. Black only if resisted.”
- Testimony from a kobold rigger

No single card proves the entire scheme. Three consistent pieces should convince a fair listener; contradictory or planted evidence creates debate rather than automatic failure.

## Pressure Without a Railroad

Use three clocks only when the table slows or an action clearly changes the situation.

### Delegation arrival - 0/4

Advance when the party spends extended time, takes a long detour, or alerts an outrider. Reduce it by obstructing, bargaining with, splitting, or misleading the column.

### Snow instability - 0/4

Advance for violent force, a major weather shift, careless work on anchors, or deliberate triggering. At 2, small slides reveal the danger. At 3, Rimehook begins evacuation on its own. At 4, resolve the current configuration of the shelves—not a predetermined avalanche.

### Suspicion - separate by faction

Track one simple state for **Black Fang**, **kobolds**, and **Frostvein**: Unaware / Watching / Acting. Do not combine them into a single alert clock.

## Clue Web

Every important conclusion has at least three routes.

### “The avalanche is being engineered.”

- Humming anchors and buried resonator
- Survey sighting sticks and altered snowpack
- Tikka or another rigger's testimony
- The prepared safe pocket and rescue chute

### “Someone expects to perform a rescue.”

- Delegation's pre-cut ropes and relief bundles
- Prepared miracle route and sleds
- Timing cord synchronized to the column's pace
- Blue chalk shared between supplies and route

### “The lethal expansion may not be the kobolds' plan.”

- Original sketch differs from the broad-hand alteration
- Tikka reacts with genuine alarm to the black cord
- Missing riggers were moved or killed near the upper anchor
- Vekka's slate distinguishes white, blue, and black outcomes

## What Happens If the Party Does Nothing

Do not hide this from yourself, but do not force it.

1. Red kite rises as the delegation enters the basin.
2. The road shelf releases and strikes Rimehook.
3. The upper cornice begins to fail because of Vekka's alteration.
4. Gorak leads an impressive rescue along the prepared route.
5. Vekka tries to destroy the timing slate and silence surviving kobolds.
6. Frostvein witnesses a genuine Black Fang rescue after an apparently natural disaster.

The party can interrupt this at any point, including after the slide. Discovery after harm still creates meaningful choices about rescue, evidence, diplomacy, vengeance, and public accusation.

## Valid Directions of Play

Support all of these without steering toward one:

- **Delay the delegation:** obstacles, negotiation, misdirection, stolen animals, false signals.
- **Investigate quietly:** follow tracks, study equipment, watch the column, contact kobolds.
- **Prevent the disaster:** vent the resonator, retune anchors, evacuate Rimehook.
- **Redirect the disaster:** block the road, isolate the delegation, expose the prepared rescue.
- **Let it happen safely:** preserve the limited slide but neutralize the upper cornice, then observe who moves first.
- **Expose the scheme publicly:** collect evidence and bring witnesses before Frostvein.
- **Turn the conspirators:** reveal Vekka's lethal alteration to Tikka or Gorak.
- **Exploit the plan:** arrive first as rescuers or force the Black Fang to save people without political control.
- **Attack:** possible, consequential, and not treated as an automatic campaign failure.

## Improvised Rulings

- If an idea directly changes a physical object and no one opposes it, it works.
- Roll only when timing, precision, danger, or resistance matters.
- On a failed check, reveal information and change the situation; do not simply say “nothing happens.”
- Damage to the rig changes the avalanche's shape. It does not automatically trigger the worst outcome.
- Clever use of the recovered air-launch device should work once dramatically, then leave it damaged or depleted if needed.
- Social leverage changes what an NPC risks, not what they know.

## Spotlight Hooks

- **Rowan:** A trapped kobold rigger and a Frostvein child are endangered in different directions. Neither is a fake choice; allies and tools can save both.
- **Whisper:** Vekka's outrider uses concealed signs and dead drops. Whisper can shadow, replace, or reverse them.
- **Umbrail:** The resonator borrows principles from Erie windcraft. Is this theft, old shared knowledge, or evidence of an Erie intermediary?
- **Alder:** Shadows beneath the cornice move before the snow does. His patron can identify the most fragile point, but wants a moment alone with Rubius's axe.
- **Rubius:** The axe offers to cut the entire shelf free cleanly. It may be right. The consequence is an enormous, controllable act of destruction.
- **Lil Chang:** He is ahead at Frostvein, but a signal, witness, or rescued survivor can reach him and change the diplomatic hearing.

## Ending the Session

End when the basin has changed hands politically, not necessarily when every secret is solved. Good stopping images include:

- Gorak lowering the peace banner while confronting Vekka.
- Tikka standing before Frostvein witnesses with the altered sketch.
- The party watching the harmless road shelf fall while the upper cornice remains suspended.
- Rimehook survivors entering the hearing carrying Black Fang blankets and the party's evidence.
- The delegation arriving intact, unaware that its “miracle” has been dismantled.
- A larger avalanche beginning after everyone realizes the original conspiracy is no longer the only problem.
