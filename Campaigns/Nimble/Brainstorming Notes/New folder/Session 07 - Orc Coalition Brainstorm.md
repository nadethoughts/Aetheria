# Session 07 — Orc Coalition Brainstorm

> [!summary] Session premise
> The party approaches neutral orc tribes as outsiders seeking negotiation, not as commanders. Each tribe controls access to the conversation and requires the party to solve a local problem before it will even consider an alliance against Razor.

## Current Canon

- [[World/Factions/Erie|The Erie]] and [[World/Factions/Cloud Seers|the Cloud Seers]] are allied against [[World/Characters/Razor|Razor]].
- The [[World/Factions/Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] and most mountain kobolds serve Razor.
- Black Fang warriors carry broken fangs as tribal tokens. Earlier broken-fang raiders and the Black Fang are the same faction.
- The party is level 3 and cannot fight Razor directly.
- [[World/Characters/Zing Zow|Zing Zow]] and the Cloud Seers are handling the nearby Erie mine and requesting reinforcements.
- The party's declared objective is to recruit neutral orc tribes into the resistance.
- The party has not yet selected a first tribe, route, offer, or spokesperson.

## GM Goal

The party should leave the session with one of these outcomes:

- A neutral tribe commits real support to the coalition.
- A tribe agrees to a conditional alliance and sends representatives to General Brand.
- The party earns a useful faction connection, intelligence, or safe route even if formal alliance fails.

The party is building trust, not claiming authority. Avoid making a single Persuasion roll determine the outcome.

## Session Spine

1. Zing Zow gives the party two or three possible neutral tribes.
2. A short mine report creates pressure: the mine is safe for now, but Black Fang movement or missing ore has been detected.
3. The party chooses a tribe and route.
4. Mountain travel produces one clue or complication.
5. The tribe blocks access to its leader until the party addresses a local crisis.
6. The crisis can be solved through combat, exploration, negotiation, or a combination.
7. The party earns an audience and negotiates a conditional alliance.
8. The session closes with evidence of Razor's next move.

## Seven Neutral Tribe Options

These are draft names and identities. Select three before the session; only the selected tribes need full preparation.

### Hollow Drum Orcs

    **Identity:** Cave-dwellers who communicate across the mountains through coded drums.

    **Crisis:** Their warning drums have begun sounding by themselves, drawing predators and Black Fang patrols toward the settlement.

    **Proof of worth:** Identify who—or what—is sending the false signals without destroying the communication network.

    **Combat version:** Black Fang infiltrators and Rock Scale kobolds attack multiple drum posts. The party must defend the network while locating the signal-maker.

    **Negotiation value:** The Hollow Drum can provide early warnings, coded messages, and intelligence about troop movement.

    Motivation: - **Fear:** What will Razor do if we refuse him?

###  Frostvein Orcs

    **Identity:** Winter climbers and avalanche guides.

    **Crisis:** An avalanche has trapped families, but clearing the obvious route will trigger a second collapse.

    **Proof of worth:** Rescue the trapped people through an indirect route, controlled avalanche, or difficult logistical plan.

    **Combat version:** Wargs or ice-adapted predators attack the rescue site while the party digs and stabilizes ropes. Large explosions worsen the avalanche.

    **Negotiation value:** The Frostvein can guide the party through dangerous passes and move people or supplies through winter terrain.

    Motivation: **Resentment:** What do we remember about the Erie or neighboring tribes?


### 7. Graven Hand Orcs

    **Identity:** Keepers of burial caves and ancestral law.

    **Crisis:** An ancestral relic has been stolen from the burial caves. The elders are divided between neutrality, joining Razor, and aiding the Erie.

    **Proof of worth:** Recover the relic, determine who stole it, or mediate the tribe's public decision without choosing its future for it.

    **Combat version:** Black Fang raiders attempt to trade the relic to Razor while an ancestral guardian awakens inside the burial cave. The party must recover the relic intact and avoid desecration.

    **Negotiation value:** The Graven Hand can provide ancient history, burial-site access, and information about the Erie–dragon war.

    Motivation: **Practical need:** What must be solved today before we can worry about the war?


## Negotiation Structure

The tribe should not say, “Complete our quest and we automatically join.” Use this sequence:

1. The party encounters the tribe's crisis.
2. Scouts or guards prevent access to the chief.
3. The party chooses how to help.
4. The tribe grants a limited audience after seeing what the party does.
5. Negotiation begins while the tribe remains undecided.

### Conditional Alliance

The party cannot honestly promise what General Brand or the Erie government will do. A fair offer is:

- The tribe defends its own territory.
- The tribe provides guides, intelligence, or volunteers.
- The tribe sends representatives to negotiate with General Brand.
- The Erie must recognize the tribe's territorial independence before full military commitment.

Useful leader line:

> “You have earned the right to speak. You have not yet earned the right to be believed.”

- **Umbrail:** “Which part of this tribe's story about Erie rule does Umbrail recognize as true?”
- **Rowan:** “What has this party done that proves its alliance would protect the weak?”

- **Rubius:** “The tribe respects your strength. What do you do to show that strength is under your control?”

### Shadow-Eaten Camp

    A deserted camp still has a burning fire that casts no light. Shadow forms rise from abandoned gear, while a survivor hides beneath the camp. The shadows imitate the party's voices and tactics.

## Combat Principles

Every combat should have at least one objective besides defeating enemies:

- Hold a tunnel open.
- Save civilians.
- Preserve a food store or forge.
- Disable a wind harness.
- Recover a relic intact.
- Protect a communication network.
- Capture an enemy for information.
- Choose whether a corrupted creature can be saved.

After each combat, give the tribe a visible political reason to care:

- The party protected civilians.
- A captured enemy revealed useful information.
- A restrained creature became evidence of Razor's corruption.
- A destroyed resource damaged the tribe's willingness to cooperate.
- A surviving enemy carried the party's reputation to another tribe.

## Outcome Ladder

### Strong Success

- The tribe joins the coalition.
- The party gains guides, intelligence, and volunteers.
- The tribe agrees to contact General Brand.

### Mixed Success

- The tribe agrees to defend its own territory.
- It sends a delegation or limited volunteers.
- Full support depends on Erie recognition of its independence.

### Costly Success

- The tribe joins, but the Black Fang escape with ore or intelligence.
- Razor learns that the party is recruiting allies.
- Umbrail's artifact becomes easier for Razor to track.

### Failure Forward

- The tribe refuses formal alliance but gives the party a safe route, intelligence, or a connection to another neutral tribe.
- Individual volunteers accompany the party.
- The party still learns something about Razor's next target.

## Closing Revelation

The rescued scouts or recovered documents identify Razor's next target as an eastern Erie site along General Brand's mysterious circuit. The map also includes a symbol Umbrail recognizes from the unidentified artifact taken from his mentor.

- [ ] Prepare one non-combat solution and one combat solution.

- [ ] Decide what report Zing Zow sends from the mine.
- [ ] Confirm level-three abilities and subclasses.
- [ ] Confirm Rubius's wound, Shadow Point, and axe damage.
- [ ] Decide what Umbrail's artifact looks like.
- [ ] Decide what evidence points toward General Brand's next destination.
- [ ] Keep Razor distant: shadow, orders, fear, and consequences rather than a direct encounter.
