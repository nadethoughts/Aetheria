# Session 11 Introduction — Icehold’s Choice
The great hall of Icehold is warm with firelight, but the air between its guests is cold.

Chieftess An Yue sits with her advisors beneath the carved figures of Frostvein’s ancestors. Lil Chang is already there. The rest of you have crossed the ice labyrinth and arrived at last, carrying the marks of a hard journey—and the knowledge that one Black Fang scout may have escaped to tell his own version of what happened on the plateau.

The Black Fang delegation heads this direction beneath its banner of peace. Inside, Frostvein waits to hear what each side will offer: not just a promise to stand against Razor, but a reason to believe that victory won’t cost them their home, their independence, or the stores they need to survive the winter.

An Yue looks from the newcomers to her advisors.

“I will not speak of alliances until I have heard what both sides have to offer,” she says, "but I will gladly hear what you have to say about yourselves. You do not come from the Mountains. What drives your ambitions?"
