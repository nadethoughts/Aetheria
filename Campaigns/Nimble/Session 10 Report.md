# Nimble Session 10 Report

## Executive Summary

Session 10 brought the land party through the Frostvein plateau and into Icehold, where they reunited with Lil Chang just as Chieftess An Yue was preparing to hear their proposals. The trip was dangerous, but the party avoided another fight with the Black Fang and chose a delay tactic that would not endanger Frostvein's water or deliberately trigger an avalanche.

The group iced a section of the Black Fang delegation's switchback route and felled a tree across it. During the night, an owl-like creature attacked them in a cave. Rubius was reduced to zero hit points and took a Wound, but recovered with a caustic brew. Umbrail frightened the creature away with Voice of Thunder. The party later crossed the magically warded ice labyrinth, with Umbrail navigating from memory and countering the maze's disorienting magic.

At the end of the session, the party had reunited in Icehold's great hall. An Yue and her advisors were ready to hear their proposals, but the recording ended before negotiations began. Frostvein had made no commitment, the Black Fang delegation's status was unknown, and the escaped scout from Session 09 could still affect how the party's actions were received.

## Detailed Chronological Report

### 1. The Party Delayed the Delegation

The land party began above the Black Fang diplomatic camp, after the patrol fight in Session 09. The Black Fang delegation was traveling under a banner of peace. The party considered ways to slow its progress, but rejected poisoning the water because Frostvein could also be harmed, and decided that an avalanche was too difficult and risky to cause.

Instead, they packed snow onto the switchbacks and melted it to make the path icy, then felled a tree across the route. The group left the exposed area to find shelter before nightfall. They found a cave and took watch.

### 2. An Owlbear-Like Creature Attacked

Rowan noticed eyes and movement in the darkness. A large, quadrupedal owl-like creature emerged and attacked with a screech that dealt 14 damage to everyone, ignoring armor.

Rubius fell to zero hit points and took a Wound rather than becoming unconscious. The Axe of Snaggletooth let him enter a rage for free, but he had only one action while below zero. He used it to drink a caustic brew and regain hit points; the brew also poisoned him for several rounds.

Alder summoned shadow minions and used Umbrail's extra action to direct them against the creature. The party protected Rubius while keeping the attacker occupied. Umbrail then used **Voice of Thunder** through Tempest's Command, booming his voice to frighten it off. The group considered drawing it toward the Black Fang camp but did not trust the plan enough to try. They moved to a more sheltered ledge in the cave and completed a field rest.

### 3. The Party Crossed the Tundra

The party considered using the creature or the earlier battle's bodies to cause trouble for the delegation, then chose to continue toward Icehold. Umbrail scouted from the air. By midday, the Black Fang delegation had not yet climbed onto the plateau, consistent with the obstacles having delayed it, though the party did not learn what the delegation would do next.

Umbrail spotted distant bipedal figures but could not identify them. The party also found small, four-toed tracks of unknown origin. They traveled until late afternoon and camped beside an ice outcropping. Their watch passed without incident. They saw an owlbear at a distance, but it did not approach.

### 4. Umbrail Guided the Party Through the Ice Labyrinth

The party reached an immense ice wall concealing the approach to Icehold. From above, Umbrail saw the ice labyrinth and memorized its layout, then sketched the route for the others. Movement atop the wall suggested that someone was watching them.

The maze's ice blocks were protected by frost magic, and a broader magical effect interfered with the party's perception. Umbrail recognized the effect and used Tempest's Command to help the group keep its bearings. They crossed the labyrinth without getting lost.

A Frostvein warden in white furs and a white dire-wolf headdress challenged their purpose. The party said it came peacefully on behalf of the Erie alliance and Cloud Seers, hoping to join its companions. When warned not to bring war to Frostvein's borders, one traveler replied, “War is at your borders whether you want it to be or not.” The warden then granted passage. Concealed Frostvein guards emerged and escorted the party through the gate.

### 5. The Party Reunited at Icehold

Icehold combined snow-grown structures with rooms carved into ice sculptures. Statues of ancient orc warriors stood near the entrance, and a fountain sent snow rising and falling into water that melted and returned to it.

The party entered the great hall and found Lil Chang eating with the Frostvein. Chieftess [[World/Characters/An Yue|An Yue]] stood with her advisors and welcomed the travelers. She said she had heard they had proposals for the tribe. The recording ended before the negotiations began.

## Potential Plot Lines to Close by Session 12

These are GM-facing possibilities based on the current campaign state, not events established in Session 10. Each can be resolved, partially resolved, or deliberately left open by the end of Session 12.

### 1. Decide Frostvein's Position

Let the party negotiate with An Yue, then hear the Black Fang offer under its peace banner. Frostvein could make a clear choice by Session 12: join the coalition under written independence guarantees, remain neutral under a limited defense compact, or reject both sides until specific conditions are met. A conditional answer still gives the players a concrete political outcome.

### 2. Resolve the Plateau Battle's Diplomatic Fallout

The escaped scout and the party's decision to delay the delegation give the Black Fang a credible grievance. By Session 12, decide whether the delegation confronts the party, accepts an explanation, demands restitution, or uses the incident to pressure Frostvein. Scritch's knowledge of Black Fang command and the party's account of the patrol attack can help distinguish a defensive encounter from an unprovoked ambush.

### 3. Make Frostvein's Winter Terms Concrete

The party could secure or lose a specific aid package, hunting-ground arrangement, training exchange, or limit on troops and pass access. By Session 12, resolve at least one practical need—such as food, safe passage, or signed guarantees—so Frostvein's decision has visible consequences beyond speeches.

### 4. Turn the Unknown Watchers into a Lead

The figures on the tundra, movement atop the labyrinth, and four-toed tracks can connect to a single scout, creature, or local guide—or remain separate clues. By Session 12, reveal one useful fact about who is watching the routes and what they want. This can point toward the next mission without forcing an immediate detour.

### 5. Advance the Axe's Cost and Rubius's Response

Rubius suffered another Wound while bonded to the Axe of Snaggletooth, but Session 10 did not record a new bargain or Shadow Point. By Session 12, clarify how the Wound interacts with the artifact's rules and give Rubius a consequential choice: set limits on using the axe, seek counsel or safeguards, or accept another bargain to meet an immediate need. The aim can be a clear next step rather than resolving the axe's full curse.

### 6. Establish What Da Long Can Promise

Da Long has opened the Frostvein conversation but cannot bind the Cloud Seers or Erie. By Session 12, decide whether he can obtain a formal mandate, arrange a meeting with an authorized representative, or only deliver a message and return with a later answer. This defines how quickly Frostvein's terms can become enforceable.
