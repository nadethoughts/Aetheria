# Nimble Session 05 Report

## Executive Summary

Session 05 expanded the party's mountain investigation into a regional mystery and ended with a devastating personal loss for Umbrail. The group began among the bodies of the orcs and boars defeated at the end of Session 04. Every orc carried some form of broken fang as a tribal token, providing the first recognizable marker for the hostile group. The party also discussed how best to use the newly recovered Flamecaller's Talisman.

The campaign shifted into a hex-crawl structure, with mountain hexes normally requiring three days of travel and a growing chance of encounters. The party initially followed the orcs' trail, but severe weather slowed the journey and forced them into shelter beneath a rocky overhang. Giant wolf-like predators attacked during the storm. Lil Chang used exceptional speed and repeated attacks to knock one creature over a cliff and crush another's throat, while the rest of the party survived a dangerous grapple-heavy fight.

After an unsafe field rest, the group lost the orc trail but found a desecrated Birdfolk shrine dedicated to an air or wind deity. Kobolds had altered the figure to look scaled and snouted. The party made a rough attempt to restore it despite Umbrail's bitter lack of faith in gods who had not protected his declining people.

Continuing through the mountains, Rubius and Umbrail found small quarries, molted black scales, and other signs of increased kobold activity. Scouts also glimpsed the shadow of an enormous flying creature and later found claw marks far larger than those of Snaggletooth. The threat was clearly beyond the party's present ability to confront directly.

Deeper in the mountains, the party discovered kobold tracks and iron-shod orc prints traveling in the same direction without evidence of a fight. The trail led to an **Erie outpost** associated with Umbrail's people. The elevated fortress had been burned, blasted, and toppled despite being nearly inaccessible to non-fliers. The Erie defenders had apparently expected trouble and reinforced the normal garrison, but roughly twenty to twenty-five Birdfolk still died under overwhelming numbers of orcs and kobolds.

Among the dead, Umbrail found the unnamed mentor who first taught him magic. He became nearly catatonic with grief. Rowan reconstructed the battle while the others examined strange air-powered magical devices used by the attackers to launch themselves toward the elevated position. The party built separate funeral pyres, treating the Erie dead with respect and burning the attackers apart from them.

The outpost's destruction also exposed a deeper secret. The Erie guard something of ancient significance in the region, and every Birdfolk is sworn not to reveal it. Umbrail did not disclose the secret to the party and was barely able to answer ordinary questions after finding his mentor.

The group climbed to the outpost's high crest and camped there. At dusk, a griffin approached directly, hunting and apparently viewing the party as easy prey. The session ended before initiative, creating the combat cliffhanger resolved in Session 06.

## Session at a Glance

- **Starting situation:** The party had defeated a broken-fang-marked orc war party and recovered the Flamecaller's Talisman.
- **Exploration mode:** Mountain hex crawl, normally three days per hex with escalating encounter risk.
- **First major encounter:** Giant wolf-like predators attacked the party's storm shelter.
- **Major clues:** Desecrated Erie shrine, black kobold scales, small quarries, and signs of an enormous flying creature.
- **Major anomaly:** Orc and kobold tracks moved together toward an Erie outpost without signs of conflict.
- **Major tragedy:** The Erie outpost was destroyed, and Umbrail found his magical mentor among the dead.
- **Technological discovery:** Air-powered magical launch devices apparently allowed ground attackers to assault the elevated fortress.
- **Secret revealed to Umbrail:** The Erie guard something of ancient significance in the region.
- **Cliffhanger:** A hunting griffin flew directly toward the party's camp.

## Detailed Chronological Report

### 1. Broken-Fang Tokens and the Flamecaller's Talisman

The session resumed immediately after the mountain-pass ambush. The party searched the orc bodies and noticed that each attacker carried a broken fang from some creature as a token or tribal marker. The exact formal name of the group was not established here, but the repeated symbol made future identification possible.

The players also reviewed the **Flamecaller's Talisman**. It can use an action to make several creatures Smoldering, which improves Umbrail's fire effects and may interact with other classes. Because activation competes with the mage's attack actions, the party considered giving it to a supporting character rather than automatically assigning it to Umbrail. No permanent holder was confirmed before travel began.

### 2. The Mountain Hex Crawl Began

The GM introduced the regional travel procedure. A normal mountain hex required approximately three days to cross, with rougher terrain potentially taking longer. Navigation checks could preserve or improve the party's pace. A d6 encounter pool grew over time; rolling a 1 triggered an encounter, while 6s could reduce the accumulated risk.

The group clarified that its original purpose was still to investigate the black-scaled kobolds, not necessarily to begin a war with every orc tribe in the mountains. Rubius nevertheless wanted to track the ambushers long enough to learn where they came from and whether they posed a continuing threat to travelers or Clackston.

Umbrail privately considered whether to approach the Erie and chose avoidance. He was willing to guide the party through familiar territory, but not yet ready to confront his people or his guilt about leaving them.

### 3. Bad Weather and a Predator Encounter

A failed navigation attempt led the party into worsening weather. Rain became snow and ice, the wind intensified, and the group made slow progress through steep mountain terrain.

After several days, they sheltered beneath a rock overhang near a cave entrance. In the darkness, the howling wind seemed to answer itself. Several enormous wolf-like creatures approached the firelight and prepared to pounce.

Characters who noticed the predators in time received a single preparatory action. Umbrail was caught unaware, while others repositioned, raised the alarm, or grabbed a burning branch.

### 4. The Giant-Wolf Battle

The predators relied on **Rip Apart** attacks that grappled a victim and followed with additional damage. Rowan and Lil Chang were both caught in their jaws during the fight, forcing the party to spend actions on defense, escape, and rescue rather than pure offense.

Important moments included:

- A burning branch was thrown at one wolf, singeing it before the main exchange.
- Umbrail used Heart's Fire to grant an ally another action once he understood the danger.
- Rowan moved to intercept attackers and absorbed repeated bites while grappled.
- Alder's shadows added bodies and attacks to a cramped defensive position.
- Whisper exploited wounded targets and openings created by the front line.
- Rubius supplied the strength and durability expected from the party's largest member.
- Lil Chang used a high initiative result for multiple opening actions, battered one wolf backward and over the cliff, and later killed another by crushing its throat.

The party killed the predators and survived the storm, but the fight consumed health and resources.

### 5. An Unsafe Rest

The group completed a field rest at the shelter after the attack. Because the location was not secure, it did not count as a safe rest and did not automatically restore all hit points.

Instead, wounded characters could spend Hit Dice for maximum results plus the normal Strength contribution. The rules discussion emphasized that Hit Dice are limited until replenished by a full rest, so characters needed to avoid wasting healing beyond their missing health.

The storm broke the following morning, and the party continued.

### 6. The Orc Trail Was Lost

The party attempted to relocate the ambushers' camp but failed the required Perception check. The trail went cold among the difficult slopes.

This failure prevented the mountain arc from becoming a direct raid on an unknown orc settlement. It also returned the group to its original objective: finding evidence of kobolds and understanding why they had moved toward Clackston.

### 7. The Desecrated Erie Shrine

Instead of the orc camp, the party found an old shrine built by Umbrail's people and dedicated to an air or wind deity. Kobolds had vandalized and reshaped the figure, replacing birdlike features with scales, a snout, and other draconic or kobold traits.

The shrine confirmed that kobolds were active in this part of the mountains. It also exposed Umbrail's alienation from his own traditions. He did not feel that the gods had meaningfully protected his declining people and showed little interest in devotional restoration.

Other party members wanted to repair the desecration out of respect. Without a reliable image of the original form, they could only gather pieces and make a rough reconstruction. Umbrail accepted the gesture but did not recover his faith.

### 8. Quarries, Black Scales, and a Huge Shadow

The group moved into another mountain region and deliberately searched for kobold signs. Rubius used Nature Craft, with Umbrail contributing knowledge of local kobold habits and terrain.

They found:

- Several small quarry or excavation sites
- Molted black kobold scales
- Evidence that kobold activity was heavier on this side of the mountain range

The dig sites' age was uncertain, but they had appeared during the years Umbrail was away. The evidence suggested a sustained presence rather than a single wandering band.

During scouting, the party noticed the shadow of an enormous flying creature. It passed too quickly for identification. Umbrail later searched from the air and found a high ledge marked by claws vastly larger than those of a normal kobold—or even the enlarged Snaggletooth.

The GM made the danger explicit: the creature represented an underlying regional threat and was not something the level-2 party should seek out for a direct fight.

### 9. The Party Chose to Continue Deeper

The group considered descending toward the river valley or remaining in mountain terrain. Because kobolds prefer defensible, ambush-friendly ground, they chose the route deeper through the high country.

The party was less worried that these distant warrens could immediately threaten Clackston; the travel distance was substantial. The objective shifted from stopping an imminent raid to understanding the migration, alliances, and larger danger that produced Snaggletooth's group.

As the party approached Umbrail's homeland, he became quieter and visibly more anxious.

### 10. Orc and Kobold Tracks Converged

The party found black-scaled kobold tracks and iron-shod orc boot prints traveling along the same route. There was no evidence of fighting between them.

This contradicted Umbrail's regional knowledge. Orcs and kobolds were not natural allies and generally disliked one another. They might have used the same path at different times, or one group might have followed the other, but the tracks looked uncomfortably coordinated.

Umbrail then recognized that the trail was heading toward an **Erie outpost**. The tracks were only a few days old, and the outpost was approximately half a day away. Flying ahead would not change whatever had already happened, so the party advanced together.

### 11. The Destroyed Erie Outpost

Umbrail scouted from the air and saw the outpost first. It had been razed and was still smoldering.

The destruction was difficult to explain. Erie outposts were built on treetops, rock pillars, and other elevated positions accessible primarily by flight. Ordinary orcs and kobolds should not have been able to bring a large assault force to the top.

At the site, the party found:

- Burned wooden buildings
- Stone structures toppled as if struck by hurricane-force winds
- Rubble and bodies at the base of the rock pillar
- Birdfolk, orc, and kobold dead
- Evidence of explosions, fire, and overwhelming numbers

The normal garrison could be as small as ten, but the party counted roughly twenty to twenty-five Erie bodies. The outpost had apparently received reinforcements or expected an attack. Even prepared defenders were overwhelmed.

### 12. Rowan Reconstructed the Battle

Rowan used his familiarity with battlefields to build a likely sequence. The assault began on the eastern side and swept through the position. The Erie were stronger individual combatants but could not withstand the attackers' numbers.

Orcs appeared to be the main force at the top. Their use of fire, explosives, and direct raiding tactics was recognizable. The toppled stone structures were harder to explain and suggested powerful air magic or some ancient mechanism.

Kobold bodies were more common below. Several lay in dogpiles around strange devices, as though many of them had grabbed onto a single mechanism at once.

### 13. Umbrail Found His Mentor

While Rowan studied the battlefield, Umbrail searched the Erie dead for familiar faces. He found the mentor who had first taught him magic.

The mentor was unnamed during the session. Their body lay among a large number of slain attackers, implying that they fought fiercely before being overwhelmed.

Umbrail stopped and became almost catatonic. Rowan eventually stood beside him without forcing conversation. For the rest of the session, Umbrail gave only brief answers and did not explain everything he knew about the outpost or the region.

This was the direct realization of Umbrail's stated regret from Session 04: he had returned to find that his people needed him, and he had not been there.

### 14. The Air-Launch Devices

The party cleared kobold bodies from the strange mechanisms. The devices resembled incomplete pulley systems but used no visible ropes. Poking one produced an internal whir and a burst of air.

Further examination showed that the attackers had used magical-mechanical air propulsion to launch or carry themselves upward. More complete devices lay near orc bodies at the top and around the outpost's edges.

The kobolds appeared not to understand the technology. Groups had piled onto individual launchers, overloaded them, failed to reach the crest, and died in the fall. The orcs used the devices more effectively.

The party discussed taking an intact example so that someone more knowledgeable could study it. The transcript supports the intention to recover one, but the final inventory entry and operational condition should be confirmed.

### 15. Funeral Pyres and the Erie Secret

The party spent the remainder of the day understanding the battle and handling the dead. They built a respectful pyre for the Birdfolk and a separate pyre for the orcs and kobolds. The distinction honored the defenders while preventing the enemy bodies from being left to rot around the site.

When the party asked why anyone would attack this outpost, Umbrail knew that it did not guard a single obvious object. The wider Erie presence in the area, however, protects **something of ancient significance**, and every Birdfolk is sworn to secrecy regarding it.

Umbrail did not reveal the secret. His silence was not merely strategic; he was emotionally unable to have a full conversation after finding his mentor.

### 16. The Griffin Approached

By evening, the group found a way to climb to the outpost's high crest using trees, ropes, flight, and the terrain. They established camp where the defenders had lived.

As the party prepared to rest, a large flying shape approached. They identified it as a **griffin** flying directly toward them. It appeared to be hunting, and the exhausted party looked like easy prey.

The session ended before combat began. Session 06 opened with the griffin fight.

## Character-by-Character Impact

### Umbrail

This was Umbrail's defining session to date. He began by deliberately avoiding his home and ended beside the corpse of the person who taught him magic. The desecrated shrine tested his faith, the converging tracks tested his regional assumptions, and the destroyed outpost turned his abstract regret into a direct loss. His oath of secrecy now conflicts with the party's need to understand why the Erie are being attacked.

### Rowan Hearthboro

Rowan protected the party during the wolf fight and later provided emotional and professional support at the outpost. His battlefield reconstruction gave the group a coherent picture of the assault, while his decision to stand quietly beside Umbrail showed restraint appropriate to his protective role.

### Lil Chang

Lil Chang was the standout combatant in the predator encounter. His speed allowed multiple early attacks, he knocked one wolf over the cliff, and he killed another through brutal close-range control. The attack also demonstrated that even his mobility does not make him immune to grappling predators.

### Rubius Haggard

Rubius combined tracking and front-line combat while carrying the unaddressed shadow axe. He helped locate the quarries and scales, kept the party moving toward the kobold objective, and showed interest in salvaging the attackers' unusual technology. The axe's influence remained quiet but unresolved.

### Alder

Alder continued to use shadow minions as expendable protection during a difficult field encounter. The discovery of organized excavation may also resonate with his practical homesteader background, although no direct link to his lost heirloom emerged.

### Whisper

Whisper contributed to the wolf fight and mountain scouting but did not receive a major personal revelation. The ruined outpost creates opportunities for his investigative and infiltration skills, especially if the party begins tracing how the attackers obtained their technology.

## Overall Assessment

Session 05 transformed the mountain journey from open-ended exploration into the campaign's central conflict. The desecrated shrine and quarries showed that the kobolds had been active for years. The shared trail and destroyed outpost proved that someone had overcome the region's normal hostility between orcs and kobolds and equipped ground forces to attack the Erie's greatest tactical advantage: flight.

The outpost scene worked because the strategic mystery and Umbrail's personal story arrived together. The party did not merely discover a ruined fortress; Umbrail found evidence that his worst fear had come true while he was away. His silence about the ancient secret is understandable but cannot remain neutral for long, because that secret is probably connected to the attackers' objective.

The griffin cliffhanger then prevented the party from processing the loss in safety. Session 06 was positioned to reveal whether the creature was another random danger, part of the unknown flying threat, or connected to the wider forces moving through the Balshar Mountains.
