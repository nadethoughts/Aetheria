# Nimble Campaign Synopsis — Through Session 08

> [!warning] GM-facing spoilers
> This document combines events the characters have experienced, information discussed openly at the table, confirmed background from the campaign canvas, and unused preparation that the players have not encountered. Sections labeled **GM truth** or **optional prep** should not be shared as a player-facing recap without removing spoilers.

## Executive Summary

The campaign began as a local livestock-recovery job in **Clackston** and has developed into a regional war for control of the **Balshar Mountains**.

The original posse—**Rubius Haggard**, **Whisper**, **Umbrail**, **Rowan Hearthboro**, and **Alder**—followed black-scaled kobolds into a newly exposed Second Age barracks. Inside, they rescued **Lil Chang**, recovered Clackston's animals, and killed the kobolds' magically enlarged leader, **Snaggletooth**. Rubius then claimed Snaggletooth's intelligent shadow axe despite an ancient warning against the thing that “drinks the light.”

The party traced the kobolds toward the Balshar Mountains. There they found **Black Fang** orcs carrying broken-fang tribal tokens, increased kobold excavation, a desecrated Erie shrine, evidence of a huge flying creature, and a destroyed **Erie** outpost. Umbrail found his former magical mentor among the dead. The attack demonstrated that orcs and kobolds were cooperating and using magical air-launch devices to overcome the Erie's normal advantage of flight.

At the ruined outpost, the party encountered **Zing Zow** and the **Cloud Seer** griffin riders. Zing Zow revealed that a dragon named **Razor** has established a roost in the Balshar Mountains, won the devotion of nearly all local kobolds, allied with the hostile **Black Fang** orcs, and begun a campaign to dominate the range. The Erie and Cloud Seers are resisting, while other orc tribes remain neutral.

Razor is seeking the strategic advantages guarded by the Erie: ancient anti-dragon artifacts, a mine whose ore can strengthen draconic breath weapons, and—according to the GM campaign canvas—the ancient resting place associated with the defeated dragon flights. Umbrail confirmed that he had unknowingly taken one of his mentor's lesser artifacts. He cleansed and activated it as the **Talon of Unmaking**, an anti-armor talisman with an enhanced effect against dragons.

The party cannot defeat Razor through an ordinary fight. Their chosen contribution is to recruit the unaligned orc tribes and build a coalition. Zing Zow and his riders have taken responsibility for checking the nearby mine and requesting reinforcements. A Black Fang column of five diplomats under **Gorak** and fifteen warriors aligned with Quartermaster **Vekka** is traveling beneath a peace banner toward the Frostvein settlement. Lil Chang and the rescued Cloud Seer scout **Da Long** flew ahead on Da Long's griffon, **Quickfeather**. The land party recruited the escaped kobold **Scritch**, passed the Black Fang camp unseen, and reached the snowbound Frostvein plateau.

At the same time, a second campaign-scale threat is developing below the mountains. Rubius's axe is a cursed intelligent shadow artifact that becomes more powerful and more corrupting with use. During Session 08 it consumed three kobolds, overrode Rubius's judgment, and drove him to attack Alder. Rubius now has three tracked Shadow Points. Alder openly admitted that his patron wants Rubius killed and the axe taken, and that his lost family heirloom lies in the **Undershadow**. The **Shadow Well** there remains the only known way to remove the axe's power.

## The Story So Far

### Arc One: Warren's Want — Sessions 01–04

Clackston faced a winter crisis after animals and grain disappeared from its largest farms. **Master Glenson** offered 25 gold per person and a 5-gold bonus for a resolution within one week. The newly formed posse tracked black scales and clawed footprints southeast of town.

The party captured a kobold dragging a stolen mule. Rowan restrained and questioned it, learned about a crater, a doorway, and a “big boss,” then released it with a warning. At the crater, the group found an ancient stone entrance newly exposed by a destructive impact. The Old Tongue inscription warned the Keepers of the Dawn about the “Shadow of the Unmade Sun” and a thing that “drinks the light.”

Inside, the party discovered a Second Age barracks occupied by kobolds. They fought through minions, a trapped resonant corridor, a treasury, animal pens, and regimented defenders. They collected pieces of an orichalcum sun crest that functioned as the key to the deepest chamber.

In the animal-pen area, the group found the stolen livestock and defeated a Kobold Clangor. In a nearby prison, they rescued Lil Chang, who had been captured while traveling to answer Clackston's posse call. The kobolds intended for their leader to “test something” on him.

Graffiti announced **“Snaggletooth's Ascension.”** Behind the circular door, the party found Snaggletooth enlarged to roughly thirteen or fourteen feet and wielding a shadow axe. Lil Chang delivered the killing blow. Snaggletooth shrank back to ordinary kobold size at death, demonstrating that his transformation came from an external power rather than natural growth.

The party recovered the animals and returned to Clackston in time to prevent a winter catastrophe. Glenson paid the promised reward. The original job was completed successfully.

The cost of victory was the axe. Rubius claimed it after it resized itself for him and filled him with peace and safety. It spoke inside his mind: **“Welcome, friend. Let's drink the light together.”** The party suspected that it was the object named in the entrance warning, but Rubius experienced it as a friendly and extremely effective weapon.

### Arc Two: Following the Kobolds — Sessions 04–05

The black-scaled kobolds had traveled far beyond the territory where Umbrail expected to find them. Rather than disband, the party chose to investigate their origin in the Balshar Mountains.

Before leaving, Umbrail communed with his ancestors and was directed toward the nearest great danger: the Balshar range. Alder communed with his abyssal patron and learned that his lost family heirloom lies in the Undershadow. The patron also identified Rubius's axe as a competitor and offered Alder a boon for removing the weapon and its wielder. Alder did not accept the murder request as an immediate objective, but the patron's hostility established that the axe belongs to a significant shadow power.

Umbrail found a written account of a similar shadow axe. The account described a cursed intelligent weapon that grows more powerful and corrupts its bearer toward allegiance to a dark god. The only identified method of stripping its power was immersion in the Shadow Well within the Undershadow. The party had clues, but not proof, that Rubius's weapon was the same artifact.

In the mountains, a fur-clad Black Fang orc attempted to stop the party while hidden allies and war boars waited in ambush. The party defeated the raiders and found their broken-fang tribal tokens. They also recovered a Flamecaller's Talisman.

The group then began a hex-based mountain search. Severe weather led to an encounter with giant wolf-like predators. Rubius fell to zero hit points during that fight and gained a wound; his character note also tracks one Shadow Point associated with carrying the axe, though the transcript does not clearly narrate the point's rules.

After the storm, the party lost the orc trail but found a desecrated Erie shrine. Kobolds had altered a Birdfolk wind deity into a scaled, snouted image. Farther on, the group discovered small quarries, black kobold scales, and signs of sustained excavation.

Scouts saw the shadow of a tremendous flying creature and found enormous claw marks on a high ledge. The creature was not identified and was explicitly presented as beyond the party's current combat ability.

Kobold tracks and iron-shod orc prints then converged without signs of a fight. The trail led to an elevated Erie outpost that had been burned, blasted, and toppled. Roughly twenty to twenty-five Erie defenders had died despite apparently expecting the assault. Orcs and kobolds used magical air-launch devices to reach a fortress normally protected by flight.

Umbrail found the mentor who first taught him magic among the dead. The party reconstructed the battle, examined the launch devices, and built separate funeral pyres for the Erie and their attackers. Umbrail knew that the Erie guarded something ancient in the region but remained bound by secrecy and overwhelmed by grief.

### Arc Three: Razor's Mountain War — Session 06

A griffin attacked the party at the ruined outpost. The group wounded it badly, destroyed the Flamecaller's Talisman while setting it Smoldering, and forced it to retreat without killing it.

Six more griffins arrived carrying blue-clad orc riders. The wounded creature indicated that the newcomers were friends. Their leader was **Zing Zow**, a childhood connection from Lil Chang's time in the mountains and a member of the **Cloud Seer** tribe.

Zing Zow explained the regional war:

- **Razor**, a dragon from the southwestern hill country, has established a mountain roost.
- Nearly all local kobolds joined him rapidly and worship him almost like a god.
- The **Black Fang orcs** allied with Razor for practical advantage rather than religious devotion.
- The **Erie and Cloud Seers** formed an alliance against him.
- Other orc tribes remain neutral and are waiting to see which side will win.

Razor cannot be treated as a normal boss encounter. He is a campaign-scale threat comparable to a level-20 monster. Multiple companies of Birdfolk and orc warriors have already gone to face him and failed to return.

The Erie protect artifacts connected to the old dragon war. Their greatest artifact is kept in the Erie capital, another lies in a vault beneath the ancient battlefield, and another is kept near the resting place of the Erie general who defeated the dragons. Lesser artifacts were entrusted to important individuals.

Umbrail's mentor had been one such bearer. Umbrail established that when he left home, he took possessions from the mentor without realizing that one of them was an Erie object of power. During Session 07 he cleansed and activated it as the **Talon of Unmaking**, a talisman that lowers armor and works especially well against dragons.

The nearby Erie mine contains ore capable of empowering a dragon's breath weapon. Zing Zow took responsibility for checking and securing it. The party then chose to focus on a task an outside group might accomplish more effectively than local rivals: persuading neutral orc tribes to join the Erie–Cloud Seer resistance.

## Central Conflict: The War for Balshar

### Razor's Publicly Known Position

The party knows that Razor is gathering followers, hunting Erie power, and building a permanent foothold. They know a direct fight is impossible and that coalition-building, denial of resources, and protection of artifacts are more realistic objectives.

### Confirmed GM Truth

The **Mountains of Unusual Scale** canvas establishes additional facts not yet fully known to the characters:

- Razor migrated from the **Meegalalor Hills** into the western Balshar range.
- He is testing whether the mountains can once again become a home for dragons.
- The Erie once controlled the entire Balshar range but have declined into a small remnant because young people leave and birthrates continue to fall.
- The Erie are not only regional guardians. They secretly protect the resting place associated with the dragon flights defeated in the ancient war for the eastern and western ranges.
- Razor's arrival is therefore a return to ancestral draconic territory, not merely a random raid for treasure.

This creates a stronger long-term motive than simple greed: Razor is attempting to reverse an ancient defeat and reestablish a draconic claim to Balshar.

### Likely Campaign Endgame

The current material supports an endgame based on several combined achievements rather than a straightforward dragon fight:

1. Unite enough Erie and orc forces to deny Razor uncontested rule.
2. Keep the breath-enhancing ore away from him.
3. Protect or relocate the Erie artifacts.
4. Learn the truth of the dragonflight resting place and ancient battlefield.
5. Discover what Razor needs from those sites.
6. Find a weakness, artifact, ritual, political fracture, or terrain advantage that can neutralize him.
7. Break the Black Fang alliance or undermine kobold worship.
8. Force Razor to leave, imprison him, strip his power, or make a coalition capable of finishing what the ancient Erie began.

None of those final methods is currently defined as canon. The campaign has established the problem and the necessary ingredients, but not a single predetermined solution.

## Things the Players Have Yet to Discover

### Confirmed Secrets in Existing GM Material

#### Razor Is Attempting a Draconic Return

The players know Razor wants control, but the GM canvas frames the move as an attempt to make Balshar a home for dragons again. His campaign is tied to the ancient war in which the Erie defeated the dragon flights, not merely to a convenient mountain lair.

#### The Erie Guard the Dragonflight Resting Place

The characters know that the Erie protect ancient artifacts and sites. They have not been clearly told that the Erie secretly guard the resting place associated with the defeated dragon flights. This is likely the deepest reason Razor needs the Erie removed.

#### The Erie Were Once an Empire

The party knows the Erie are declining. The canvas establishes that they once held superiority across the whole Balshar range and are now little more than a village and its surrounding defenses. The present war is therefore the final stage of a much older decline.

#### The Rock Scale Kobolds Have a Specific Identity

The canvas identifies the mountain kobolds as the **Rock Scale** tribe: small, evil, cowardly, and completely devoted to Razor since his arrival. The characters have not heard that formal tribe name in the recorded sessions.

#### The Black Fang–Erie Blood Feud Predates Razor

The canvas establishes the **Black Fang** as a cave- and tunnel-dwelling tribe that historically avoided the open because the Erie could prey on them from the air. The two peoples have maintained a blood feud for generations. Black Fang warriors wear broken fangs as tribal tokens. This history helps explain why they allied with Razor even though they do not worship or fully trust the dragon.

### Partially Revealed Truths That Still Need Payoff

#### The Axe's Remaining Secrets

Session 08 made the corruption undeniable. The axe consumed three slain kobolds and one shadow minion, replaced Rubius's decision about the beehive, turned his rage indiscriminate, and caused him to attack Alder. Tempest Command and repeated Dispel Magic suppressed active surges without cleansing the artifact. The campaign still needs to reveal:

- The complete trigger list and thresholds for Shadow Points.
- Whether the axe causes enlargement, emotional dependence, allegiance, or physical transformation.
- Whether Snaggletooth's “ascension” was the axe's standard effect or a special ritual.
- Why the axe regards Alder's patron as a rival.
- How and why it returned after being thrown over the cliff.
- What dark god or shadow power it serves.

Rubius has **three tracked Shadow Points**: one from previously dropping to zero hit points, one for taking the hive under the axe's influence, and one for attacking Alder.

#### The Shadow Well

The party openly discussed the Shadow Well after Rubius attacked Alder, but has not found the Undershadow entrance, the well, or the cost of using it. It is not established whether depowering the axe will destroy it, free its intelligence, transfer its curse, or require a sacrifice.

#### Alder's Heirloom

Alder told the group that the rot-heirloom lies somewhere below in the Undershadow and that he senses a distant route toward it. The players do not know:

- Who took it or how it reached the Undershadow.
- Whether it is still a benign agricultural artifact.
- Whether its accelerated decay is connected to necrotic power, the Shadow Well, or Alder's patron.
- Whether the patron wants Alder to recover it, corrupt it, or use it as leverage.

#### Umbrail's Lesser Erie Artifact

The artifact is now the **Talon of Unmaking**, a slotless talisman that allows Umbrail to spend 2 mana after a damaging spell to lower an armored target's armor by one step. Its duration is longer against dragons. It remains unknown whether the mentor meant Umbrail to inherit it, whether Razor can detect it, and how the Erie will respond to Umbrail's possession.

#### General Brand's Circuit

Brand repeatedly travels through the eastern Balshar Mountains for weeks. No source currently defines why. Possible connections include artifact inspections, ward maintenance, troop coordination, the general's tomb site, the ancient battlefield, or a private agenda. These are possibilities, not established answers.

#### The Missing Warrior Companies

Mixed groups of Erie and orcs went to confront Razor and did not return. It is unknown whether they died, were captured, defected, became enthralled, or discovered something that prevented their return.

#### The Air-Launch Technology

The party understands the devices' general function but not their creator, source, or limits. It remains unclear whether they were built by the Black Fang, supplied by Razor, stolen from the Erie, or recovered from an ancient site.

#### The Huge Flying Creature

The party saw only a shadow and enormous claw marks. The creature has not been identified. It may be Razor, another dragon, a giant mountain predator, or something unrelated. No current note confirms the answer.

### Optional Preparation Not Yet Canon

The character notes include unused preparation for additional **Cloud Seer** members named **Kora Skybreak**, **Jun Ashstep**, and **Elder Mauk**. Those characters have not appeared in played canon. Session 06 established **Zing Zow** and the Cloud Seers as Lil Chang's mountain connection.

This additional Cloud Seer material can still be used in one of three ways:

1. Introduce Kora, Jun, and Mauk as members of another Cloud Seer settlement or patrol.
2. Use their names and conflicts to deepen Lil Chang's history with Zing Zow's people.
3. Retire any unused elements that duplicate Zing Zow's established role.

Until those characters appear, Kora, Jun, Mauk, Lil Chang's old Cloud Seer nickname, and the wind-harness rescue remain **optional hooks**, not undiscovered facts.

Session 08 preparation also defines **Three-Bells Basin**, the Frostvein waystation **Rimehook**, Rock Scale engineer **Tikka-of-the-Cairns**, and a manufactured avalanche. Vekka intends the Black Fang to arrive as rescuers; Gorak knows only that a crisis is likely; Tikka designed a limited slide and does not know Vekka expanded it toward mass casualties. None of that entered Session 08 play. Preserve it as GM truth for the route ahead, not as player knowledge.

The campaign canvas also lists possible encounters—an owlbear, an orc patrol, a kobold excavation crew, three lions, wargs, a griffin, and a dragon shadow. The warg, griffin, and shadow beats have already been used. The remaining entries are encounter options, not obligations or established off-screen events.

## Sessions 07–08 Update — The Race for the Neutral Tribes

Session 07 moved the campaign from strategic planning into an immediate diplomatic race. Zing Zow identified three neutral tribes and their chiefs:

- The **Frostvein**, high-peak orcs led by the cool and level-headed **An Yue**
- The **Graven Hand**, religious keepers of the orc burial grounds led by **Bao Jun**
- The subterranean **Hollow Drum**, who communicate through drums and are led by the action-oriented **Chen Wei**

The tribes are not actively hostile toward one another, but they share longstanding resentment of the Erie. The Frostvein settlement is nearest the northern ford and became the party's first destination.

During the journey, the party peacefully withdrew from wild wargs protecting injured pups. Near the river, a riderless Cloud Seer griffon named **Quickfeather** led Lil Chang to its dying rider, **Da Long**. Lil Chang revived the scout, who reported that Black Fang diplomats and an escort of approximately fifteen to twenty orcs were already traveling toward the Frostvein.

Lil Chang and the scout flew ahead on Quickfeather. The rest of the party took secret paths and began considering ways to delay the delegation. The Black Fang group is conducting recruitment; the party has no evidence that it intends to attack the village.

In Session 08, the land party found and recruited **Scritch**, the kobold it had captured and released near Clackston. Scritch had escaped the Black Fang column after being injured and mistreated. He identified five diplomats under **Gorak** and fifteen warriors aligned with **Vekka**, all traveling beneath a peace banner. He suspects an undisclosed objective but lacks proof.

Seven kobolds ambushed the party. The group defeated them, but Rubius's axe consumed three of the attackers and drove him to attack Alder. Umbrail identified active soul consumption and progressive corruption, while Alder disclosed that his patron wants Rubius killed and the axe taken. Rubius now has three tracked Shadow Points.

At the Stone Stairs, the party climbed above the Black Fang camp without detection and reached a stormbound Frostvein plateau. The prepared Three-Bells Basin avalanche did not enter play.

At the ruined outpost, Umbrail also confirmed, cleansed, and activated the **Talon of Unmaking**. Alder communed with Erabose, who remains displeased about an unfulfilled command. Rubius's safe rest healed his wound, and he recharged his True Strike Wand.

## Continuity Status

### Resolved: Black Fang Identity

- **Canonical name:** Black Fang, as established in Session 06.
- **Tribal marker:** Warriors carry broken fangs taken from various creatures.
- **Older preparation:** Any earlier alternate Fang name refers to this same tribe.

The mountain-pass raiders, the cave-dwelling orcs with the Erie blood feud, and Razor's allied orc clan are one faction: the **Black Fang**.

### Resolved: Cloud Seer Identity

**Cloud Seer** is the canonical tribe name, and **Zing Zow** is played canon. Kora Skybreak, Jun Ashstep, and Elder Mauk are optional, unrevealed Cloud Seer NPCs rather than members of a separate tribe.

### Partly Resolved: Axe Rules

The played transcript describes the Axe of Snaggletooth as dealing **3d4** rather than a normal battleaxe's **1d8**. The older Warren canvas lists **2d4+2**. The transcript and character sheet should control current mechanics unless deliberately revised.

Session 08 establishes **three current Shadow Points** and shows two new awards: one for yielding to the axe over the beehive and one for attacking Alder. The full threshold and progression rules still need a single written reference before the next trigger.

### Current Levels

Session 06 explicitly advances the party to **level 3**. Several PC notes still say level 2 and “likely approaching level 3.” Those notes are now stale.

### Razor's Roost and Origin

Session 06 places Razor's roost in the **southern Balshar Mountains**, while the GM canvas places him in the **Western Balshar Mountain Range**. These can be reconciled as the southwestern portion of the range, or one location should be selected as canon before exact travel distances matter.

The name **Meegalalor Hills** appears only in the GM canvas and should receive a spelling confirmation before being presented to the players.

### Resolved: Umbrail's Artifact

Umbrail's lesser Erie artifact is the **Talon of Unmaking**. He cleansed and activated it during Session 07. It reduces an armored target's armor by one step after Umbrail damages it with a spell; the duration is longer against dragons.

### In-Character Knowledge

Session 08 confirms that the whole land party discussed Alder's murder order, the Shadow Well account, the axe's returning bond, Alder's heirloom, and the curse's active effects. Umbrail openly used his knowledge to explain the danger.

Still confirm whether Lil Chang and Whisper receive that information when the party reunites, and whether all characters know Umbrail carries the Talon of Unmaking or the exact locations of the three greater Erie artifacts.
