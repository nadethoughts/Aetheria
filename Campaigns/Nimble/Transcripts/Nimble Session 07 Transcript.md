## 1. At the Ruined Erie Outpost

The session opened with a recap of the party's encounter with the griffin and its riders.

**GM:** You defeated the griffin, but it was still alive. About six more griffins approached with orcs riding them. One rider recognized Lil Chang. His name was [[World/Characters/Zing Zow|Zing Zow]], and he was of the [[World/Factions/Cloud Seers|Cloud Seer]] tribe.

The party had learned that several orc tribes lived in the mountains, but only two had chosen sides in the conflict.

- The Cloud Seers had allied with the [[World/Factions/Erie|Erie]] against [[World/Characters/Razor|Razor]].
- The [[World/Factions/Black Fang Orcs|Black Fang]] had allied with Razor.
- The mountain kobolds had also joined the dragon.
- The remaining orc tribes were still neutral.

The party's chosen objective was to win the neutral tribes over before Razor could.

**Alder:** I do not think there are dragons underground. We could just go underground.

The group acknowledged that the mine was another option. It held a special ore that could affect a dragon's breath weapon, but Zing Zow and his griffin riders had offered to secure it.

**GM:** They are planning to secure the mine. That was what the Erie outpost was guarding. You had discussed going to find the rest of the orc tribes.

The party also revisited the Erie artifacts and General Brand's unexplained circuit through the mountains. At least three major artifacts were hidden at separate sites. Umbrail suspected that he might have taken another artifact from his mentor without recognizing it.

## 2. A Safe Rest

It was late enough that the Cloud Seers waited until first light before leaving for the mine. The ruined outpost was damaged, but the party was protected by the griffin riders.

**GM:** Considering that you are in an Erie outpost and are being guarded by the Cloud Seer griffin riders, I would qualify this night as a safe rest.

The table reviewed the rest rules:

- A **safe rest** restores health, mana, hit dice, and other normal resources.
- A **field rest** does not refresh everything, but a hit die spent during it grants maximum healing.
- A safe rest heals one wound.

Rubius had one wound, so the safe rest reduced his wounds to zero.

Rubius also buried his True Strike Wand at the battlefield overnight, satisfying its recharge condition.

## 3. Alder Communes with Erabose

During the safe rest, Alder communed with his patron, [[World/Religion/Deities/Erabose|Erabose]].

The group clarified Erabose's identity and symbol. Erabose is a fallen and malevolent power whose crest is a murder of crows consuming the sun.

**GM:** The general feeling you get from Erabose is that he is still waiting for you to do what he asked. He is not particularly happy that you have chosen to defy him, but you are still proving yourself useful at the moment.

**Alder:** Not defy. It just has not been convenient.

No new bargain was made. Erabose's original demand remained outstanding.

## 4. Umbrail Recovers the Talon of Unmaking

Umbrail searched the outpost's remaining books for information about the Erie–dragon war and the object he might have taken from his mentor. The outpost had no true library, but it did contain a small bookshelf with local volumes.

Umbrail used his lore and access to the books to investigate three questions:

1. Had he taken an important magical artifact?
2. How serious was the theft?
3. What did he need to do to repair the situation?

With a strong result, Umbrail confirmed that he had taken an Erie artifact. It was not one of the three major artifacts, but it was significant.

The text also gave him the incantations necessary to cleanse and reactivate it.

Five possible forms were considered:

- **Feather of Elemental Discord**
- **Crown of the High Sky**
- **Eye of the Rock Sage**
- **Sky-Binding Focus**
- **Talon of Unmaking**

Umbrail chose the **Talon of Unmaking**, a talisman useful against armor:

> [!item] Talon of Unmaking
> When Umbrail's spell damages an armored creature, he may spend 2 mana to reduce its armor by one step until the start of its next turn. Against a dragon, the reduction lasts until the end of the dragon's next turn.

The artifact does not occupy an equipment slot. It functions as a carried talisman that Umbrail can activate when its conditions are met.

## 5. The Three Neutral Tribes

At dawn, Zing Zow approached Lil Chang as the Cloud Seers prepared to leave.

**Zing Zow:** The path you have chosen is not an easy one. There are three tribes that you must win over. None of them will be easy.

He named the three tribes:

### Frostvein Orcs

The [[World/Factions/Frostvein Orcs|Frostvein]] live among the high mountain peaks where the air is cold and crisp. Their chieftess is [[World/Characters/An Yue|An Yue]], known for her cool demeanor and level-headedness.

Their settlement is the closest of the three to the ford across the gorge.

### Graven Hand Orcs

The [[World/Factions/Graven Hand Orcs|Graven Hand]] are a more religious tribe and oversee the burial grounds of the mountain orcs. Their chief is [[World/Characters/Bao Jun|Bao Jun]], whom Zing Zow described as unusual, much like the tribe itself.

### Hollow Drum Orcs

The [[World/Factions/Hollow Drum Orcs|Hollow Drum]] are subterranean orcs who live in caverns and underground halls. They specialize in long-distance communication using drums. Their chief is [[World/Characters/Chen Wei|Chen Wei]], a person of action whom Zing Zow was surprised had not yet chosen a side.

The three neutral tribes were not actively hostile toward one another. Their greater obstacle was a longstanding resentment of the Erie.

**Zing Zow:** There is a reason the Erie have no presence in the eastern Balshar Mountains. These tribes pushed them out many years ago. Your Erie friend may have a harder time in the negotiations.

He clarified that Umbrail could still earn their trust. His ancestry created an uphill battle, not an automatic refusal.

Zing Zow believed all three tribes would eventually be needed to defeat the Black Fang, the Rock Scale kobolds, and Razor.

Before departing, he promised to send a signal once the mine was secured.

## 6. The Road to the Ford

The party considered using Cloud Seer griffins to shorten the journey. Zing Zow said a griffin or two could be spared, but the animals were uncomfortable around Rubius and Alder.

Rubius had a troubling presence that unsettled them, while Alder also seemed wrong to their instincts. The other party members could potentially ride while the rest caught up by land.

The group chose to remain together and travel toward the northern ford. Although the Hollow Drum settlement was closer by air, the Frostvein were closest by the practical land route.

Zing Zow warned that the mountains themselves were treacherous and full of predators, though he believed the party capable of surviving the crossing.

After several days of easier downhill travel, the party found large wolf-like tracks along a game trail.

Umbrail flew ahead to scout while another party member examined the tracks. Umbrail saw a pack of wargs traveling in the same direction as the party.

## 7. The Warg Family

Closer observation showed that the wargs were wild rather than trained:

- They wore no tack or barding.
- Their fur showed no signs of saddles.
- They were moving slowly because several pups were injured.
- The adults were protecting the young.

The party discussed waiting, leaving the trail, or finding a route through the forest. They chose to navigate around the pack.

The detour went poorly. After roughly an hour and a half of difficult travel, the party emerged from the trees directly into the warg pack.

The adults closed around the injured pups and growled, but they did not immediately attack.

Umbrail used **Tempest's Command**, amplifying his voice to a thunderous volume. Rubius lifted a boulder to make the group appear even more dangerous. The wargs backed up and tightened their protective formation.

Whisper assessed the situation and determined that the pack would not attack as long as the party did not advance aggressively.

The party withdrew. Rubius threw the boulder into the trees as a final display of strength, then followed the others out of the clearing.

The wargs did not pursue. For the rest of the day, both groups continued in the same direction while keeping their distance.

> [!success] Encounter outcome
> No combat occurred. The party recognized that the wargs were protecting injured young, intimidated them without attacking, and left the family alive.

## 8. Quickfeather's Plea

The party reached the broad river gorge and began crossing the open ground toward the ford. A flying creature appeared in the distance and approached without obvious aggression.

It was a riderless griffin wearing Cloud Seer tack and barding.

The griffin landed near the group, seized Lil Chang's clothing, and tried to pull him back in the direction from which it had come.

Through translation, the griffin communicated that its friend needed help.

Lil Chang mounted the griffin. It flew him across the gorge to a rocky outcrop where an unconscious Cloud Seer scout lay sprawled on the stone. The rest of the party traveled toward the ford and caught up as quickly as possible.

The scout was not breathing. Black-tipped, barbed arrows in his body identified his attackers as Black Fang orcs.

Lil Chang used one of the party's foul-tasting healing potions and performed chest compressions. The scout began breathing and regained consciousness.

**Scout:** What did you put in my mouth?

**Lil Chang:** Poison. It is disgusting, but it works.

The griffon that fetched Lil Chang was **Quickfeather**, the companion of the Cloud Seer scout **Da Long**.

## 9. The Black Fang Delegation

The revived scout, **Da Long**, explained that the Black Fang were farther east than the Cloud Seers had expected.

He had discovered a Black Fang group traveling toward the neutral tribes. They noticed him before he could escape and shot him down.

The force was not the entire Black Fang clan. It was a full platoon of approximately fifteen to twenty orcs accompanying an entourage of diplomats.

Their purpose was to persuade the neutral tribes to join Razor's side.

**Scout:** Having the neutral tribes or not could be the difference between winning the war and losing it.

He warned that the mountain tribes were proud and would not simply be bought. The Black Fang group was heading toward the Frostvein settlement, the closest neutral village to the ford.

The scout believed Black Fang war pigs might be sent to finish him off, though none appeared before the party departed.

## 10. Racing to the Frostvein

The party debated whether to confront the Black Fang delegation, travel to a different tribe, or send someone ahead.

The Black Fang had a lead, but they were traveling as a group of fifteen to twenty. Quickfeather could carry Lil Chang and the scout by a faster route.

The scout knew secret mountain paths that would help the rest of the party follow. The paths were not enough to overtake the delegation on foot, but the party might travel alongside it, split off part of the force, or slow it down.

Lil Chang was chosen to go ahead because he was an orc and had the best chance of receiving an immediate hearing.

**Lil Chang:** The two of us can run ahead and talk to the clan. We can tell them that this group did not just come to recruit them—they shot you out of the sky.

The scout agreed to ride with him.

**Scout:** Quickfeather is faster than the entourage. We should be able to get there with some time to spare.

Before leaving, the scout gave the rest of the party directions through the mountain paths. Lil Chang and the scout mounted Quickfeather and flew toward the Frostvein settlement.

The remaining party members discussed slowing the Black Fang delegation without making it appear that the delegation intended an attack. Their tentative idea was to lure small groups away or otherwise create delays, giving Lil Chang time to begin negotiations.

No final plan for dealing with the Black Fang platoon was settled before the session ended.
