## 1. Howls on the Plateau

The session opened at the top of the [[World/Locations/Stone Stairs|Stone Stairs]]. Umbrail, Alder, Rubius, Rowan, and [[World/Characters/Scritch|Scritch]] had climbed past the Black Fang delegation while it camped below. Snow swept the Frostvein plateau, but the party could still look back over the edge and see the column's camp.

Howls sounded through the wind behind them.

A Black Fang patrol approached with enormous, horse-sized wargs. Their markings distinguished them from the Frostvein and connected them to the faction camped below. The encounter escalated into combat before either group attempted a parley.

Rubius was already worried about repeating the uncontrolled rage that had turned him against Alder. He tried to center himself before entering another rage, but failed the Willpower check. He chose to rage anyway. Although he initially kept his hands away from the wrapped axe and fought with other weapons, the artifact's bond did not depend on physical contact.

Alder summoned two shadow minions. Scritch and Rowan moved forward carefully, while Umbrail took to the air above the plateau.

## 2. Lightning and Black Fang Steel

Umbrail opened the battle with **Arc Lightning**, chaining electricity between the patrol's armored fighters. He activated the [[World/Items/Artifacts/Talon of Unmaking|Talon of Unmaking]] to crack and weaken their armor after his spells struck.

The patrol included a veteran, archers, a heavy shield-bearer, and war wargs. Their attacks immediately demonstrated why the Black Fang had sent them into the storm. An arrow struck Umbrail in flight, forcing him to conjure a Frost Shield. The shield-bearer slammed into the front line, while the veteran crossed the battlefield and delivered a rapid series of punishing attacks.

Rubius interposed himself against the veteran's heaviest blow and used his Mountainheart resilience and Fury to absorb part of it. The wargs tried to isolate and maul the smaller members of the party, while the archers continued firing from a distance.

Umbrail cast Arc Lightning again. The overloaded shield-bearer's protection finally exploded inward, driving fragments into the orc and killing him. The Talon continued opening weaknesses in the patrol's armor.

Alder's shadows swarmed one of the wargs. He followed their attacks with a concentrated Shadow Blast that caved in the creature's chest through rapid necrotic decay.

Scritch used the confusion exactly as a Cheat should. His first sling stone went wide, but he turned the second opening into a critical strike against an exposed Black Fang fighter.

## 3. The Axe Takes Another Soul

The Black Fang veteran remained standing after Umbrail's lightning, but only barely.

Rubius's anger overcame his effort to keep the shadow axe wrapped. He reached for it almost without realizing what he was doing and attacked the veteran.

The axe offered him a bargain: accept another Shadow Point and increase its damage after a critical hit. Rubius accepted.

The veteran died beneath the empowered blow. His body became black sludge and flowed into the blade. The axe pulsed with dark energy and took on a sharper, more wicked shape.

Unlike the previous battle, Rubius did not turn on an ally when the axe fed. He stayed engaged with the Black Fang patrol. The restraint did not make the bargain free, however. The axe had taken another soul, and Rubius had voluntarily accepted more of its power.

Later in the fight, Rubius critically struck a warg and accepted the artifact's escalating offer a second time. The next axe attack rose to 3d8. He was also explicitly wounded during the encounter, though no additional Shadow Point for that wound was announced at the table.

## 4. One Scout Escapes

The second warg seized Rowan and shook him in its jaws. Rowan drove his spear upward through the roof of its mouth and killed it while still caught between its teeth.

Rubius, Alder, Scritch, Rowan, and Umbrail continued breaking the patrol's remaining line. When the surviving Black Fang scouts recognized that the battle was lost, they fled across the plateau.

The party struck one runner as he withdrew. Combined attacks opened his legs and brought him down in a pool of blood after only a few more steps.

The last scout was already too far away. He escaped into the snow.

As the fight ended, Alder's summoned creatures melted back into the shadows. The blackness receded from Rubius's eyes, and the immediate fury passed. One Black Fang survivor now had a route back toward the delegation, making the party's earlier secret passage at the Stone Stairs a temporary advantage rather than a lasting one.

The scene then cut away before the land party resumed its journey.

## 5. Lil Chang Reaches Icehold

Lil Chang and [[World/Characters/Da Long|Da Long]] had flown ahead on [[World/Characters/Quickfeather|Quickfeather]]. From the air, Lil Chang watched forest become foothills, foothills become mountain, and mountain become a frozen tundra plateau. An ice labyrinth spread across part of the ground below.

Frostvein scouts watched the sky from stone towers along the approach. Rather than fly directly over their positions and appear hostile, Da Long landed near an outer watch post.

The senior warden there introduced himself as [[World/Characters/Feng Rui|Feng Rui]]. He recognized Da Long as a Cloud Seer and regarded Lil Chang as an unknown outlander.

Lil Chang dismounted and kept his weapons lowered.

**Feng Rui:** What are you doing here?

**Lil Chang:** We have come to seek partnership against the dragon. Have you seen how it circles and terrorizes the tribes?

Feng Rui knew of Razor but did not pretend the Frostvein had already judged him an enemy. Lil Chang warned that Razor was gathering the mountain tribes to conquer the range and that Frostvein independence would not survive the dragon's victory.

He asked to speak with the Frostvein leader. Feng Rui agreed to escort both visitors to [[World/Locations/Icehold|Icehold]].

## 6. Before the Chieftess

Icehold lay several hours beyond the outpost. It was a substantial but austere settlement in which every structure and tool appeared to have a practical purpose. A central great hall served as the seat of tribal government.

On the way, Feng Rui gathered two additional members of the Frostvein council: [[World/Characters/Jian Bo|Jian Bo]], the quartermaster responsible for food stores and supplies, and [[World/Characters/Mei Rong|Mei Rong]], the tribe's lore keeper.

The visitors entered the great hall and found Chieftess [[World/Characters/An Yue|An Yue]] seated on a large chair built from tusks and bone and covered in carefully arranged furs.

Feng Rui presented the travelers and their warning. Lil Chang made the coalition's case plainly: Razor and the Black Fang could defeat the divided tribes one at a time, while a united orc front supported by the Erie and Cloud Seers might preserve their independence.

An Yue and her council immediately challenged the premise. Frostvein had fought for generations to free itself from Erie authority. Replacing a dragon's domination with the restoration of the old mountain hierarchy would not count as freedom.

**Mei Rong:** We have been here before. Alliances have been formed over dire threats. When the fires have burned low, it is us who have been at the bottom.

Da Long acknowledged that he had been spying on the Black Fang column. He showed the wound they had given him when they shot him from the sky and refused to disguise what had happened as a misunderstanding.

## 7. The Price of Resistance

The council asked what the coalition actually wanted. Lil Chang answered that it needed bodies—warriors willing to oppose Razor—and that he would share the danger rather than asking Frostvein to bleed in his place.

That answer exposed the first obstacle.

Frostvein was approaching winter in a precarious position. Its people were needed to hunt, tend stores, and keep the passes open. Every warrior sent away would be one fewer worker keeping the settlement alive. Jian Bo had no intention of exchanging real supplies and labor for a speech about honor.

The Frostvein did not trust the Black Fang's motives, but neither would they reject a peace-bannered delegation based only on rumor. An Yue stated that the approaching envoys would receive peace until they gave Frostvein a reason to withdraw it. Da Long asked only that the tribe take every precaution and watch them closely.

He then offered something immediate. Through his work with Quickfeather, Da Long knew of overlooked hunting grounds near Frostvein territory—farther from the usual routes, but fertile enough to improve the tribe's winter position. He gave the location freely as proof of good faith.

An Yue's council recognized that the information would not double their stores or solve the winter outright, but it could materially improve their chances.

## 8. Terms Before Allegiance

The discussion shifted from whether Frostvein should trust the visitors to what an acceptable agreement would require.

Lil Chang explained that the Erie possess artifacts designed to fight dragons. He hoped those weapons could make the war decisive rather than prolonged. Mei Rong answered that victory over Razor did not resolve what came afterward. Frostvein would require written and signed assurances that it would not find itself beneath the Erie's talon again once the common enemy was gone.

Lil Chang and Da Long could not bind Erie or Cloud Seer leadership to a formal treaty. They could arrange a meeting with people who held that authority. The council treated that distinction as important rather than evasive.

Several possible terms emerged:

- Formal recognition of Frostvein independence and control of its passes.
- Specific limits on soldiers, supplies, and access rather than an unlimited levy.
- Material support sufficient to protect the settlement through winter.
- Exchange of route knowledge and military specializations.
- Training for Frostvein defenders without placing them beneath foreign command.
- A coalition agreement that ends occupation rights when the war ends.

Frostvein had fewer troops available than outsiders assumed, but its knowledge of the high passes could be more valuable than raw numbers. Da Long offered his own scouting, hunting, and animal-handling knowledge freely. The Frostvein considered whether information and training might form the first stage of cooperation while larger terms were negotiated.

The council also asked the visitors to be present when the Black Fang made its case. If the approaching delegation was as violent and deceptive as Da Long claimed, Frostvein wanted to test both stories in the same room.

## 9. Hospitality and an Adjournment

An Yue did not declare an alliance.

She said she would confer privately with her advisors and wait for both the rest of Lil Chang's unnamed company and the Black Fang delegation to arrive. In the meantime, Icehold extended its hospitality, fire, and shelter to Lil Chang, Da Long, and Quickfeather.

Da Long asked whether he should begin the long flight home to request a properly authorized delegation. An Yue encouraged him to contact his superiors as soon as he could. He planned to remain for the night and leave the following morning.

Lil Chang agreed to stay in Icehold until the rest of the party arrived.

When An Yue asked what his group called itself, Lil Chang admitted that it had no name. He promised the companions would vote on one after they reunited.

The session ended with two races still unfinished. A Black Fang scout was fleeing the battlefield toward his people, while the Black Fang diplomats continued toward a council that had agreed to hear them. Lil Chang had opened the door at Icehold, but only a binding agreement—and the conduct of everyone arriving behind him—could decide which way Frostvein would walk through it.
