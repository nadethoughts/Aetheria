## 1. Delaying the Delegation

The session resumed with the land party on the frozen plateau, above the Black Fang diplomatic party's camp. The party had defeated most of the Black Fang scouts, but one had escaped into the snow. The delegation below traveled under a banner of peace, leaving the party to weigh the risk of delaying them against the danger of being blamed for an attack.

The group decided against poisoning the nearby water or causing an avalanche. The maintained route and rocky cliff made an avalanche difficult to produce, and poisoning the water could harm the Frostvein as well as the Black Fang. Instead, they worked to make the switchbacks harder to climb. They packed snow onto the path and melted it with magic to create an icy stretch, then felled a tree to obstruct the road.

With night falling and the party already tired, they moved away from the exposed route and looked for shelter. They found a cave and set a watch. Rowan noticed a pair of eyes in the darkness and heard a quiet sound from deeper inside.

## 2. The Owlbear in the Cave

A large, quadrupedal owl-like creature emerged from the cave. Rowan raised the alarm and drew its attention while the others woke. Umbrail struck it with a Flame Dart, but the creature answered with a savage screech that dealt 14 damage to everyone, ignoring armor.

Rubius was reduced to zero hit points and took a Wound rather than falling unconscious. The Axe of Snaggletooth let him enter a rage for free, but he had only one action while he remained below zero. He used that action to take a caustic brew and recover hit points, though the potion left him poisoned for several rounds.

Alder summoned shadow minions and used Umbrail's extra action to direct them against the creature. The group protected the injured Rubius and kept the creature occupied. Umbrail then used **Voice of Thunder** through Tempest's Command, making his voice boom to frighten it. The creature fled into the darkness before it could screech again.

The party considered whether to lure it toward the Black Fang camp, but could not be sure how it would react or whether the plan would work. They moved to a higher, more sheltered ledge within the cave and finished the night without another encounter. This was a field rest, not a safe rest.

## 3. Across the Tundra

At first, the group considered using the creature—or the bodies left from the earlier battle—to create trouble for the Black Fang delegation. They decided the effort and uncertainty were not worthwhile and continued toward Icehold instead.

Umbrail flew up to check the route. By midday, the delegation had not yet climbed onto the plateau, suggesting the party's obstacles had delayed it. He also spotted several distant bipedal figures across the tundra, too far away to identify. The party noticed small, four-toed tracks crossing their path, but could not determine what had made them.

They traveled until late afternoon and found an ice outcropping that offered some shelter for the night. A watch passed without incident. The party saw an owlbear at a distance, but it did not approach.

## 4. The Ice Labyrinth

The next day, the party reached an enormous ice wall that hid the approach to Icehold. From above, Umbrail could see an ice labyrinth beyond it. He studied the maze from the air and committed its layout to memory, then sketched it for the others before they approached the entrance. Movement along the top of the wall suggested that someone was watching them.

The maze's ice blocks carried protective frost magic. Umbrail also sensed a broader magical effect in the air inside. As the group followed the route he remembered, that magic interfered with their perception. He used Tempest's Command at the moments he recognized the effect, helping the party keep its bearings. They spent the rest of the day crossing the labyrinth and reached the far side without becoming lost.

A Frostvein warden in white furs and a white dire-wolf headdress waited near the exit. He asked what business the travelers had with the tribe. The party explained that it came in peace, on behalf of the Erie alliance and the Cloud Seers, and hoped to join its companions who had arrived ahead of them.

The warden challenged their connection to the Cloud Seers and asked what part they had in the conflict. One traveler answered that the dragon's threat reached all the mountain tribes, and that he stood against tyranny alongside his friends. The warden warned them not to bring war to Frostvein's borders.

**Traveler:** War is at your borders whether you want it to be or not.

**Frostvein warden:** Then we will allow you passage to the halls of Icehold.

Other Frostvein guards emerged from concealed doors in the wall and revealed that the warden had not been alone. They escorted the party through the gate toward Icehold.

## 5. At the Icehold Hall

Icehold's buildings combined structures grown from snow with rooms carved into impressive ice sculptures. Statues of ancient orc warriors stood near the entrance. In the main square, a fountain sent snow rising and falling into water that melted and returned to the fountain.

The warden brought the party to the great hall, where Lil Chang was already waiting and eating with the Frostvein. Chieftess [[World/Characters/An Yue|An Yue]] stood at the head of the hall with her advisors. She welcomed the travelers and said she had heard they had proposals for the tribe.

The recording ended there. The negotiations between the land party and the Frostvein had not yet begun.

## Session-End State

- One Black Fang scout had escaped after the previous session's battle. The group did not know whether he reached the delegation or what he would report.
- An owlbear-like creature attacked the party in a cave and fled after Umbrail frightened it with Voice of Thunder.
- Rubius was reduced to zero hit points, took a Wound, and recovered with a caustic brew. He remained poisoned for several rounds; he did not fall unconscious.
- The party crossed the magically warded ice labyrinth with Umbrail navigating from memory and countering effects on their perception with Tempest's Command.
- The Frostvein warden granted the party passage after confirming its stated peaceful intent.
- The land party reunited with Lil Chang in Icehold's great hall, where Chieftess An Yue and her advisors were waiting to hear their proposals.
- No alliance or terms were agreed in this session. Negotiations were set to begin in the next session.
