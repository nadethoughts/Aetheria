## 1. Into the Secret Trail

The session opened with a recap. Lil Chang had revived the Cloud Seer scout [[World/Characters/Da Long|Da Long]], who had been shot down by Black Fang arrows while observing a diplomatic column bound for the Frostvein. Lil Chang and Da Long had flown ahead on [[World/Characters/Quickfeather|Quickfeather]]. The rest of the party intended to use Da Long's secret route to catch and delay the column.

The hidden game trail entered a dense pine forest. The canopy turned daylight into twilight, and the path repeatedly vanished beneath old growth before reappearing farther ahead. Without Da Long's directions, the party would not have found or followed it.

Near a clearing, the group found scraps of black cloth caught in brambles. The weave resembled Black Fang clothing, suggesting that someone connected to the delegation had passed through the woods.

The party initially prepared an ambush. Rubius searched for wildlife that could create a distraction and, with a strong Nature Craft result, found a beehive within half an hour. Umbrail took position in a tree near a small stream while the others considered traps and ways to draw orcs away from the column.

When they finally observed the clearing, however, there were no orcs in sight—only a lone kobold shivering beneath a Black Fang blanket.

## 2. A Familiar Kobold

The kobold was the same one the party had captured in Session 01 while he tried to steal an uncooperative mule near Clackston. He was still suffering from an injured leg and looked abandoned rather than dangerous.

Rowan approached openly and without a drawn weapon. The kobold tried to charm his way through the reunion, but quickly admitted that the Black Fang were no longer his friends.

**Scritch:** They left me high and dry. I was wrestling with this injured leg, and they just up and left me. I'm stranded here.

Rowan gave him a healing draught. It tasted terrible but restored enough strength for the kobold to travel.

Rubius joined the conversation and recognized the small thief he had tackled outside Clackston. Despite some initial suspicion from the group, the kobold did not try to lure them anywhere or flee.

**Rowan:** We've met before, haven't we?

**Scritch:** I'm still feeling the last time we crossed paths.

When asked his name, the kobold introduced himself as **Scritch**. The party invited him to accompany them, and he accepted—partly for safety and partly because he wanted revenge on the people who had discarded him.

## 3. Scritch's Account of the Black Fang

Scritch had returned to the Balshar Mountains after the party released him. With nowhere else to go, he joined Razor's kobolds despite his reservations about the dragon. He later traveled with the Black Fang column until his injury slowed them down. After they began mistreating him as dead weight, he slipped into the woods during a rest, became lost, and took shelter in the clearing.

He confirmed that the column contained **twenty orcs**:

- Five were diplomats.
- Fifteen were warriors or raiders.
- The military contingent appeared to answer to Quartermaster [[World/Characters/Vekka Ash-Eye|Vekka]].
- The diplomats were led by Envoy [[World/Characters/Gorak Broken-Tusk|Gorak]].

The orcs carried a peace banner and were openly traveling to negotiate with the Frostvein. Scritch had not seen obvious tension between the diplomatic and military groups, and he had not witnessed any of them casting spells. The group included both heavily armored warriors and more mobile fighters.

Scritch nevertheless believed something about the mission was hidden. He did not know the secret objective, but he had noticed that Vekka held authority over the warriors while Gorak directed the diplomats.

**Scritch:** Their stated goal is to make a treaty with the Frostvein, but there seems to be something else going on. I don't know what it is.

The column had left Scritch roughly two hours earlier and was skirting the tree line in the same direction as the party.

## 4. Rubius and the Beehive

Before leaving, Rubius attempted to recruit the bees he had found. His affinity with wild creatures allowed him to communicate with them, but the colony did not want its hive moved. The bees were preparing for winter and needed their home to survive.

Rubius initially decided to respect their wishes. Then the [[World/Items/Artifacts/Axe of Snaggletooth|Axe of Snaggletooth]] pressed against his judgment.

His eyes flashed black. He seized the branch holding the hive and carried it away while the unhappy bees followed. The GM assigned Rubius a Shadow Point for yielding to the axe's influence.

The party continued along the hidden trail with Scritch and the displaced hive. As they walked, Scritch explained the Black Fang column's composition and his escape.

## 5. The Kobold Ambush

While the group was absorbed in conversation, seven kobolds emerged from the trees and attacked. The ambushers included lightly armored slingers, an alchemist carrying traps and a bee-filled bomb, and a heavily armored Clanger who drove the others into a frenzy by striking two cleavers together.

The party began the fight at a disadvantage because no one noticed the ambush in time.

### Opening Exchanges

Umbrail used his limited opening to cast **Heart's Fire** on Rubius. Rubius spent the extra mobility returning the hive to a tree before joining the fight.

Alder summoned shadow minions. Their pseudopods rotted kobold flesh with necrotic energy, though the Clanger's heavy armor absorbed the first attack aimed at it.

Scritch immediately proved that joining the party had not made him helpless. He struck a kobold in the head with a sling stone, killed it, and earned some trust from the companions who had suspected him of leading them into the ambush.

Rowan assessed the nearest attacker while preparing to protect the others.

The kobolds answered with blades, traps, and noise. Their alchemist shattered a vessel that released a violent cloud of bees, injuring Rowan and Rubius. The Clanger's ceaseless metal rhythm pushed the attackers into a frenzy.

### Rubius Loses Control

Rubius entered a rage, but the axe distorted it. He declared that he would attack anything within reach rather than choose his targets.

His first two swings struck kobolds. Each body collapsed into black sludge and was drawn into the axe instead of remaining on the ground.

**Player:** That's not how you normally kill things, right?

The others had no time to answer. Alder continued summoning and commanding shadows. Scritch moved into flanking positions and used his Cheat abilities to turn openings into brutal rapier strikes. Rowan lowered the Clanger's armor with **Blessed Aim**, then struck it with radiant force.

Umbrail descended into the fight, conjured a tight storm of snow and ice with **Snowblind**, and teleported to a nearby metal object with **Spark Step**. Rubius then charged the blinded Clanger and killed it. Its armor remained behind, but the kobold inside it was absorbed into the axe.

With no enemy immediately in reach, Rubius turned on Alder. His eyes were entirely black as he struck the shroomling for twelve damage.

Alder survived the blow and continued fighting. His minions and Scritch finished the remaining kobolds, ending the ambush before Rubius could attack another ally.

## 6. The Axe Reveals Its Hunger

Umbrail used **Tempest Command** to suppress the supernatural rage long enough for Rubius to regain control. Looking back, Rubius realized that everything had seemed reasonable while he was enraged, even the attack on Alder. Only afterward did he understand that his behavior had been wrong.

Rubius apologized and healed Alder. Rowan and the party confronted him about what they had witnessed: his black eyes, the indiscriminate violence, and the axe consuming its victims.

Umbrail recognized the pattern from his earlier research. The weapon resembled an intelligent shadow axe that corrupts even honorable bearers, consumes souls, and eventually turns allies into enemies. A bearer who has not fully surrendered may lose awareness of the axe's influence and rationalize actions that would otherwise be unthinkable.

The GM assigned Rubius a second Shadow Point for attacking Alder, bringing his recorded total to **three**.

**Umbrail:** The axe is beginning to exert influence over you. Should you continue to wield it, it will ultimately and irreparably corrupt you despite your best intentions.

Umbrail restated the one known remedy: the axe may be separated from Rubius—or stripped of its power—at the [[World/Locations/Shadow Well|Shadow Well]] in the [[World/Locations/Undershadow|Undershadow]]. Rubius reminded the group that he had already thrown the axe from a mountain ledge; it returned to his back roughly thirty minutes later.

The confrontation also brought Alder's secrets into the open. Alder admitted that his patron wants him to kill Rubius and take the axe. He also revealed that his missing family heirloom lies somewhere in the Undershadow, giving him a mental sense of the route even though it is far away.

**Rowan:** Who is your patron?

**Alder:** I don't know. He's a bird guy.

The others pressed Alder on why he had accepted magic from a nameless patron and why he had not explained the demand earlier.

Alder then attempted to have one of his shadow minions consume the axe as an offering to his patron. The attempt backfired: the axe consumed the minion and became empowered. Rubius felt another forced rage rising, but Umbrail repeatedly cast **Dispel Magic** until the surge subsided.

The experiment established that feeding shadow minions to the axe strengthens it and must not be repeated.

The party considered traveling to the Shadow Well before confronting Razor, but the site was distant and the dragon remained an immediate threat to the mountain peoples. Rubius wrapped the axe in an animal pelt, and the group agreed to continue while watching him closely.

## 7. The Stone Stairs

The pine forest thinned as the party reached the [[World/Locations/Stone Stairs|Stone Stairs]], a series of long switchbacks climbing toward Frostvein territory.

Smoke rose from a Black Fang camp partway up the route. The column was approximately half a day ahead by road but had stopped for the night while several hours of daylight remained. The switchbacks offered enough brush for a careful approach, especially with Scritch's skill at avoiding notice.

Rather than approach the camp directly, the party decided to climb the face away from the road and get above the delegation. Umbrail flew between ledges to secure Rubius's heavy rope around rocks. Rubius then climbed and helped the smaller companions ascend. The group succeeded in reaching the switchback above the Black Fang without being detected.

They considered dislodging boulders or engineering a rockslide, but the immediate terrain offered little loose debris. More importantly, continuing upward while the Black Fang slept would give them a much larger lead and better options.

## 8. The Frostvein Plateau

The party climbed to the top of the pass.

There, the landscape changed abruptly. Heavy arctic wind swept across a plateau covered in snow and ice. Visibility was poor in an active snowstorm. Strange arctic wildlife appeared and vanished among the drifts, including polar bears in terrain that seemed unnaturally sudden after the forest below.

The Black Fang remained behind them on the Stone Stairs. Lil Chang and Da Long remained somewhere ahead near Frostvein.

The session ended as the party stepped into the storm.
