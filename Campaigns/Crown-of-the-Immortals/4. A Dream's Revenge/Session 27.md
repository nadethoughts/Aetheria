## Session 27 Summary — Crown of the Immortals

Session 27 picked up mid-combat from the previous session, then moved deeper into the Tomb of Delius.

**Finishing the First Combat**

The session opened with the tail end of the goblin fight. Highlights included Duncan's **Judgment** ability chaining kills — when a judged target dies, Duncan can immediately re-judge a new target, meaning goblins were dying the moment they took actions. Aurora used **Kinetic Grip** to fling Po into better position, and Po's **Conflagration** ended the fight spectacularly, with Aurora's **Flashback** ability spending clarity to let Po repeat the ability for free, wiping out the remaining snipers in one dramatic burst. The group earned their **second victory**, starting future encounters with more heroic resources.

**Into the Tomb**

After recovering, the group explored deeper. Aurora found clues suggesting someone besides the goblins had been in the tomb — an oversized staff covered in **dark sorcery runes**, and what appeared to be Violet's lunch wrapped in a handkerchief. Po identified the staff as a magical implement tied to shadow magic, not a magic item in itself.

Moving down a narrow hallway lined with frescoes of fallen knights and a silver chalice procession, Croft spotted and disarmed a **scythe pressure plate trap**, wedging it open with improvised smithing — no follow-up roll needed thanks to an excellent tier three result.

**The Burial Chamber**

The hallway opened into a burial chamber with twelve stone sarcophagi and a **glowing red skull** painted on the floor. A statue of a proud human warrior bearing the inscription _"Once this fails, the battle is lost"_ stood at the north end, with **sinister chanting** audible from behind the wall.

When Po approached to examine the coffins, all twelve erupted — unleashing **zombies, skeletons, and ghouls**, all fixated on him. The session ended at the start of this new encounter, with the group holding initiative and ready to fight.
