## Session 01 Summary — Nimble Campaign

### 📖 World & Campaign Introduction

You (the GM) introduced the game and setting. Key points you laid out for the players:

- **The System:** Nimble, built on the 5e chassis but playing quite differently — no spell slot management, streamlined combat, and a single-roll attack system.
- **The World:** The continent of [[Solaris]] in the world of [[Aetheria]] — familiar territory from the previous campaign, but the new adventure is set inland at **the town of [[Clackston]]**, a small farming community.
- **The Hook:** [[Clackston]] has been losing livestock (sheep, goats, and eventually a mule) and grain stores at an alarming rate. With winter approaching and the harvest done, the town won't survive if the losses continue. The party has been recruited as a **posse** by the **Farmer's Grange**.
- **The World Map:** You briefly oriented the players to the broader continent — [[World/Locations/The Blessed Realm|The Blessed Realm]], [[Luminael]] (elven peninsula), [[World/Locations/Ironhold|Ironhold]] (dwarven territory), the Free Cities of the Vale, and [[World/Locations/Verdant Strand|Verdant Strand]] where [[Clackston]] sits.

---

### 🧙 Class Overview

You walked through all **11 classes** in Nimble and their 5e equivalents:

- **Berserker** (Barbarian) — tactical rage dice system
- **Cheat** (Rogue) — the only 1-star complexity class
- **Commander** (no direct equivalent — tactical/marshal type)
- **Hunter** (Ranger)
- **Mage** (Sorcerer/Wizard combined) — full school access, no spell selection headaches
- **Oathsworn** (Paladin)
- **Shadowmancer** (Warlock)
- **Shepherd** (Cleric-adjacent) — a companion/partner pet class that shares your HP pool
- **Songweaver** (Bard) — noted as potentially OP with 3 cantrips
- **Stormshifter** (Druid)
- **Zephyr** (Monk)

You also explained **Extra Subclasses** — alternate subclasses that require roleplaying your way into them starting at level 3, not something automatic. The campaign is expected to reach at least level 5, with the introductory arc getting the party to level 2.

### 🍺 The Tavern — Meeting the Party

The party convened at the unnamed tavern at the **posse table**, waiting for Master Glenson. Brief character introductions were played out — Alder nervously picking at his shovel, Umbrail's eyes constantly darting, Rowan sizing up the odd crew from under his straw hat, Rubius mainlining a barrel of ale, and Whisper hanging back observing everyone.

---

### 🌾 The Briefing — Master Glenson

A farmhand led the group to the **Meeting House**, where they met **Master Glenson** — a gruff, salt-and-pepper-haired man in his mid-50s, walking with a crutch due to a stiff leg. He delivered the quest briefing:

- Four of the largest farms (his own, plus George Ballack, Fred Fendroy, and Patricia Keel) have all lost livestock and feed.
- The losses are happening at night or when no one is watching — too fast and numerous for normal predators.
- **Clues found:** a handful of small black scales and clawed footprints around the pastures.
- **Payment:** 25 gold per person, with a 5 gold bonus if completed within a week.

Dave (as Umbrail) made a pointed comment about the pay being low for the scope of the task. An **Insight check** on Glenson revealed he's frustrated that his injury prevents him from leading the posse himself — he wants to be out there but can't.

Glenson tossed the party one of the black scales and sent his farmhand to guide them to the southeastern pasture where animals were last taken.

---

### 🔍 The Investigation — The Pasture

At the pasture, the party spread out. Rubius (Rowan) and Alder examined tracks and found a trail heading south. Umbrail took to the air and spotted — some distance away — **a black kobold struggling to drag a very uncooperative mule southward**. A **Nature Craft check of 18** by Rowan identified the scales as kobold in origin — small dragon-kin creatures.

---

### ⚔️ First Encounter — Catching the Kobold

The party moved toward the kobold. Rubius Haggard **charged in and tackled the kobold**, bowling it over (though dealing no damage on a roll of 1). Rowan clapped manacles on the kobold, Rubius calmed the very angry mule using his Bond of Peace cantrip, and Umbrail circled overhead shaking his head at the chaos below.

Rowan interrogated the kobold with a successful **Influence check (22)**, and after the kobold glanced nervously at the half-giant who had just sat on him, he gave up the information: there's a **large ditch to the south with a door**, and the animals are being taken there. He implied a "big boss" is involved. Rowan released the kobold with a warning, and it bolted in the opposite direction from town.

---

### 🕳️ The Dungeon Entrance

Umbrail scouted south and confirmed the kobold's tip. The party found a large **crater** — clearly connected to the recent fiery catastrophe that preceded the campaign — with burnt earth and obsidian glass around its edge. On the **eastern side of the crater** was a stone door, slightly ajar, completely untouched by the impact, as if it had been _revealed_ by it rather than damaged. Scattered around the crater: more molted black scales, confirming a **full warren of kobolds** inside.

The party took stock, weighed their approach (the Berserker briefly considered just charging the door), and crept to the edge of the crater. That's where the session ended — **posse at the entrance, dungeon ahead.**
