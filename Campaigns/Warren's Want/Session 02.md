# Session 02 Outline — _Nimble_ RPG

## I. Session Recap & Character Introductions

- The GM recaps **Session 01**: the party arrived in the town of **Claxton** to investigate disappearing sheep and goats for the local Farm Guild. They met the surly but clearly pained head gaffer, **Master Glenson** (injured leg, couldn't lead himself).
- The party followed tracks southeast, spotted a **kobold dragging a mule**, captured and interrogated it, and got directions to a kobold warren inside a **meteor crater**.
- They scouted the crater entrance and found an ominous inscription in the **Old Tongue**: _"Turn back, keepers of the dawn. Within lies the shadow of the sun. Touch not the thing that drinks the light, lest your soul be sung into night."_
- Umbrail (mage/birdfolk) deciphered the inscription as a mage with knowledge of the old tongue.

## II. Character Background Questions

Each player answers the same question: _Why did your character start adventuring?_

- **Rowan Hearthboro (Ramsey)** — Former farmer, always answered the call for his community; eventually felt he could help more broadly and left after training a successor.
- **Umbrail (Dave)** — Aging birdfolk from a sparse, dying population; wanted to see the world before the end.
- **Rubius Haggard (Ian)** — Half-giant on a mission to prove he's not like the giants, searching ruins for clues about a giant enclave he wants to stop.
- **Whisper (Jason)** — Simply bored, looking for excitement.
- **Alder (Derek)** — A shroomling farmer whose own goats and sheep are being stolen; he's taking it personally.

## III. Combat Rules Explanation (Tutorial)

The GM walks the group through the _Nimble_ system combat rules for the first time:

- **Action economy**: 3 actions per round (refreshed at end of turn); first-round actions determined by initiative roll (1 action <10, 2 actions 10–19, 3 actions 20+).
- **No armor class**: Attacks always hit unless a 1 is rolled. Damage is reduced using the **Defend reaction** (costs 1 action).
- **Reactions**: Defend, Interpose (Rowan's specialty — move and take a hit for an ally), Opportunity Attack (costs 1 action), Help.
- **Advantage/Disadvantage**: Stack cumulatively. Multiple attacks in a turn apply stacking disadvantage.
- **Enemy types**: Minions (1 HP, die in one hit), Flunkies (have HP but can't crit), Normal enemies (HP + can crit), Bosses.
- Enemies have **no reactions** — players can freely disengage without opportunity attacks.

## IV. First Combat: The Entrance Hall

- The party decides on an aggressive entry rather than negotiation, led by Rubius charging in to intimidate.
- **Initiative rolled**; combat begins against 10+ kobold minions in the entrance hall.
- **Key moments**:
    - **Rubius** charges and attacks, establishing a front line.
    - **Whisper** uses his class ability _The Cheat_ to flip a rolled 1 into a crit on a dagger attack.
    - **Alder** summons a **shadow minion** (tentacled shadow creature) to devour kobolds; uses Shadow Blast.
    - **Umbrail** attempts a lightning bolt (Zap), critically misses and shocks himself, becoming **Charged** — then uses his **Overload** ability (AoE lightning to everything within reach 2), wiping out most of the clustered kobolds at once.
    - **Rowan** uses **Interpose** to jump in front of Whisper and take the Overload hit with his shield, negating the damage.
- **Outcome**: All kobolds in the entrance hall are killed. The party takes minimal damage.

## V. Exploration: The Entrance Hall

- The party loots the bodies and finds:
    - A **golden-orange metal piece** — looks like part of a crest, possibly shaped like a sun. Umbrail immediately snatches it with his beak.
    - A **Caustic Brew** (superior potion: heals 3d6 HP but inflicts _Poisoned_ for 3 rounds). Given to Alder.
- **Lore checks** reveal:
    - The inscription references a relic from the **Second Age** called the **Shadow of the Sun**, used by the **Shadow Spawn** in the _War of the Fractured Sun_.
    - The Shadow Spawn are associated with demigod-like beings called **La Pèlu**.
    - The architecture is **Second Age barracks style** — brutalist, functional, old.
    - The structure was buried for thousands of years and only revealed by a recent **meteor impact** (part of a larger "flaming retribution" event).
    - The inscription itself is **newer than the architecture** — added later.
- **Perception check by Rowan** reveals kobold **graffiti** depicting: a multi-stage plan of conquest against Claxton, with imagery of a mysterious **large kobold leader** appearing at disproportionate scale — possibly a "big boss."
- Alder crafts **torches** via a Nature Craft roll so the party can descend deeper.

## VI. The T-Junction: Choosing Paths

- The party descends stairs into a lower tunnel lined with stone reliefs of the sun and soldiers fighting shadowy figures (with kobold graffiti scrawled over them).
- They reach a **T-junction**:
    - **North**: Sounds of **bleating animals** (likely the stolen sheep/goats).
    - **East**: A single **resonating musical note**.
- The party chooses **East**.

## VII. Skill Challenge: The Trapped Corridor

The eastern corridor is a **death trap** containing four hazards:

1. **Resonance Plates** (vibrating floor tiles) — Arcana or Lore check (fail = 1d6 crush damage).
2. **Steam Bursts** (jets from walls/ceiling) — Finesse or Nature Craft (fail = Burned, disadvantage on next physical check).
3. **Falling Ceiling** (unstable rubble) — Examination or Finesse (fail = 1d6 crush damage).
4. **Fallen Pillar** blocking the treasury — Might, Lore, or deal 20 damage to it (fail = unintended consequences outside the room).

Also visible: two **dead kobolds** who failed to navigate the traps, one holding something glittering (jagged gold).

**Each party member takes one hazard:**

- **Umbrail** — Resonance Plates: Rowan assists (passes DC 12), Umbrail reads the sub-tones of the note and caws in rhythm for the group to follow. ✅
- **Alder** — Steam Bursts: Natural 20 on Nature Craft; predicts geyser timing from pressure release signs; grants a teammate advantage on their roll. ✅
- **Whisper** — Falling Ceiling: Rolls 15, meets DC exactly; uses street experience to read structural instability and guide the group. ✅
- **Rubius** — Fallen Pillar: With advantage (granted by Alder's nat 20), rolls 23 on Might; physically lifts the pillar, opening the treasury vault. ✅

**Outcome**: The group navigates all hazards successfully with no damage.

## VIII. The Treasury

- Inside: old weapons racks with **brittle, unusable weapons**, decaying tapestries, and general Second Age detritus.
- Rubius pokes the chest with his axe, puncturing it and spilling gold — then opens it.
- Loot: **60 gold pieces** (12 GP each).
- Umbrail spots a **second piece of the crest** (orichalcum metal, same sun-shaped design as the first piece).
- The two fragments appear to be parts of the same crest — there are **more pieces** out there.

## IX. Second Combat: The Animal Pen Room

- The party backtracks and heads **North** toward the animal sounds.
- They enter a long pillared room with two small chambers at the far end.
- They encounter **8+ kobolds** (mix of minions and flunkies) plus a **Kobold Clangor** — a special support enemy who boosts kobold damage by clanging metal pieces together rhythmically (does not attack directly but enables crits/bonus damage for allies).
- Initiative rolled; second combat begins.

**Key moments:**

- **Umbrail** starts with only 1 action (low initiative) and uses it to cast **Heart's Fire** on Whisper, granting him a bonus action (4 total actions this round).
- **Rubius** charges and kills a minion.
- **Whisper** kills two minions with daggers, crits a flunky for massive damage (19 points via The Cheat + exploding dice), and kills a third enemy with a sling.
- **Alder** administers the Caustic Brew to **Rowan** (who went down), healing him 7 HP but inflicting _Poisoned_ (disadvantage for 3 rounds). Then hurls a Shadow Bolt.
- **Rowan** goes down temporarily after taking heavy hits from multiple kobolds but is revived.
- **Umbrail** uses **Snow Blind** (ice spell that slows targets and blinds on hit) and **Flame Dart** to kill enemies.
- **Rubius** rages repeatedly, cleaving enemies in two.
- The **Clangor** is prioritized as the most dangerous enemy; Rubius eventually kills him with a 17-damage axe blow, splitting him crown to groin.

**Session ends mid-combat cleanup**, with the Clangor dead and remaining kobolds nearly wiped.

## X. Cliffhanger / End of Session Notes

- GM notes the **two orichalcum crest fragments** that were found — the pieces fit together and there are more scattered in the dungeon/area.
- The **stolen animals** are still in the northern chamber — the mission objective is within reach.
- The kobolds appear to be part of a larger, organized threat against Claxton, with a mysterious **giant-scaled leader** hinted at in the graffiti.
- Session ends before the room is fully searched; the party will pick up with **looting and aftermath** next session.
